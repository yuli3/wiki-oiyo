**The Transformer of Daily Life — Routines and health become realms of control.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Pluto enters the 6th House, the question, "Where do I go to the depths to be reborn?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service.**

## How This Placement Works

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Pluto enters this space, the energy of **transformation, power, and rebirth** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that manifests after mastery is achieved.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive the gift of Pluto (**regenerative power**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Virgo, one can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Pluto in the 6th House is **becoming overly obsessed with that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Pluto in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Pluto is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
