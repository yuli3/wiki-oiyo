**The family interpreter — clarity comes when you put your inner world into words.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mercury is in the 4th house, the question "how do I think and speak?" unfolds in the realm of **home, parents, and inner foundations**.

## How This Placement Works

The 4th house deals with home, parents, and inner foundations. When Mercury enters this house, the energy of **thought, language, and communication style** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the most visibly expressed places in the chart — this placement is often noticeable to the people around you.

## When It Works Well

When this placement operates in a healthy way, the realm of home, parents, and inner foundations receives Mercury's gift: **clarity**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Cancer, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage with more depth.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mercury in the 4th house is **becoming overly concentrated on this stage** — balance is needed so that home, parents, and inner foundations do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What is the difference between houses and signs?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed, or its style; a house shows **where** it unfolds, or its stage. With Mercury in the 4th house, the stage is home, parents, and inner foundations, while the acting tone is set by the sign where Mercury is placed.

**Q. What do I need to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your birth **time and place**. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
