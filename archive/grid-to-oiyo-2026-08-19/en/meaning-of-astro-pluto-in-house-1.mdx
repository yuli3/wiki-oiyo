**An intense presence — a charisma that leaks out even when one tries to hide it.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Pluto enters the 1st House, the question of "Where do I go to the very bottom and be reborn?" unfolds in the realms of **first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things.**

## How This Placement Works

The 1st House deals with first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things. When Pluto enters this house, the energy of **transformation, power, and rebirth** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most visibly manifested positions in a chart — this placement is easily noticed by those around you.

## When Manifested Positively

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things receive the gift of Pluto (**regenerative power**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Aries, one can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Pluto in the 1st House is **becoming overly fixated on that stage** — balance is needed to ensure that the realms of first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things do not become the entirety of life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Pluto in the 1st House, the stage is your first impressions, body, and way of initiating things, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Pluto is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
