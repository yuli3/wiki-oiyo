A tower on top of a rocky mountain is struck by lightning and burns. A crown is blown away, and people fall from the tower. It may look like the most frightening card, but there is only one reason the tower collapses: it was built incorrectly from the beginning. Lightning is destruction, and also a flash of illumination.

## Reading the Symbols

The tower on the rocky mountain symbolizes isolated arrogance, the falling crown represents the end of false authority, and the lightning is an unavoidable flash of truth. The 22 flames correspond to the 22 Major Arcana cards, meaning every journey passes through this dismantling.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when a structure you trusted begins to shake. It may be shocking, but what has collapsed was going to collapse someday. More important than quick cleanup is seeing what had been built on the wrong foundation.

- **Love**: Hidden truth revealed, sudden change in the relationship. Only after the shock can the real relationship be seen.
- **Work & Study**: Organizational restructuring or sudden change. The place that collapses becomes ground for a new opportunity.
- **Money**: Possible unexpected expenses or losses. Check emergency funds and insurance.

## Reversed Meaning

The Tower reversed shows something that should collapse still holding on, or an inner earthquake happening instead of an external collapse. Even if you narrowly avoid a crisis, without checking the foundation it is only a postponement.

## Card Combinations

- **The Tower + Death**: A double signal of dismantling and ending. You are in the middle of a major transition.
- **The Tower + The Star**: Healing immediately after collapse. Hope grows in the place that fell.

## FAQ

**Q. The Tower appeared and I feel very anxious. How should I understand it?**

A. The healthiest way to read The Tower is as a scheduled inspection. If something is shaking now, look at its foundation first. For someone who repairs what can be repaired in advance, The Tower becomes renovation rather than collapse.
