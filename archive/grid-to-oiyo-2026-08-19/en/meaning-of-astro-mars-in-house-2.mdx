**Fighting power directed toward earning — firm when protecting resources.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mars is in the 2nd house, the question "what do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the realm of **money, talents, and self-worth**.

## How This Placement Works

The 2nd house deals with money, talents, and self-worth. When Mars enters this house, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** takes up residence on that stage.

A succedent house is a place that stabilizes and accumulates — this placement builds results slowly, but steadily.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of money, talents, and self-worth receives Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Taurus, can make the atmosphere of this stage easier to read in three dimensions.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 2nd house is **becoming overly concentrated on that stage** — balance is needed so the realm of money, talents, and self-worth does not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed, as its style; houses show **where** it unfolds, as its stage. With Mars in the 2nd house, the stage is money, talents, and self-worth, while the acting tone is set by the sign where Mars is located.

**Q. What do I need to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by birth **time and place**. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
