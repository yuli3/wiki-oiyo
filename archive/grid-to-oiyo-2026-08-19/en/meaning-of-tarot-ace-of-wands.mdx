A hand reaching out from the clouds holds a staff with new shoots. The Ace of Wands is the spark of pure passion before it has taken form — the moment when the feeling of "I want to do this" first catches fire.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | Ace — the seed of a beginning |
| Key words | Spark of inspiration, new project, creative impulse |

## Upright Meaning

A strong inspiration for new work, a project, or a hobby has arrived. Even if the plan is not perfect, what matters is taking the first action while this spark is still alive.

- **Love**: A new attraction with real sparks, or the rekindling of a stale relationship. The person who approaches first takes the lead.
- **Work & Money**: A green light for a new business or plan. Financially, this is the stage where the seed of a new income stream appears — sowing, not harvest.

## Reversed Meaning

There is motivation, but the engine will not start, or something begins and quickly cools. Often the problem is not a weak spark but a lack of firewood — a concrete plan.

## Today's Advice

> Do not wait for the perfect time. Take the smallest first action today.
