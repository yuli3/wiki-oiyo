The Gap-Sul (甲戌) Ilju is the 11th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It consists of the Heavenly Stem Gap (Yang Wood), representing a giant tree reaching straight toward the sky, sitting upon the Earthly Branch Sul (the Dog), which represents loyalty. It is the image of a giant tree standing guard on an autumn ridge—a reliable guardian.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gap-Sul Ilju is a reliable, "eldest sibling" type with a strong sense of responsibility. Because they sit upon wealth, they possess excellent management skills and treat their promises as if they were their own life.

- **Strengths**: Strong sense of responsibility, ability to manage and protect wealth, and unwavering loyalty.
- **Points to Note**: They can be stubborn and insistent on their own methods. Developing flexibility in the face of change is a challenge for them.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Gap (甲) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gap/) sits upon the [Sul (戌) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sul/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the 10 Gods correspond to [Pyeonjae (偏財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeonjae/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to [Yang (養)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-yang/). Pyeonjae is the star of liquid assets—the sense for circulating money and opportunities is embedded in the daily branch. As it sits in the "Yang" (Nourishment) stage, it is a structure of preparation, waiting for the right time to build strength.

In terms of Nabeum (phonetic element), it belongs to Sanduhwa (山頭火, Mountain-Top Fire), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same phonetic element.
