A fish peeks out of the cup held by a young person standing before the waves, as if offering a greeting. The Page of Cups is the strange and lovable signal sent by the heart — an apprentice of emotion and imagination.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | Page — the one who learns |
| Key words | Discovering sensitivity, unexpected affection, creative inspiration, the greeting of the fish |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when sensitivity and intuition awaken. Welcome emotional news may arrive, such as an unexpected confession or message, and creative inspiration can surface suddenly like a fish. Do not dismiss the heart's signal just because it seems young.

- **Love**: A fresh signal of affection and a shy confession. If you are receiving it, notice the sincerity; if you are offering it, notice the courage.
- **Work & Money**: The sprouting of a creative idea. This is a better time to invest in developing your senses through learning and experience than to focus only on money.

## Reversed Meaning

Emotional swings, childish sulking, or indifference that lets inspiration pass by. When the fish of the heart speaks, write it down.

## Today's Advice

> Write down one odd feeling that comes to you today. It may be the first line of your next work, or your next connection.
