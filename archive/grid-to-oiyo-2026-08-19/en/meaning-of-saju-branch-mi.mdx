Mi (未) represents the dry earth at the end of midsummer and the afternoon hour when a flock of sheep grazes. It is a Yin Earth (陰土) character that harbors a firm, stubborn core beneath a soft and gentle exterior.

## Personality Traits

Individuals with a strong Mi (Earth) influence are gentle, family-oriented, and deeply devoted to those close to them. They have a strong affinity for food and a refined sense of taste. While they are as docile as sheep in daily life, they become more resolute than anyone else when their core convictions are challenged.

## Samhap and Chong

It forms the Wood Bureau (木局) through the Hae-Mao-Mi (亥卯未) Samhap (Triple Harmony). It clashes with Chou (丑) in the Chou-Mi Chong (丑未沖) — a collision between two gentle Earth elements, often associated with changes in the home or internal personal spheres.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How does the 'stubbornness' of Mi (Earth) manifest?**

A. They typically accommodate others, but if their core values (family, beliefs, or self-esteem) are crossed, they become quiet yet absolutely unyielding. Because it is a form of stubbornness that persists without outward fighting, those around them often do not realize it until much later.
