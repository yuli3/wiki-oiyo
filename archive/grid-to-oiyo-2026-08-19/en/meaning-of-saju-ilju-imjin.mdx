The Imjin (壬辰) Ilju is the 29th pillar of the sexagenary cycle, where the Heavenly Stem Im (壬)—the Yang Water of a vast, deep ocean—sits upon the Earthly Branch Jin (辰), the Dragon that harbors change. It represents a great river where a dragon plays—an image of one who commands the harmony of wind and clouds.

## Personality and Temperament

The Imjin Ilju is a "soldier of fortune" type with grand ambitions and exceptional strategic intellect. Driven by the energy of the *Goegang* (魁罡), they reject the mundane and only feel satisfied when operating in a "large pond."

- **Strengths**: Large-scale strategic thinking and charisma that shines in times of crisis.
- **Points of Caution**: Because they are highly intelligent, they may be tempted to rely on cunning schemes. A straightforward approach is, in fact, their greatest strategy.

## Structural Analysis — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Im (壬) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-im/) sits upon the [Jin (辰) Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-jin/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji, the 10 Gods correspond to [Pyeongwan (偏官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeongwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to [Myo (墓)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-myo/). Pyeongwan is the star of challenge and responsibility—it creates a strong vessel that is tempered by enduring pressure. The position of *Myo* (Grave) indicates a structure of management, gathering, and preservation.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to *Jangryusu* (Long-flowing Water), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
