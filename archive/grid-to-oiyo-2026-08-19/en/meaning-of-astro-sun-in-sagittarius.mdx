**Someone whose identity lies beyond the horizon — stand still and the light dims.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Sagittarius makes that answer come out far and free, toward a larger meaning.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Sagittarius ♐ — exploration, meaning, optimism |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Jupiter |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Sagittarius, it shows up far and free, toward a larger meaning.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Sagittarius's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **an openness to set out anywhere**
- **an optimistic vision**
- **philosophical insight**

## Watch out for

- details and promises can be neglected
- frankness can sound careless

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Sagittarius way (far and free, toward a larger meaning) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Sagittarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
