**A flowing light — your identity is larger than any fixed frame.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Pisces makes that answer come out boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Pisces ♓ — empathy, imagination, transcendence |
| Element · Modality | Water · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Neptune |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Pisces, it shows up boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Pisces's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **empathy that feels others' emotions as one's own**
- **imagination that pictures beyond the real**
- **unconditional acceptance**

## Watch out for

- blurred boundaries lead to burnout
- escapism becomes a sweet trap

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Pisces way (boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Pisces, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
