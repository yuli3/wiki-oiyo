In (寅) is the time when the tiger stirs before dawn and the first month of the year. It marks the true "start" of the Earthly Branches, where the Yang energy of spring first breaks through the earth.

## Personality Traits

Individuals with a strong In (寅) influence are dynamic, possess a strong desire for honor, and do not hesitate to initiate tasks. Like the tiger, they prefer grand endeavors over trivial details, and their pride serves as their primary motivation.

## San-hap and Chong

In (寅) forms a Fire-frame (火局) through the San-hap combination of In-Wu-Xu (寅午戌). It clashes with Shen (申) in the In-Shen Chong (寅申沖)—a clash between two "Travel Stars" (Yeokma), which leads to frequent movement and change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does having an In-Shen Chong mean I will move houses often?**

A. Since both In and Shen are "Travel Star" (驛馬) characters, it is true that their clash increases volatility. However, in a modern context, this is interpreted as a "lifestyle with high mobility," such as frequent business trips, job changes, or international travel. If harnessed well, this wide range of activity can become a significant opportunity.
