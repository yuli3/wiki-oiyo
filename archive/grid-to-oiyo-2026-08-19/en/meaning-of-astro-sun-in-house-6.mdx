**The Sun shining in the workplace — Competence is identity.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Sun is in the 6th House, the question "Who am I?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service.**

## At a Glance

| Category | Content |

| House | 6th House · House of Everyday Life — Duties, habits, body care |
| Stage of Life | Work, health, routines, service |
| Natural Sign · Classification | Virgo · Cadent House |

## How This Placement Works

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When the Sun enters this house, the energy of **identity, vitality, and the core of the self** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that reveals itself after mastery is achieved.

## When It Manifests Positively

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive the Sun's gift (**vitality**). Referring to the nature of Virgo, the natural sign of this house, can help provide a more three-dimensional understanding of the atmosphere of this stage.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Sun in the 6th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is required to ensure that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of one's life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Sun in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which the Sun is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
