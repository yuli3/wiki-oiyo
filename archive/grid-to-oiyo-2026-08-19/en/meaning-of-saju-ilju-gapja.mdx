The Gapja (甲子) Ilju is the first pillar of the sixty-pillar sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Gap (Yang Wood), a towering tree reaching straight toward the sky, sitting upon the Earthly Branch Ja (the wise Rat of the deep night). It is the image of a green, towering tree standing tall above the cold winter waters—growing by drawing nourishment from clear wisdom.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gapja Ilju is scholarly, characterized by intelligence and high ideals, with a lifelong thirst for learning. While they benefit greatly from the support of mothers, mentors, and noble patrons, they possess a certain innocence that can make them clumsy with mundane, practical tasks.

- **Strengths**: Quick comprehension, high discernment, and the virtue of teaching and leading others.
- **Points of Caution**: Ideals may outpace reality, making it easy to miss out on practical gains; the mind is prone to frequent fluctuations.

## Structural Analysis — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gap (甲) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gap/) sits upon the [Ja (子) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-ja/). Based on the primary energy (Jeonggi) of the Ilji, the 10 Gods correspond to [Jeongin (正印)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeongin/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to [Mokyok (沐浴)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-mokyok/). Jeongin is the star of academics and receptivity—the foundation is built on the power to learn, accept, and receive help from others. The position of Mokyok (Bathing) means their senses and charm shine, but it is also a structure prone to change and fluctuation.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Haejunggeum (Gold in the Sea), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
