**Mars here must guard against workaholism — labor itself becomes the outlet.** A house is the **life stage** where a planet acts. When Mars enters the 6th house, the question "What do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the areas of **work, health, routines, and service**.

## How This Placement Works

The 6th house deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Mars is placed here, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** takes up residence on that stage.

Cadent houses are places of learning and adaptation — this placement often works as an inner process first, then becomes visible after skill and practice have accumulated.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Virgo, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more layered way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 6th house is **becoming too concentrated on this stage** — balance is needed so that work, health, routines, and service do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed, meaning its style. A house shows **where** it unfolds, meaning its stage. With Mars in the 6th house, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the acting tone is determined by the sign Mars occupies.

**Q. What do I need in order to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
