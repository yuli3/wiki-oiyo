**Drive aimed at a cause — the farther the goal, the stronger the fire.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Sagittarius makes that answer come out far and free, toward a larger meaning.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Sagittarius ♐ — exploration, meaning, optimism |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Jupiter |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Sagittarius, it shows up far and free, toward a larger meaning.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Sagittarius's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **an openness to set out anywhere**
- **an optimistic vision**
- **philosophical insight**

## Watch out for

- details and promises can be neglected
- frankness can sound careless

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Sagittarius way (far and free, toward a larger meaning) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Sagittarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
