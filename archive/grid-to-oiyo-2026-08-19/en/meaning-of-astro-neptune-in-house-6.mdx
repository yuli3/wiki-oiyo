**The place of service — finding meaning in helping others, and receiving warnings against burnout.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Neptune enters the 6th House, the question of "Where do I dream, and where do I blur?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service.**

## How This Placement Functions

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Neptune enters this space, the energy of **dreams, ideals, and the dissolution of boundaries** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that manifests after mastery.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive the gifts of Neptune (**inspiration**). By referring to the nature of Virgo, the natural sign of this house, one can read the atmosphere of this stage in more depth.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Neptune in the 6th House is **becoming overly immersed in that stage** — balance is required so that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Neptune in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Neptune is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
