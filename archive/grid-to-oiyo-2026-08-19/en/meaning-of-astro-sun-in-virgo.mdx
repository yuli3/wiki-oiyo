**A Sun that shines through usefulness — quality is your pride.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Virgo makes that answer come out carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Virgo ♍ — analysis, improvement, usefulness |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Virgo, it shows up carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Virgo's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **analysis that misses no detail**
- **quiet, reliable competence**
- **ceaseless self-improvement**

## Watch out for

- perfectionism delays the start
- the critic's edge turns on the self

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Virgo way (carefully and precisely, seeking a better way) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Virgo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
