Beneath the night sky, a woman kneels beside a pond and pours water from two jars onto the land and into the water. She is naked, but without shame. This is the most honest and peaceful state reached only after losing everything in The Tower.

## Reading the Symbols

The one large star and seven smaller stars symbolize essential hope and the alignment of the seven chakras; the water from the two jars represents inner resources that do not run dry; and the naked body symbolizes sincerity without pretense.

## Upright Meaning

A dark period has passed, and recovery begins. This is a time when confidence in the direction matters more than immediate results. It is ideal for healing the body and mind, receiving creative inspiration, and setting a long-term vision.

- **Love**: Healing from wounds and new hope. Things improve slowly, but surely.
- **Work & Study**: Long-range plans shine. Step away from pressure for immediate results and set a vision.
- **Money**: The beginning of recovery. Focus on restoring financial health rather than chasing large profits.

## Reversed Meaning

The Star reversed is starlight hidden behind clouds. You may have lost hope or begun doubting yourself, but the star has not disappeared; it is only covered. Begin again with small recovery routines.

## Card Combinations

- **The Star + The Sun**: The best flow, where hope leads to real achievement.
- **The Star + The Hermit**: A quiet time of healing. Recovery in solitude is needed.

## FAQ

**Q. Does The Star mean things will get better soon?**

A. The Star is not a card of "immediately," but of "surely." Like stars in the night sky, it shows direction, but dawn has not arrived yet. For timing questions, it points more to the medium or long term, but you can trust the direction of recovery.
