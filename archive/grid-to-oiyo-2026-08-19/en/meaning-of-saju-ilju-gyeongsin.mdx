The Gyeongsin (庚申) Ilju is the 57th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It is a combination where the Heavenly Stem Gyeong (庚), representing hard iron or raw ore (Yang Metal), sits upon the Earthly Branch Sin (申), representing the clever monkey (Metal). It resembles an iron peak atop a rocky mountain—a form where hardness reaches its zenith.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gyeongsin Ilju is an action-oriented type with exceptional decisiveness and drive. Because it sits in the position of *Geonrok* (Established Prosperity), they forge their own paths and protect others through sheer loyalty.

- **Strengths**: Rapid decision-making, strong execution, and unwavering loyalty.
- **Points of Caution**: They tend to act before they think. It is recommended to add the wisdom of flexibility to their linear strength.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gyeong (庚) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gyeong/) sits on the [Sin (申) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sin-monkey/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the Ten God is [Bigyeon (比肩)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-bigyeon/) and the 12 Stages of Life is [Geonrok (建祿)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-geonrok/). Bigyeon is the star of the ego and subjectivity—the power to stand on one's own is supported by the daily branch. As it is a *Geonrok* position, it is a robust structure that relies on personal competence for self-reliance.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to *Seokryumok* (Pomegranate Wood), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
