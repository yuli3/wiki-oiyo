The Gyeongja (庚子) Ilju is the 37th pillar of the Sexagenary cycle. It consists of the Heavenly Stem Gyeong (Yang Metal), representing hard iron or raw ore, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Ja (Rat), representing the wisdom of the deep night. It is the image of iron being tempered in the cold water of midwinter—a symbol of brilliance sharpened by the cold.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gyeongja Ilju is an innovator with an extraordinary mind and a strong critical spirit. With the sharp edge of the Sanggwan (Hurting Officer), they are adept at breaking down the old to establish the new.

- **Strengths**: Exceptional intellect, analytical skills, and reformative drive.
- **Points of Caution**: Their sharp tongue can leave deep wounds. A blade is most valuable when used for surgery.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gyeong (庚) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gyeong/) sits atop the [Ja (子) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-ja/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10-God is [Sanggwan (傷官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-sanggwan/), and the 12-stage life cycle is [Sa (死)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-sa/). Sanggwan is the star of eloquence and creativity; this expressive power that breaks molds is both their charm and the seed of potential controversy. The 'Sa' (Death) stage indicates a structure of deep immersion, focusing on one path.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Byeoksangto (壁上土, Wall Earth), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri alongside paired pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
