The Gyeong-In (庚寅) Ilju is the 27th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It is a combination where the Heavenly Stem Gyeong (庚), representing hard iron or raw ore (Yang Metal), sits upon the Earthly Branch In (寅), representing the valiant tiger (Wood). It is the image of an axe entering a tiger's forest—a figure that clears a path without hesitation.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gyeong-In Ilju is an active, pioneer-type personality with excellent business acumen. They are loyal and quick to make decisions, making them adept at opening up new markets and creating new opportunities.

- **Strengths**: Quick decision-making and action, business sense, loyalty.
- **Points of Caution**: They tend to take excessive risks when their momentum is high. They require a strategist or advisor to act as a "brake."

## Structural Reading — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gyeong (庚) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gyeong/) sits on the [In (寅) Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-in/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the 10 God is [Pyeonjae (Indirect Wealth)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeonjae/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Jeol (Extinction)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jeol/). Pyeonjae is the star of liquid assets—the sense for managing money and opportunities is embedded in the daily branch. Because it sits in the stage of "Extinction," it is a dynamic structure characterized by repeated changes and comebacks.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Songbaekmok (Pine and Cypress Wood), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
