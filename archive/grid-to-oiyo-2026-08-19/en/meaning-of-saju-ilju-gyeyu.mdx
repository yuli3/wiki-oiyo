The Gye-Yu (癸酉) Ilju is the 10th pillar of the Sexagenary Cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Gye—Yin Water, like soaking rainwater—sitting atop the Earthly Branch Yu, the precise Rooster. It is the image of dew gathered on a blade of a sword at dawn in autumn—a cool, clear, and intuitive form.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gye-Yu Ilju is a clean, principled, and highly intuitive analyst. They dislike clutter and are trusted for their precise way of handling tasks.

- **Strengths**: Precise analytical skills, intuition, and meticulous work habits.
- **Points of Caution**: Standards bordering on perfectionism can be exhausting for others. Remember that moderation is also a virtue.

## Reading the Structure — Saju Interpretation

The [Gye (癸) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gye/) sits atop the [Yu (酉) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-yu/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10 God is [Pyeonin (偏印)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeonin/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Byeong (病)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-byeong/). Pyeonin is the star of intuition and special talents—unique ideas and professional expertise grow from the Day Branch. Because it sits in the stage of 'Sickness' (Byeong), it is a structure that deepens sensitivity and empathy.

In terms of Nabeum, it belongs to Geombong-geum (Sword-tip Metal), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same phonetic element.
