**The mind of the strategy briefing — you are evaluated through public speech.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mercury is in the 10th house, the question "How do I think and speak?" unfolds in the realm of **career, reputation, and social calling**.

| Category | Details |

| House | 10th house · House of Achievement — career, fame, and the summit of life |

## How This Placement Works

The 10th house deals with career, reputation, and social calling. When Mercury enters this house, the energy of **thought, language, and communication style** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the most visibly expressed places in the chart — this placement is easy for people around you to notice.

## When It Flows Well

When this placement works in a healthy way, the realm of career, reputation, and social calling receives Mercury's gift: **clarity**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Capricorn, can help you read the mood of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mercury in the 10th house is **overconcentration on that stage** — balance is needed so the realm of career, reputation, and social calling does not become all of life while the other houses turn into empty rooms.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What is the difference between a house and a sign?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed, meaning its style; a house shows **where** it unfolds, meaning its stage. With Mercury in the 10th house, the stage is career, reputation, and social calling, while the acting tone is set by the sign Mercury occupies.

**Q. What do I need in order to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
