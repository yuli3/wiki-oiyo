Under the moonlight, a dog and a wolf howl, a crayfish crawls out of the water, and a path disappears between two towers. Moonlight illuminates things, but it also distorts them. What you see now is not the whole picture, and half of your fear is an illusion made by shadows.

## Reading the Symbols

The dog and wolf symbolize domesticated fear and wild fear, the crayfish represents old anxiety rising from deep within the unconscious, and the winding path symbolizes a process that must be walked even without certainty.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when information is incomplete and emotions fluctuate. Postpone major decisions and write down the source of your anxiety. Half of it has no substance. At the same time, dreams, intuition, and creative sensitivity become unusually sharp.

- **Love**: Misunderstanding and suspicion can grow easily. Confirmation protects the relationship better than guessing.
- **Work & Study**: A situation has hidden circumstances behind it. A rushed contract or decision can become a trap.
- **Money**: Be careful with opaque offers. The more someone says it is "guaranteed," the more suspicion is needed.

## Reversed Meaning

The Moon reversed is a state where dawn is near. The fog lifts, hidden facts are revealed, and the shape of vague anxiety becomes clear. Truth may be uncomfortable, but it is safer than illusion.

## Card Combinations

- **The Moon + The Sun**: From darkness into light. A combination showing the end of confusion.
- **The Moon + The High Priestess**: Intuition at its peak. However, do not mistake anxiety for intuition.

## FAQ

**Q. If The Moon appears, does it mean the other person is lying?**

A. Not necessarily. The more accurate meaning is, "There is something I do not know." It may be deliberate deception, simple lack of communication, or your own projection. The remedy of The Moon is not suspicion, but verification.
