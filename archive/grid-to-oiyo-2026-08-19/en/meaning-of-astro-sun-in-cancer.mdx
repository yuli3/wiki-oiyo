**A Sun like moonlight — the self is completed by protecting and caring.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Cancer makes that answer come out cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Cancer ♋ — protection, memory, care |
| Element · Modality | Water · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Moon |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Cancer, it shows up cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Cancer's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a protective instinct that shelters others**
- **emotional sensitivity that reads the room**
- **devotion to one's own people**

## Watch out for

- old wounds are remembered and one withdraws
- the tides of mood run high

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Cancer way (cautiously at first, then holding close and deep) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Cancer, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
