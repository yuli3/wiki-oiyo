Hae (亥) is the final character of the twelve Earthly Branches; it marks the beginning of winter and the time when the day goes to sleep. As a vast body of water that gathers and embraces all things, it is a position where endings and new conceptions coexist.

## Personality Traits

Individuals with a strong Hae (Water) influence are generally optimistic, profound, and generous. Often called a character that attracts fortune in food and relationships, they are pure-hearted and can be easily deceived; however, it is precisely this purity that ultimately draws people to them.

## Samhap and Chong

Hae forms a Wood (木) configuration through the Samhap (Three Harmonies) of Hae-Myo-Mi (亥卯未). It clashes with Sa (巳) in a Sa-Hae-Chong (巳亥沖)—a clash of the "Traveling Star" (Yeokma), which is deeply associated with long-distance travel and connections to foreign countries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Are people born in the Year of the Pig truly blessed with good fortune?**

A. It is true that Hae contains the structure of abundance, as it holds Gap-mok (life) and Im-su (wisdom) within its hidden stems, representing "having both the means to eat and the means to nurture." However, fortune comes from how one utilizes these traits rather than the structure itself. If one maintains their generous nature while cultivating the discernment to judge people, the structure will manifest as true fortune.

*In Saju, Earthly Branches represent 'time and environment.' The manifestation of the same character varies depending on whether it is located in the Year, Month, Day, or Hour pillar, and what kind of combinations (Hap) or clashes (Chong) it forms with surrounding characters.*
