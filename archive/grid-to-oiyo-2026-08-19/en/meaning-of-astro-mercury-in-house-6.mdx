**A mind that designs processes — the craftsperson of work manuals.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mercury is in the 6th house, the question "How do I think and speak?" unfolds in the realm of **work, health, routines, and service**.

## How This Placement Works

The 6th house deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Mercury enters this house, the energy of **thought, language, and communication style** takes up residence on that stage.

Cadent houses are places of learning and adaptation — this placement often works as an inner process first, then becomes visible after skill has been developed.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of work, health, routines, and service receives Mercury's gift: **clarity**. Looking at the texture of its natural sign, Virgo, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more layered way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mercury in the 6th house is **overconcentration on this stage** — balance is needed so that work, health, routines, and service do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed, its style, while a house shows **where** it unfolds, its stage. For Mercury in the 6th house, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the acting tone is set by the sign Mercury occupies.

**Q. What do I need to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
