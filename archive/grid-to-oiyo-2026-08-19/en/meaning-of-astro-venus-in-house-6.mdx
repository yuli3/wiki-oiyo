**The mood-maker of the workplace — love may blossom amidst camaraderie.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Venus enters the 6th House, the question of "what do I find beautiful and how do I love?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service**.

## How This Placement Works

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Venus enters this house, the energy of **love, aesthetics, and attraction** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that manifests after mastery.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive the gift of Venus (**harmony**). By referring to the nature of Virgo, the natural sign of the 6th House, one can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Venus in the 6th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of one's life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (the style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Venus in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Venus is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
