A king in a cloak patterned with salamanders looks ahead. The King of Wands turns passion into vision, and vision into the movement of an organization. This is fire energy in its mature form.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | King — The one who governs |
| Key words | Visionary leadership, entrepreneurship, bold decisions, influence |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period of leadership that presents the big picture and moves people. Set direction and delegate rather than doing everything yourself. The luck is strong for entrepreneurial decisions.

- **Love**: A reliable and proactive partner, or mature leadership that guides the relationship.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable for oversight, launching a business, or expanding an enterprise. Financially, your judgment is alive for large-scale decisions.

## Reversed Meaning

Dictatorial and hasty orders, or holding a position without any vision. A king's authority comes not from title but from vision.

## Today's Advice

> Delegate one thing today. A king's work is not to do everything, but to make things happen.
