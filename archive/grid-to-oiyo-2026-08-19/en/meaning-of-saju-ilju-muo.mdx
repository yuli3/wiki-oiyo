The Mu-O (戊午) Ilju is the 55th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It is a combination where the Heavenly Stem Mu—a large, unshakable mountain of Yang Earth—sits upon the Earthly Branch O, the horse of passion. It represents a volcano erupting—the image of a mountain's weight combined with the momentum of fire.

## Personality and Temperament

The Mu-O Ilju is of the "Emperor" type, possessing self-esteem and spirit that pierce the sky. Due to the intense energy of Yang-in (陽刃), they are usually steady and heavy, but they exert explosive power at critical moments.

- **Strengths**: Overwhelming spirit; a combination of academic depth and charisma.
- **Points to Note**: The challenge lies in the aftermath of an explosion. A volcano is most majestic when it is dormant.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Mu (戊) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-mu/) sits on the [O (午) Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-o/). Based on the main Qi of the daily branch, the 10-God is [Jeong-in (正印)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeongin/), and the 12-Stage Life Cycle is [Jewang (帝旺)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jewang/). Jeong-in is the star of academics and receptivity—the foundation is built on the power to learn, accept, and receive benevolence from others. As it sits in the position of Jewang, it is a strong, prosperous structure where energy reaches its peak.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Cheonsanghwa (天上火, Fire in the Heavens), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
