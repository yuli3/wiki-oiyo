The Eulsa (乙巳) Ilju is the 42nd pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It consists of the Heavenly Stem Eul (Yin Wood), representing a flexible, climbing vine, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Sa (Snake), representing insight. It evokes the image of a vine winding around a snake—a figure of brilliant, spreading wit.

## Personality and Temperament

The Eulsa Ilju is a strategic type with excellent eloquence and charm. With the talent and flexibility of the Sanggwan (Hurting Officer), they make their presence felt in any setting.

- **Strengths**: Brilliant oratorical skills, charm, and flexible strategy.
- **Points of Caution**: If words precede action, gossip may follow. The more silk-like the tongue, the more it needs a sheath.

## Structural Reading — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Eul (乙) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-eul/) sits atop the [Sa (巳) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/). Based on the main qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10 God is [Sanggwan (傷官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-sanggwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Mokyok (沐浴)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-mokyok/). The Sanggwan is the star of eloquence and creativity—the ability to break molds through expression is both their charm and the seed of potential gossip. Being in the stage of Mokyok (Bathing), it is a structure where senses and charm shine, but fluctuations also accompany them.

In terms of Nabeum (Phonetic Element), it belongs to Bokdeunghwa (Lamp Light), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
