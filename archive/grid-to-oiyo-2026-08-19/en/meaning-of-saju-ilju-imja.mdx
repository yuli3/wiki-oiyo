The Imja (壬子) Ilju is the 49th pillar of the sexagenary cycle, where the Heavenly Stem Im (壬)—the Yang Water of the vast, deep ocean—sits upon the Earthly Branch Ja (子), the wise Rat of the midnight hour. It represents a great ocean where the horizon is invisible—a shape of unfathomable depth.

## Personality and Temperament

The Imja Ilju is a magnanimous, broad-minded type with wisdom as deep as the sea. With the intense energy of the Yang-blade (Yangin), once they set their mind to something, they sweep across the field like a massive wave.

- **Strengths**: Ocean-like magnanimity and wisdom, sweeping drive.
- **Points of Caution**: If the waves are too violent, they can swallow ships. The stronger the energy, the more important the rudder becomes.

## Structural Reading — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Im (壬) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-im/) sits upon the [Ja (子) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-ja/). Based on the main qi of the Ilji (daily branch), the Ten God is [Geopjae (劫財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-geopjae/), and the 12 Stages of Qi is [Jewang (帝旺)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jewang/). Geopjae is the star of competition and ambition—energy thrives amidst rivalry, but it also brings fluctuations in wealth. As it sits in the position of Jewang, it is a strong, prosperous structure where energy has reached its peak.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Sangjamok (桑柘木), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
