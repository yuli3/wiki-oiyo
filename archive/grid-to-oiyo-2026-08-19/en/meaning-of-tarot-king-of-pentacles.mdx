A king in robes patterned with grapevines sits with a castle behind him, holding a scepter in one hand and a pentacle in the other. The King of Pentacles is the mature ruler of the material world, able to turn what he touches into an asset.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material matters, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | King — The one who governs |
| Key words | Completed wealth, stable power, business skill, the Midas touch |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when business and financial sense reach a peak. You have the trust and resources needed for large, stable decisions, and your own kingdom grows stronger as you enrich the people around you.

- **Love**: The love of a dependable adult: a relationship proven through responsibility more than words.
- **Work & Money**: An ideal time for management, investment, and asset operation. Expanding in a proven field creates the Midas touch.

## Reversed Meaning

Money has become the only standard, leading to rigid materialism or workaholism. If you have built a castle but are not living inside it, what was the castle for?

## Today's Advice

> Before today's decision, ask just once: does this increase wealth, or does it increase life?
