Siksin (食神), true to its name as the "God of Food Luck," represents the natural outflow of one's energy—symbolizing speech, writing, food, creative works, and the leisure of life. It is considered a benevolent star (吉神).

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Definition | The element I produce (Saeng) with the same Yin-Yang polarity |
| Governed Areas | Expression, food/clothing/shelter, talent, leisure |
| Key Keywords | Expressiveness, food luck, optimism, immersion |

## Personality Traits

Those with a prominent Siksin have a natural gift for expression and possess the ability to immerse themselves deeply in what they love. They are generally optimistic, enjoy eating and creating, and are known for being comfortable to be around.

## Career and Work

They excel in fields where they "draw from within to create," such as cooking, content creation, education, research, and the arts. Their strength lies in a craftsman-like immersion, focusing on a single field for a long time.

## Wealth

As the source that generates Jaeseong (財星, Wealth Star), their structure is one where "talent becomes their livelihood." They are at their healthiest when the work they enjoy naturally leads to income.

## Excess and Deficiency

When in excess, one may become overly focused on eating, playing, or doing only what they want. When deficient, one may feel frustrated due to blocked self-expression.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Which is better, Siksin or Sang-gwan (傷官)?**

A. Siksin represents deep, immersive expression, while Sang-gwan represents flamboyant, expansive expression. Siksin excels in stability and sustainability, while Sang-gwan excels in impact and innovation. It is not a matter of superiority, but a difference in the method of expression.

*The Ten Gods (Sipsin) are a language used to interpret the relationships between the Day Master (the self) and other characters. The manifestation of each Ten God varies depending on its position, strength, and interactions (Hap/Chung); there is no such thing as an inherently "good" or "bad" Ten God.*
