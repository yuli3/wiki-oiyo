Geonrok (建祿) is the fourth stage of Sibiunseong (the twelve stages of life), representing the position where one ascends to an official post and begins to receive a salary (祿). It is a representative auspicious star that symbolizes the time when one establishes their own livelihood through their own abilities—signifying self-reliance, diligence, and stable achievement.

## Meaning and Symbolism

The core of Geonrok is "to establish (建) oneself." Because it is an energy earned and accumulated by one's own hands rather than inherited, a Saju chart with strong Geonrok is often called a "self-made" type. Even if it is not flashy, the dignity of this star lies in its solid, unshakeable nature.

## Temperament of a Saju with Strong Geonrok

People who have Geonrok in their daily pillar (Ilji) feel uncomfortable relying on others and are only satisfied when they resolve their own affairs. In an organization, they are the type who silently speak through their performance.

- **Strengths**: Consistency and sense of responsibility, endurance in building expertise, and unshakeable vitality.
- **Points to Note**: There is a tendency to shoulder everything alone. Accepting that asking for help is also a form of competence will further expand one's capacity.

## Encountering Geonrok in Major Luck (Daewoon) and Annual Luck (Sewoon)

The Geonrok luck cycle is a period when the results of one's efforts are converted into income and status. It is considered a good time for "standing under one's own name," such as changing jobs, becoming independent, or starting a business. However, balance is required so as not to miss opportunities for expansion by falling into the inertia that stability provides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I heard that if you have Geonrok, you lack the support of your parents.**

A. This is an exaggerated interpretation of the "star of the self-made." The original meaning of Geonrok is not about the presence or absence of parental support, but rather that one "has the strength to stand on their own." It is closer to saying that regardless of whether one has an inheritance or not, they are ultimately a person who grows through their own merit.
