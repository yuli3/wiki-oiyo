The Byeongja (丙子) Ilju is the 13th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Byeong—the Yang Fire of the sun that illuminates all things—sitting atop the Earthly Branch Ja, the wise Rat of midnight. It is the image of the sun rising over a calm lake, where the light reflects off the water, shining twofold.

## Personality and Temperament

The Byeongja Ilju is a gentlemanly type who is polite and possesses a strong desire for honor. They combine bright charisma with calm wisdom, allowing them to shine in public settings.

- **Strengths**: Dignified manners, public reliability, and bright ambition.
- **Points of Caution**: They may focus too much on saving face, making it easy to overlook practical benefits or the expression of their inner feelings.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Byeong (丙) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-byeong/) sits atop the [Ja (子) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-ja/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the 10 Gods correspond to [Jeonggwan (正官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeonggwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to [Tae (胎)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-tae/). Jeonggwan is the star of honor and order, forming a foundation of dignity that seeks recognition within established norms. As it sits in the stage of 'Tae' (Conception), it is a structure of conceptualization that always harbors new possibilities.

In terms of Nabeum (phonetic element), it belongs to Ganhasu (澗下水), a classification frequently used in traditional Saju analysis to group pillars sharing the same phonetic element.
