Among the 78 cards in a tarot deck, the **22 Major Arcana cards** deal with the great themes of life.
The sequence that begins with The Fool at number 0 and reaches completion with The World at number 21 is
called **The Fool's Journey**: a map of growth from an innocent beginning (0), through learning and trials,
to integration (21).

## The Fool's Journey — Three Acts

**Act 1 (0-7) · Into the World**: The Fool receives the tools of The Magician and the intuition of
The High Priestess, meets the parents (The Empress and The Emperor) and society (The Hierophant),
chooses love (The Lovers), and wins a first victory (The Chariot). This is the stage of outward growth.

**Act 2 (8-14) · Into the Inner Self**: The Fool masters the self through Strength, turns inward through
The Hermit, passes through Wheel of Fortune and Justice, and reaches the pause of The Hanged Man,
the transition of Death, and the integration of Temperance. This is the stage of inner maturity.

**Act 3 (15-21) · Into Transcendence**: After the trials of The Devil's bondage and The Tower's collapse,
The Fool moves through the hope of The Star, the darkness of The Moon, and the joy of The Sun, answers
the call of Judgement, and arrives at the completion of The World. This is the stage of integration.
