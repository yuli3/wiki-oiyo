A knight charges at full speed through a headwind. The Knight of Swords runs the moment he reaches a conclusion. This card holds both the fastest mind in tarot and one of its most impatient tempers.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | Knight — The one who acts |
| Key words | Charge, direct speech, swift logic, storm-like drive |

## Upright Meaning

It is time to push forward quickly and decisively. The logic is sound and the drive is strong. Still, look back once in a while so you do not trample the feelings of those around you with your speed.

- **Love**: A direct approach and fast-moving development. You may persuade well, but empathy can be missing.
- **Work & Money**: Quick decisions and head-on breakthroughs work now. Financially, beware of rushed bets; speed and accuracy are not the same.

## Reversed Meaning

Runaway motion without direction, aggressive words, and hasty conclusions. A sword without brakes can cut allies too.
