Saju-palja (四柱八字, the "Four Pillars, Eight Characters") records the year, month, day, and hour of your birth as **four Heavenly Stem characters (the energies of the sky) + four Earthly Branch characters (the seats of the earth)** — eight characters in all. The language that reads the relationships between those eight characters is the **Ten Gods (十神, Sipsin)**.

- **Heavenly Stems (天干, Cheongan)** — the visible archetypes of temperament and character. Your Day Stem (日干, Ilgan) in particular is "you yourself."
- **Earthly Branches (地支, Jiji)** — time (season, hour), environment, and your zodiac animal. If the stems are the actors, the branches are the stage.
- **Ten Gods (十神, Sipsin)** — the language of relationship, read from the Day Stem. Wealth, honor, expression, and learning are all read here.

## The Twelve Stages (十二運星) — The Life Cycle of an Energy

A language that likens the life of a character's energy — from birth to dissolution — to twelve stages of a human life. It is used together in reading the Day Branch and the Luck Pillars (Daeun).

| Stage | Life Phase | Key Traits |
|---|---|---|

| [Geonrok (建祿)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-geonrok/) | Independence | Skill, diligence, self-made success |

## The Sexagenary Cycle (六十甲子) — A Dictionary of 60 Day Pillars

Sixty stem-branch pairs formed by combining the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches — the stem-branch of your birthday is your Day Pillar (日柱, Ilju), the "you yourself" of the chart. We have organized all 60 day pillars, from Gapja to Gyehae, together with their Twelve Stages, Ten Gods, and Sound-Element (Napeum).
