In Korea, a pig dream is one of the most widely recognized dreams of money luck. A fat pig, piglets, or a pig entering the house is often interpreted as an auspicious dream in which blessing and profit come in. For an international reader, it is useful to know that pig dreams in Korea are commonly associated with wealth, good fortune, and sometimes even the idea of buying a lottery ticket after the dream. Still, a dirty or aggressive pig, or a pig that runs away, may point to greed, unmanaged resources, or a missed opportunity. The situation should be read together with the dreamer's feelings.

## At a Glance

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Main symbols | Wealth, good fortune, abundance, greed, practical gain |
| When it is auspicious | A fat pig or piglets entering the home symbolizes money and good news. |
| When it is troubling | If a pig runs away or attacks, it may suggest lost opportunity or excessive desire. |
| Psychological meaning | It is often interpreted as a need for material stability, satisfaction, and regulation of greed. |

## Interpretations by Situation

- **A pig entering the house**: This is read as an auspicious dream of money, guests, or a good proposal coming in.
- **Seeing piglets**: This symbolizes several small opportunities appearing or results that can grow over time.
- **Catching a pig**: This shows a strong will to secure profit or make a goal real.
- **A pig running away**: This reflects worry about missing an opportunity or money you could have held onto.
- **A black pig**: This may be read as substantial money luck, but if the dream felt anxious, it can also warn against greed.

## Psychological Reading

From a Freudian perspective, the pig is often interpreted in relation to desire, the pleasure of eating, and the wish to possess. If the pig felt abundant, it may show satisfaction. If it felt unpleasant, it may reveal guilt about greed.

From a Jungian perspective, the pig can be read as a symbol of earthy abundance and instinct. It may be a message to acknowledge practical needs such as money, food, the body, and material stability.

## If You Had This Dream

You can receive it as a positive dream, but real money decisions still require planning and verification. If an opportunity comes in, review its conditions calmly and prioritize manageable gains over excessive expectations. The more a dream is about abundance, the more it asks you to look at desire and balance together.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does a pig dream mean I should buy a lottery ticket?**

A. Pig dreams are widely known as money-luck dreams in Korea, but they do not guarantee a win. It is better to treat the dream as a symbol that an opportunity may come.

**Q. Is a pig running away a bad dream?**

A. It can be read as a warning about lost opportunity or poor management. Check whether there is something you have recently been missing.
