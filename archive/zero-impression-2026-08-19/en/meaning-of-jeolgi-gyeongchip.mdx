Gyeongchip (驚蟄) is the 3rd of the 24 solar terms, falling around March 5-7 when the sun reaches celestial longitude 345°.

## Meaning and Origin

Gyeongchip means "hibernating insects (蟄) are startled (驚) awake." It is the solar term when frogs and insects are said to wake at the first spring thunder, and the activity of life begins again.

## Customs and Foods

People believed there would be no misfortune if they worked with earth on this day, so they plastered walls or built fences. They also foretold the farming year by observing the growth of barley shoots. There was also a custom in which lovers shared ginkgo nuts to express their feelings, so Gyeongchip is sometimes called a "Korean-style Valentine's Day."

## Saju and the Solar Terms

The calendar used in Myeongli studies moves by solar terms. Gyeongchip is one of the 12 nodes (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in Saju changes to the [Myo (卯) month](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-myo/).** This is why the sexagenary cycle of one's birth month is determined by solar terms, not by the lunar-calendar date.

## Wisdom for This Time of Year

This is the time to wake the body from winter mode — one of the best moments to restart light exercise and morning walks.
