Beneath four flower-decked wands, people dance in welcome. Four of Wands is the first sanctuary reached after effort: a moment when it is right to pause and celebrate.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 4 — Stability and celebration |
| Key words | Celebration, sanctuary, marriage or moving, completion |

## Upright Meaning

One stage has been safely completed, bringing something worth celebrating. This card is deeply connected with joy around home, family, and community, such as moving, marriage, or gatherings.

- **Love**: A stable settling of the relationship, moving toward a stage of "building a home together" such as living together, engagement, or marriage.
- **Work & Money**: A successful wrap-up of a project and harmony within a team. Financially, it supports home-related spending or investment.

## Reversed Meaning

Celebration may be delayed, or there may be instability within the home or team. Often the foundation itself is not collapsing; rather, you have run so hard that you skipped the moment of celebration.

## Today's Advice

> Celebrating what is complete is also fuel for the next stage of growth. Mark the moment, even in a small way.
