A craftsperson sits at a workbench, carving the eighth pentacle. Seven finished pentacles hang neatly nearby — this is mastery created by repetition, the card of someone who has become the process itself.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material life, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 8 — mastery |
| Key words | Training, detail, power of repetition, craftsperson's path |

## Upright Meaning

This is an ideal time to sharpen your skills. Plain repetition creates the compound interest of ability. It is strong luck for certifications, technical training, and building a portfolio.

- **Love**: Relationships also require practice — repeated small acts of care are the mastery of love.
- **Work & Money**: A golden period of focus and training. Financially, you are building the foundation that raises your value, hourly rate, or unit price.

## Reversed Meaning

Soulless repetition, the trap of perfectionism, or a big picture lost under too many details. Lift your head now and then to remember what the training is for.

## Today's Advice

> Carve one more today. The eighth proves the value of the seven before it.
