**When routines crumble, the heart follows — a regular daily life is your safety net.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Moon enters the 6th House, the question "What makes me feel secure?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service**.

## At a Glance

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — Emotions, instincts, need for security |
| House | 6th House · House of Daily Life — Duties, habits, body care |
| Stage of Life | Work, health, routines, service |
| Natural Sign · Classification | Virgo · Cadent House |

## How This Placement Functions

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When the Moon enters this space, the energy of **emotions, instincts, and the need for security** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that manifests after mastery.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive the Moon's gift (**a sense of stability**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Virgo, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Moon in the 6th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Moon in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign the Moon is in.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **birth time and location**. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
