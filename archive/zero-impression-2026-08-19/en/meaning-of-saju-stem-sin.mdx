Xin (辛) is a finished jewel and a sharpened blade. If Geng metal is raw ore, Xin metal is an already refined finished object: small but hard, beautiful yet sharp. As written in hanja, it is "spicy Xin."

## Personality and Temperament

The Xin metal day master is neat, refined, and meticulous in handling work. It has strong aesthetic sense and discernment, and it holds itself to very high standards. Even when it appears gentle, no heavenly stem is better at words that cut to the point.

- **Strengths**: Precision, aesthetic judgment, and clear decision-making. The ability to create value through detail.
- **Points to watch**: Its criticism can be sharp enough to wound others, and it is also deeply cut by small scratches, such as criticism or disregard. Xin metal needs to practice becoming less rigid so perfectionism does not wear it down.

## Suitable Fields

Fields where precision is a weapon, such as design, jewelry, beauty, precision medicine, financial analysis, law, and criticism.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When Xin meets Ren water, or clear water, the jewel is washed and begins to shine. This is called Dosejuok, "washing the jewel," and it is Xin metal's finest combination. Ding fire, by contrast, tries to melt down a jewel that has already been completed, so it is painful for Xin.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Why is Xin metal easily hurt?**

A. Because even a small scratch stands out on a finished jewel. Xin metal's sensitivity is not a weakness, but a high-resolution sense of perception. If that sense is used not to police the self but to refine works and tasks, Xin becomes the heavenly stem that produces the most precise results.
