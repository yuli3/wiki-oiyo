Beneath a rainy sky, three swords pierce a red heart. It is one of tarot's most direct cards of pain — yet the rain is also washing the wound clean.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Position | 3 — Painful growth |
| Key words | Heartbreak, separation, painful truth, cleansing rain |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when emotional pain such as heartbreak, separation, or betrayal is passing through. Do not deny the pain; move through it. A clear wound heals faster than vague anxiety.

- **Love**: Separation, a love triangle, or confirmation of a painful truth. The pain now may also be an incision that keeps the wound from festering.
- **Work & Money**: Painful feedback, rejection, and frustration. Facing a financial loss directly — the pain of cutting losses prevents a greater loss.

## Reversed Meaning

A recovery period after being hurt, or grief delayed while pain is pushed down. The swords must be removed before new skin can grow.

## Advice for Today

> Admitting that what hurts truly hurts is the first step of recovery. For today, that is enough.
