Ipha (立夏) is the 7th of the 24 solar terms, entering around May 5-7 when the sun reaches celestial longitude 45°.

## Meaning and Origin

Ipha means "summer (夏) stands up," marking the beginning of summer in the solar-term calendar. It is the season of fresh greenery, when frogs call more often and fields deepen into green.

## Customs and Foods

Around this time, people ate mugwort rice-cake crumble, and they read a full bloom of retusa fringe tree flowers as an omen of a good harvest. It was also when barley ears began to emerge, making farm households busier.

## Saju and Solar Terms

The calendar used in myeongli, or Korean four-pillar astrology, moves by solar terms. Ipha is one of the 12 nodes (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in saju changes to [the month of Sa (巳)](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/).** This is why the month stem-branch of a birth chart is based on solar terms rather than the lunar calendar date.

## Wisdom for This Time

In this seasonal transition, temperatures rise quickly. Before overloading your schedule, take care of hydration and your sleep rhythm first.
