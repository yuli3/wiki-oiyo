A woman in white gently closes a lion's mouth with her bare hands. There is no whip and no chain. The strength of this card is not suppression, but coexistence: the ability to rule the wildness within you, including instinct, desire, and anger, without fear and with kindness.

## Reading the Symbols

The infinity sign above her head symbolizes the same limitless potential seen in The Magician, the white clothing shows purified intention, and the flower garland represents harmony with nature. The lion is not an enemy to eliminate, but an inner source of energy to tame.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time to overcome a difficult situation through patience and gentleness rather than frontal force. You gain the courage to face fear, and even a rough counterpart can be moved through warmth.

- **Love**: The strength to dissolve conflict through acceptance. This card is especially strong for healing an old relationship.
- **Work & Study**: You conquer difficult people or assignments through persistence. Your true value appears in work that requires heavy emotional labor.
- **Money**: Restraining desire becomes profit. The more you manage your impulses, the more your assets are protected.

## Reversed Meaning

Strength reversed is a state of being dragged along by the lion. Anger, anxiety, or desire may be out of control, or the opposite may be happening: excessive self-suppression is causing inner wounds to fester. "Enduring" and "mastering" are not the same thing.

## Card Combinations

- **Strength + The Chariot**: Inner control and external drive. A combination that can overcome a major challenge.
- **Strength + The Devil**: A signal that the desire to be tamed is larger and deeper than expected. Check addictive patterns.

## FAQ

**Q. How is the "strength" of Strength different from the strength of The Chariot?**

A. If The Chariot is an outward breakthrough driven by will, Strength is inward power that governs through acceptance. The Chariot is the power to win against competition, while Strength is the power to master yourself.
