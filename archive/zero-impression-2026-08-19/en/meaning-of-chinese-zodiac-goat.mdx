<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **Goat** is the eighth animal in the Chinese zodiac and is paired with the
  Earthly Branch **Wei**. It is a symbolic and cultural cycle marker, not a
  deterministic predictor of personality, fortune, or destiny.
</HighlightBox>

## Cultural associations and traditional traits

The Goat is traditionally associated with care, gentleness, aesthetics,
cooperation, patience, and sensitivity to surroundings. In English, this zodiac
animal is sometimes translated as Goat, Sheep, or Ram, because the traditional
term can be rendered differently across languages and local customs. OIYO uses
Goat for consistency with the hub entry.

In zodiac folklore, Goat-year people may be described as considerate, artistic,
peace-seeking, family-oriented, or quietly persistent. These descriptions are
folk associations rather than personality measurements. They can help explain
the symbolic language used in New Year customs and popular calendars, but they
should not be used to decide what a real person must be like.

## Earthly Branch and cycle position

The Goat corresponds to **Wei**, the eighth of the 12 Earthly Branches. It
follows Horse and precedes Monkey in the animal sequence. The animal sign is
the public-facing mnemonic attached to the branch; the fuller traditional
calendar combines Earthly Branches with Heavenly Stems in a 60-year cycle.

Representative Goat-year labels include **1979, 1991, 2003, and 2015**. These
are common Gregorian-year references. People born near the beginning of a
Gregorian year should check the specific zodiac-year boundary, because Lunar
New Year and solar-term calculation can place early January or early February
births in the previous animal year.

## Compatibility folklore

Compatibility folklore often groups the Goat with **Rabbit** and **Pig** in a
harmonious triad. Goat and **Ox** are commonly treated as a clashing pair. This
is a symbolic classification used in traditional matching language, not a rule
that relationships must follow. Real relationships depend on people and
circumstances, not only on calendar labels.

## Common Misunderstanding

The Goat sign does not prove that someone is gentle, passive, artistic,
dependent, fortunate, or unfortunate. It is a birth-year animal label attached
to the Earthly Branch Wei. A full East Asian fortune reading uses the Four
Pillars, or saju, which reads year, month, day, and hour together through
Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. See the broader
[Chinese zodiac definition](/en/meaning-of-chinese-zodiac/) and the related
[Palja definition](/en/meaning-of-palja/) for context.

## Responsible use

Goat symbolism is a cultural and symbolic tradition for reflection and
entertainment. It should not be used as deterministic prediction or as evidence
for judging personality, compatibility, or life direction.
