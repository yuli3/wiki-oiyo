Inside a cathedral, a stonemason discusses the plans with a monk and an architect. The Three of Pentacles is the moment when skill is first recognized in public — and an achievement built not alone, but together.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material life, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Position | 3 — Collaborative growth |
| Key words | Craftsmanship, teamwork, recognized skill, shared work |

## Upright Meaning

Your skill and sincerity are beginning to be recognized. Strong results come from collaboration that gathers each person's expertise, and this is a time to build while learning and learn while building.

- **Love**: A relationship in which you are creating something together. A shared goal makes the two of you stronger.
- **Work & Money**: Collaborative projects go well, and your ability receives formal recognition. Financially, your skills begin to turn into compensation.

## Reversed Meaning

A team whose hands and feet are out of sync, effort that goes unrecognized, or foundations built too roughly. A wall built without a blueprint will have to be built again.

## Advice for Today

> Work as if you are leaving your signature on today's task. A craftsperson's reputation begins in the details.
