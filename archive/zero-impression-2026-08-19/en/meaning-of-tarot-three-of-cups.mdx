Three women lift their cups high and dance amid an abundant harvest. The Three of Cups is the joy that grows because it is shared, a card of the vitality given by friendship and community.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 3 — growth and cooperation |
| Key words | Celebration, friendship, communal joy, gatherings |

## Upright Meaning

Celebrations and enjoyable gatherings increase. Connections with friends and colleagues become channels of good fortune, so do not turn down invitations too quickly.

- **Love**: A connection that begins through a social gathering, or a lover who feels like a friend. The relationship ripens with the blessing of people around you.
- **Work & Money**: The fruits of teamwork and success in collaborative projects. Financially, spending on gatherings may increase, but it is a period when that spending is worth it.

## Reversed Meaning

Fatigue from gatherings, rumors and backbiting, or the subtle triangular dynamic created by three people. It is time to choose carefully who you raise your glass with.

## Today's Advice

> Distinguish gatherings that give you energy from gatherings that take it away, and raise your cup only to the former.
