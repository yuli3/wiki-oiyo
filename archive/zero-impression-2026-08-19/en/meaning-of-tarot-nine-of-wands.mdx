A bandaged figure leans on a staff, staying alert. They have endured eight times, and now comes the ninth and final one — the most exhausted point, but also the closest.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 9 — the final hurdle |
| Key words | Perseverance, wounded defense, one last effort, vigilance |

## Upright Meaning

You are tired, but you have almost arrived. The wounds you have taken so far have become experience, and if you endure one more time, you reach completion in the Ten of Wands. You have come too far to give up.

- **Love**: The guardedness of a wounded heart. Check your defenses, but do not place every person in the category of enemy.
- **Work & Money**: A final hurdle and the last stage of review. Financially, the right approach is to endure while protecting your emergency reserve.

## Reversed Meaning

Exhaustion and a sense of victimhood, or boundaries so excessive that even help is rejected. Enduring and hardening into stone are not the same thing.

## Today's Advice

> In the final kilometer, the answer is not new strength but maintaining your pace.
