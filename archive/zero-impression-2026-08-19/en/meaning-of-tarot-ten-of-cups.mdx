Beneath a rainbow, a couple opens their arms while children dance beside them. Ten of Cups is the destination of the emotional journey: a completion of happiness achieved together, beyond the private satisfaction of Nine of Cups.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 10 — Completion |
| Key words | Family happiness, emotional fulfillment, rainbow, harmony |

## Upright Meaning

Harmony in the family or community reaches its peak. This is a time when bonds with loved ones become your greatest asset, and it is one of the warmest cards in tarot.

- **Love**: The completion of love that leads to marriage or family, and the promise of lasting happiness.
- **Work & Money**: A period when work and life come into balance. Money shines most when it is used for the people you call family.

## Reversed Meaning

There may be a gap between the ideal image of family and reality, or discord among family members. Matching real hearts matters before creating a perfect picture.

> The rainbow is not far away. The conversation at tonight's dinner table is where it begins.
