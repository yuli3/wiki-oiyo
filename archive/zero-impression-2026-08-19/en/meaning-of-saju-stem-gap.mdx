Gap (甲) is the first character of the Ten Heavenly Stems. It represents the sprout that first breaks through the earth in spring and the giant tree that grows straight toward the sky. As the eldest of the Heavenly Stems, it represents the energy of initiation and leadership.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gap Wood Day Master is honest and upright, naturally feeling comfortable at the front of a group. They value principles and pride, and once a direction is set, they do not change it easily. With a strong pioneering spirit for starting new ventures, they shine brightest when taking on the role of "blazing a new trail" within an organization.

- **Strengths**: Drive, sense of responsibility, and honesty. A pillar-like presence that maintains stability during a crisis.
- **Points of Caution**: A large tree cannot bend. They may lack flexibility, and because they would rather break than bend, they risk being snapped. If their pride is wounded, recovery can be slow.

## Suitable Fields

Management/Entrepreneurship, Planning, Education, Public Service, and any role that involves setting a direction and taking responsibility.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When Eul Wood (vine) leans on them, they provide shelter, but they may also have their nutrients drained. Geng Metal (axe) is a tense relationship that refines Gap Wood into useful timber—hardship is synonymous with growth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I am a Gap Wood, but the position of a leader feels burdensome. Is my Saju (Four Pillars) wrong?**

A. The essence of Gap Wood is not about a "title," but about "directionality." Even without an official title, the energy of Gap Wood is sufficiently expressed by setting one's own standards for life and walking uprightly. The form of leadership also varies depending on the composition of other characters (such as the Officer or Resource stars).
