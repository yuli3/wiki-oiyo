A blindfolded woman sits with her back to the sea, holding two crossed swords in front of her chest. This is balance chosen by refusing to look — a precarious peace maintained by delaying a decision.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Position | 2 — Balance and choice |
| Key words | Delayed decision, blindfold, stalemate, truce of the mind |

## Upright Meaning

Judgment is being postponed in the face of an either-or choice. If you need more information, it is a valid truce. If it comes from fear, it is only a stalemate. The blindfold was tied not by someone else, but by yourself.

- **Love**: An inability to decide how you feel, or a cold war in a conflict. The longer the silence lasts, the heavier the swords become.
- **Work & Money**: A postponed decision, or a position where neutrality is forced on you. Financially, watching and waiting can work, but set a deadline.

## Reversed Meaning

The stalemate begins to loosen, and a decision may be forced. The time has come to face the truth you have postponed.

## Advice for Today

> Delaying a decision is also a decision. Decide now at least how long the delay will last.
