Soe (衰) is the sixth stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Stages of Life), marking the point where the energy begins to wane after passing the peak of the Emperor (帝旺). While the character itself suggests "decline," its true meaning is closer to the **wisdom of knowing when to step back**.

## Meaning and Symbolism

Soe represents the energy of a field after the harvest. Rather than flashy expansion, it excels at protecting and refining what has already been gathered. It possesses the ability to distinguish between what is possible and what is not without overextending—an asset of Soe that younger stars do not possess.

## Temperament of a Chart with Strong Soe

A person with Soe in their Day Branch (Ilji) is the type who prefers to step back and observe the big picture rather than rushing to the forefront. Their true value shines in roles where experience is a weapon, such as advisors, managers, or specialized professionals.

- **Strengths**: Prudence in anticipating risks, stable management skills, and a mature sense of balance.
- **Points to Note**: If passivity becomes excessive, one may hesitate even when faced with opportunities. One must be careful that the "power to protect" does not turn into a "habit of avoidance."

## Encountering Soe in Daewoon or Sewoon

The Soe luck cycle is more favorable for organization than for expansion. Using this time to consolidate the internal stability of ongoing projects and to audit finances and health will serve as a foundation for the next period of growth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does having Soe mean my luck is weak?**

A. No. The "strength" or "weakness" in Sibiunseong refers only to the state of energy, not to fortune or misfortune. In fact, for a chart where the Day Master (Ilgan) is excessively strong, Soe acts as a beneficial element that provides balance.
