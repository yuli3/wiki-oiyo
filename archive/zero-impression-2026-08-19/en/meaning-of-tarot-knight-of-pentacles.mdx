A knight on a black horse stands still before a plowed field. Among the four knights, he is the only one who does not run. The Knight of Pentacles is the person who eventually arrives through endurance rather than explosive speed.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material matters, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | Knight — The one who acts |
| Key words | Consistency, cautious progress, completing responsibility, slow but certain |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period of progress that is not flashy but is certain. The responsibility to complete what you have taken on builds trust, and that trust brings the next opportunity. Routine is your strongest weapon.

- **Love**: A slow but sincere approach. This love is proven through consistency more than events.
- **Work & Money**: Good fortune for long-term projects and operational work. Financially, recurring deposits and long-term investing are the right answer now.

## Reversed Meaning

Consistency has hardened into stagnation, and caution into dull stubbornness. If you are plowing the same field over and over, check the route.

## Today's Advice

> Do not double your speed. Cut the number of days you stop in half.
