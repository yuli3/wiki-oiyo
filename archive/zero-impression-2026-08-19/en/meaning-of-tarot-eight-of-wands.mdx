Eight staffs fly across a clear sky. This is a rare card with no person in the image — a time of tailwind, when the situation itself moves quickly.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 8 — acceleration |
| Key words | Fast development, news, movement, tailwind |

## Upright Meaning

Stagnation clears, and everything moves quickly. A message or result you have been waiting for arrives, and travel or movement becomes active. Get into the flow.

- **Love**: A time of rapid progress — quick messages, sudden meetings, and fast-moving development.
- **Work & Money**: Simultaneous progress and fast handling. Money flow also speeds up, so add precision to deposits and withdrawals.

## Reversed Meaning

Confusion caused by excessive speed, or frustrating delay just before arrival. The problem may be direction, not speed.

## Today's Advice

> When the wind is at your back, you do not row harder. You adjust the sail.
