A queen sits confidently on her throne holding a sunflower, with a black cat at her feet. The Queen of Wands is the light that comes from living as oneself — an embodiment of warm yet powerful charm.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | Queen — the one who holds |
| Key words | Confidence, charm, warm charisma, self-assurance |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when confidence and charm reach their peak. Your power to gather and encourage people comes forward, and the more you act like yourself, the more things begin to open.

- **Love**: A time when your charm shines and popularity follows. In relationships too, a sincere and confident attitude is loved.
- **Work & Money**: A favorable period for becoming the center of a team and for public-facing activity. Financially, investing in an area where you have confidence can bring results.

## Reversed Meaning

Confidence may be broken and turn into shrinking back, or it may be distorted into jealousy and display. When you keep glancing at someone else's stage, the queen's light grows dim.

## Today's Advice

> The moment you stop comparing, your sunflower finds the sun again.
