Soseo (小暑) is the 11th of the 24 solar terms, occurring around July 6-8, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 105°.

## Meaning and origin

Soseo means "small (小) heat (暑)," and it marks the beginning of truly hot weather. Because the monsoon front often stays over the Korean Peninsula around this time, the weather becomes humid and hot.

## Customs and foods

People enjoyed wheat-based foods so they would not lose their appetite in the heat — this solar term was considered the right season for noodles and sujebi made from newly harvested wheat. Seasonal fruits such as watermelon and Korean melon also begin to grow sweet around this time.

## Saju and solar terms

The calendar used in myeongli moves by solar terms. Soseo is one of the 12 solar nodes (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in saju changes to the [Mi (未) month](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-mi/).** This is why solar terms, not lunar calendar dates, are used as the standard when determining the sexagenary sign of the birth month.

## Wisdom for this time of year

Set up a rhythm of rest at the beginning of the heat. The strength to endure midsummer comes from the rest taken at Soseo.
