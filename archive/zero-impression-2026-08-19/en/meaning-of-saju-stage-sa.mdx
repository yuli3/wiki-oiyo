Sa (死) is the eighth stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Stages of Life), representing the point where vital activity comes to a halt. Although the name carries a heavy connotation, its interpretation in Saju (Four Pillars) is different—because movement has ceased, the **spirit converges into a single point, making it the star of immersion and expertise.**

## Meaning and Symbolism

Sa is a state where all distractions have been completely cleared away. The essence of this star is the power to dig one well without straying, and the persistence to delve into tasks that others might abandon out of boredom. This is why it is often called the star of scholars, artisans, and researchers.

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in Sa

A person with Sa in their Ilji (Day Branch) prefers narrow and deep relationships over broad and shallow ones, and chooses essence over trends. Though they may appear quiet, they are more tenacious than anyone else in their chosen field.

- **Strengths**: Deep focus, authority in specialized fields, and a calm, serious sense of reliability.
- **Points of Caution**: Flexibility and spontaneity can be weaknesses. Because their thoughts are deep, their actions may be slow, and it takes time for them to recover from a low mood.

## Encountering Sa in Daewoon or Sewoon

The Sa luck cycle is a period where external expansion pauses, but inner strength accumulates. It is considered the best time for solitary, deep-dive work such as obtaining certifications, pursuing degrees, conducting research, or writing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does having Sa mean something ominous will happen?**

A. No. Sa in Sibiunseong is not a harbinger of death, but a metaphor for a state where "active energy has become still." In actual practice, it is often interpreted auspiciously as a star of academics and technical skills.
