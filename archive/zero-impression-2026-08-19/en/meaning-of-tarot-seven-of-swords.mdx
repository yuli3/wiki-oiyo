A figure tiptoes out of an enemy camp carrying five swords, yet two swords have been left behind, and someone in the distance seems to have noticed. This is a card poised between cleverness and deception.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 7 — Strategy |
| Key words | Deception, indirect tactics, solo action, tricks that may be exposed |

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when indirect movement and strategy may be needed instead of a head-on breakthrough. However, the moment cleverness turns into deceit, everything becomes dangerous. Also watch for dishonest movement around you.

- **Love**: A relationship with something hidden, or the shadow of suspicion. Unconfirmed suspicion is poisonous in itself.
- **Work & Money**: A period of information battles and behind-the-scenes work. Financially, this is a warning to guard against scams and trick deals.

## Reversed Meaning

Something hidden is revealed, or you decide to reverse course according to your conscience. Late honesty is better than early deception.

## Today's Advice

> The difference between strategy and deception: if you would feel ashamed when exposed, it is deception.
