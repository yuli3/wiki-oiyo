A knight on a rearing horse, flame patterns flying over his armor. The Knight of Wands is a person whose body moves at the same time as the thought arrives — the fastest and hottest court card in tarot.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | Knight — the one who acts |
| Key words | Charge, adventure, charisma, impulse |

## Upright Meaning

This is the time to move boldly. Speed and daring create opportunities, and your energy draws people around you. It is a strong sign for travel, movement, and challenge.

- **Love**: Passionate pursuit and a whirlwind romance. The heat is real, but whether it can last needs to be checked separately.
- **Work & Money**: A period when aggressive momentum works. Financially, fast decisions can magnify both gains and losses, so set an upper limit in advance.

## Reversed Meaning

A reckless charge, a race with no finish, a hot-tempered impulse. It is time to get off the horse and look at the map.

## Today's Advice

> Before you set out, decide just one thing: where you will stop.
