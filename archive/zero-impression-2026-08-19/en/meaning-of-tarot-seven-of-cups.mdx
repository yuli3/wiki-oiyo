Seven cups hover on clouds, filled with a castle, jewels, a laurel wreath, a dragon, and even a veiled figure. Everything seems possible, but nothing is in your hands yet. This is a display case of fantasies.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 7 — Illusion and choice |
| Key words | Daydreams, too many options, fantasy, priorities |

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when there are so many choices that you may struggle to choose at all. Imagination becomes rich, but you must tell whether what is inside each cup is real or only foam.

- **Love**: A fantasy of the ideal partner, or wandering among several attractions. Look at the real person in front of you, not the person in your imagination.
- **Work & Money**: Too many ideas, seven plans and no decision. The sweeter a financial offer sounds, the more carefully you need to verify its substance.

## Reversed Meaning

The fog clears, and the moment comes to choose one thing. You wake from fantasy and become able to make a realistic decision.

## Today's Advice

> Of the seven cups, there is usually only one whose price you are truly willing to pay.
