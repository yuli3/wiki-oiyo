Mogyok (沐浴) is the second stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Growth Stages), representing the moment a newborn is bathed. It is a time of first encountering the impurities of the world—traditionally known as the star of puberty and the energy of Peach Blossom (桃花), symbolizing both charm and fluctuation.

## Meaning and Symbolism

Mogyok, derived from the image of being naked and bathing, signifies "exposure" and "sensitivity." While it possesses a power that is sensitive to trends and beauty, attracting the attention of others, it is also characterized by a lack of internal stability, leading to frequent changes in mindset and environment. The reason ancient texts on Saju (Four Pillars) often referred to Mogyok as "Pae (敗, failure/defeat)" is due to this inherent volatility.

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in Mogyok

Individuals with Mogyok in their Ilji (Day Branch) possess refined sensibilities and natural self-expression, making them stand out in any setting. They are deeply connected to fields that trade in aesthetics, such as art, beauty, fashion, and broadcasting.

- **Strengths**: Aesthetic sense, popular appeal, and a high sensitivity that allows for the rapid adoption of new things.
- **Points of Caution**: They tend to get bored easily and find it difficult to stay in one place for long. Frequent changes in relationships or careers are signs of the Mogyok energy when it has not yet been refined.

## Encountering Mogyok in Daewoon or Sewoon

When encountering Mogyok in Daewoon (10-year luck) or Sewoon (annual luck), one often experiences an increase in romantic luck and popularity, but this comes alongside temptations and potential for trial and error. The classic advice is to broaden one's experiences while taking a step back before making major contracts or life-altering decisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is it true that having Mogyok in one's chart means they are prone to infidelity?**

A. The formula "Mogyok = infidelity" is an oversimplification. The essence of Mogyok is the "opening of the senses," and in many cases, this manifests as artistic talent. One must always analyze the configuration of the Sipsin (Ten Gods) and the overall balance of the chart.
