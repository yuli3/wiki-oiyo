<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **Snake** is the sixth animal in the Chinese zodiac and is paired with the
  Earthly Branch **Si**. It is a symbolic and cultural cycle marker, not a
  deterministic predictor of personality, fortune, or destiny.
</HighlightBox>

## Cultural associations and traditional traits

The Snake is traditionally associated with discretion, insight, strategy,
patience, transformation, and hidden knowledge. In many symbolic systems, the
snake can suggest both danger and wisdom, because it moves quietly, sheds its
skin, and appears suddenly from concealment.

In zodiac folklore, Snake-year people may be described as observant, refined,
private, analytical, or hard to read. These descriptions are inherited cultural
associations, not psychological measurements. They can be useful for
understanding symbolic language, but they should not be used to label a real
person's character or motives.

## Earthly Branch and cycle position

The Snake corresponds to **Si**, the sixth of the 12 Earthly Branches. It
follows Dragon and precedes Horse in the animal sequence. The animal name is the
popular mnemonic; Si is the branch used in the traditional calendrical system.
Combined with the 10 Heavenly Stems, it participates in the broader 60-year
cycle.

Representative recent Snake years include **1977, 1989, 2001, 2013, and
2025**. These are common Gregorian-year labels. People born in January or early
February should verify the exact calendar boundary, because zodiac years may be
counted from Lunar New Year in popular use or from solar terms in Four Pillars
practice.

## Compatibility folklore

Compatibility folklore often groups the Snake with **Ox** and **Rooster** in a
harmonious triad. Snake and **Pig** are commonly treated as a clashing pair.
These are symbolic classifications within traditional matching language, not
evidence that a relationship will succeed or fail.

## Common Misunderstanding

The Snake sign does not prove that someone is secretive, wise, dangerous,
seductive, or destined for a particular outcome. It is a birth-year animal label
attached to the Earthly Branch Si. A full East Asian fortune reading uses the
Four Pillars, or saju, which reads year, month, day, and hour together through
Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. See the broader
[Chinese zodiac definition](/en/meaning-of-chinese-zodiac/) and the related
[Palja definition](/en/meaning-of-palja/) for context.

## Responsible use

Snake symbolism is a cultural and symbolic tradition for reflection and
entertainment. It should not be used as deterministic prediction or as evidence
for judging personality, compatibility, or life direction.
