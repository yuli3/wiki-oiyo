The Grave (墓) is the ninth stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Stages of Life), representing the position where energy is stored away in a tomb—or more accurately, a warehouse (庫). In Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny), the Grave is referred to as 'Myogo (墓庫)' and is interpreted as the **power to gather, store, and bring things to a conclusion.**

## Meaning and Symbolism

The Grave represents the act of bringing harvested grain into a warehouse. It is the energy of gathering what is scattered, distinguishing between what to use and what to keep, and locking the door securely—this is why those with the Grave star possess exceptional talent for management and accumulation.

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in the Grave

Individuals with the Grave in their Day Pillar (Ilji) do not waste resources. Whether it is goods, money, or information, they find a sense of security in systematically collecting and categorizing things. They often have deep connections to fields such as accounting, asset management, record-keeping, and archiving.

- **Strengths**: Frugal management and saving ability, aptitude for finishing and organizing, and an eye for recognizing the value of old things.
- **Points of Caution**: If the power to accumulate becomes excessive, it can turn into obsession and stinginess. If one locks the door to their heart as well, there will be no room for good relationships to enter.

## Encountering the Grave in Daewoon or Sewoon

The Grave luck cycle is a time for harvesting and organizing. It is favorable for saving, managing assets, and completing projects. Classical advice suggests using this time to fill the storehouse for the next cycle rather than starting new ventures.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I read that having the Grave makes one gloomy.**

A. That is a negative exaggeration of the Grave's introverted and cautious nature. The essence of the Grave is 'preservation,' and in modern contexts, it is much more frequently manifested as asset management skills and meticulous work habits.
