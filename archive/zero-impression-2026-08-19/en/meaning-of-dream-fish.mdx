Fish dreams combine the symbolism of water with the symbolism of life. In traditional Korean dream interpretation, catching a large fish or seeing fish swimming in clear water was viewed as a sign of wealth, conception dreams, and achievement. By contrast, dead fish or fish in murky water may indicate weakening opportunities or a clouded emotional state.

## At a Glance

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Main symbols | Wealth, conception dreams, possibility, emotion, the unconscious |
| When auspicious | Catching a large fish or seeing one in clear water symbolizes achievement and good news. |
| When inauspicious | A dead fish or a scene where you lose a fish may mean lost opportunity and disappointment. |
| Psychological meaning | Often interpreted as ideas in the unconscious, emotional vitality, and potential. |

## Meanings by Situation

- **Dream of catching a large fish**: Seen as an auspicious dream about achievement, passing an exam, wealth, or seizing a precious opportunity.
- **Dream of fish swimming in clear water**: Your emotions are healthy, and events are flowing naturally.
- **Dream of letting a fish get away**: A worry that you may miss an opportunity because of insufficient preparation or hesitation.
- **Dream of a dead fish**: Something you had hoped for may be losing momentum, or your emotional vitality may be reduced.
- **Dream of eating fish**: You are digesting an opportunity as your own and absorbing knowledge or resources.

## Reading It Through Psychology

From a Freudian perspective, fish are often interpreted as symbols of desires and vitality hidden beneath the water. A scene of trying to catch one may show a wish to grasp an achievement or a desire that has not yet been put into words.

From a Jungian perspective, a fish is an insight rising from the depths of the unconscious. Fish in clear water point to visible possibilities, while fish in cloudy water represent emotions and ideas that are not yet understood.

## If You Had This Dream

Do not let a recent idea or proposal simply pass by; try writing it down. If the dream was about catching a fish, preparing to make the opportunity real matters. Rather than defining it only as a conception dream, you can also read it as a symbol of potential that is growing.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is a dream about a large fish a good dream?**

A. It is generally seen as auspicious. It is often interpreted as a symbol of wealth, achievement, conception dreams, and major opportunities.

**Q. Is a dream about a dead fish a bad dream?**

A. It may mean disappointment or a weakening opportunity. However, it can be helpful to take it as a signal to review your current plans.
