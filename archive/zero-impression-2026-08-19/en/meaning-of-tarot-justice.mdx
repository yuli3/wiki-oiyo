A figure seated between two pillars holds a sword in the right hand and scales in the left. There is no blindfold. Tarot's Justice is not mechanical fairness with closed eyes, but precise judgment made after looking directly at everything.

## Reading the Symbols

The upright sword symbolizes clear reason, the scales represent the balance between cause and effect, and the red robe shows the weight of responsibility that follows judgment. The number 11 is 1+1: the balance point between two parties.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when you reap what you have sown. If you have built honestly, fair reward arrives. If you have postponed responsibility, settlement follows. Contracts, legal matters, exams, and other official judgments are favored.

- **Love**: A check on the balance of the relationship. It asks whether give and take are fair, and whether it is time for an official decision such as marriage.
- **Work & Study**: Results in evaluations or reviews reflect your actual ability. Keep documents and records accurate.
- **Money**: Matters involving settlements, taxes, and contracts move forward. Transparency is the strongest strategy.

## Reversed Meaning

Justice reversed is a tilted scale. It points to unfair treatment, biased judgment, or avoidance of your own responsibility. Even if things feel unfair right now, it can also advise you to organize records and evidence while waiting for the right time.

## Card Combinations

- **Justice + The Emperor**: A strong union of systems and principles. Favorable in legal disputes.
- **Justice + The Moon**: A truth has not yet been revealed. A signal to withhold judgment.

## FAQ

**Q. Is Justice connected with trials or lawsuits?**

A. In legal questions, it is one of the clearest cards for a fair outcome based on truth. Upright, it favors the honest side. Reversed, it can suggest delays in procedure or the possibility of bias. In everyday questions, read it as "a time when a decision must be made."
