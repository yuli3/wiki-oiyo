<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  A **zodiac sign** is not a planet and not a prediction. It is one of twelve
  archetypal filters in Western astrology: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo,
  Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces.
</HighlightBox>

## What Zodiac Signs Do

In a birth chart, planets describe **what** kind of psychological energy is active. Houses describe **where** it appears in life. Zodiac signs describe **how** that energy behaves.

For example, Mars represents action. Mars in Aries acts quickly and directly; Mars in Virgo acts carefully and analytically; Mars in Pisces acts through intuition and emotion.

## The Four Elements

The twelve signs are grouped into four elemental temperaments.

| Element | Signs | Core Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Inspiration, courage, immediacy |
| Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Stability, realism, material craft |
| Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Ideas, language, social connection |
| Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Emotion, memory, depth |

## The Three Modalities

Signs are also grouped by modality.

- **Cardinal:** Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Initiates motion.
- **Fixed:** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Stabilizes and intensifies.
- **Mutable:** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces. Adapts and translates.

## Common Misunderstanding

Your "zodiac sign" in popular conversation usually means your **Sun sign**. A full chart also includes Moon sign, Rising sign, and every planet's sign. This is why two people with the same Sun sign can feel very different.

### FAQ

**Are zodiac signs scientific categories?**  
No. They are symbolic categories used in astrological interpretation, not laboratory personality types.

**Which sign matters most?**  
For quick horoscope content, the Sun sign is common. For chart interpretation, Sun, Moon, Rising, and planetary placements are read together.
