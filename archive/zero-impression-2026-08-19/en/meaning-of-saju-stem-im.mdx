Im (壬) is the sea where all streams gather. It has no fixed form, so it can be held in any vessel; its depth cannot be known, and it flows without stopping. Among the Heavenly Stems, it symbolizes wisdom on the largest scale.

## Personality and Temperament

The Im Water Day Master thinks quickly, has a broad field of vision, and carries an inclusive quality that allows them to get along with almost anyone. As a free spirit who does not remain stagnant in one place, they think on a large scale and look toward the future. They also have a depth that does not reveal everything inside.

- **Strengths**: Insight, strategic thinking, and inclusiveness. The ability to read the larger board and gather people.
- **Points of Caution**: Great water must beware of flooding. Watch for fickleness and scattered attention, a pattern of starting many things without resolving them, and the impression that others cannot know what is inside.

## Suitable Fields

Strategy and planning, trade and overseas work, IT and data, scholarship, distribution, and other fields that handle flow and scale.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When it meets Byeong Fire (the sun), it creates the magnificent scene of the sun rising over the sea: honor and wisdom come together. Mu Earth (a levee) is needed for great water to have direction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I am Im Water, but I cannot stay with one thing for very long.**

A. The nature of water is "flow," so stagnation is difficult to endure. Rather than changing the job itself too often, the long-term strategy that suits Im Water is to choose an environment where flow keeps appearing within one profession, such as new projects, rotation, or expansion.
