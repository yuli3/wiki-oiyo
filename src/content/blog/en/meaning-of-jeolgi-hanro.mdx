Hanro (寒露) is the 17th of the 24 solar terms. It begins around October 7-9, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 195°.

## Meaning and Origin

Hanro means "cold (寒) dew (露)." It is the solar term just before dew turns into frost. It marks deep autumn, when the fall colors intensify and chrysanthemums come into bloom.

## Customs and Foods

People made chrysanthemum pancakes and brewed chrysanthemum wine, and they also enjoyed chueotang, a loach soup, to restore strength. This is also the time when summer birds such as swallows give way to winter birds such as wild geese.

## Saju and the Solar Terms

The calendar used in myeongli, or Four Pillars astrology, moves according to the solar terms. Hanro is one of the 12 solar-term nodes (節), and **from this day, the saju month pillar (月柱) changes to [the month of Sul (戌)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-branch-sul/).** This is why the sexagenary sign of the birth month is based on the solar term, not the lunar calendar date.

## Wisdom for This Time of Year

When the cold energy begins to arrive, warm yourself with hot broth dishes and start preparing for winter.
