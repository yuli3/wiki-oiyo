**Mars of ambition — professional fighting power that pushes all the way to the summit.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mars is in the 10th house, the question "what do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the realm of **career, reputation, and social calling**.

## How This Placement Works

The 10th house deals with career, reputation, and social calling. When Mars enters this house, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the places in the chart where expression is most visible — this placement is easy for other people to notice as well.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of career, reputation, and social calling receives Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Capricorn, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more dimensional way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 10th house is **overconcentration on that stage** — balance is needed so that career, reputation, and social calling do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (stage). With Mars in the 10th house, the stage is career, reputation, and social calling, and the acting tone is determined by the sign where Mars is placed.

**Q. What do I need in order to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
