A knight wearing a winged helmet moves forward slowly, holding up a cup. The Knight of Cups is someone arriving with the heart in hand: a card in which feelings take form as a confession, invitation, or proposal.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotion, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | Knight — The one who acts |
| Key words | Romantic offer, charm, feeling-led action, knight in shining armor |

## Upright Meaning

A romantic proposal or heartfelt invitation arrives. If you are the one offering it, sincerity is better received when it has a clear form.

- **Love**: A card of confession, proposal, or romantic event. It can also mean the appearance of someone who feels like an ideal partner.
- **Work & Money**: A proposal or collaboration request that moves the heart. Financially, this is a feeling-driven decision, so check the terms one more time.

## Reversed Meaning

Romance made only of words, promises that cannot be kept, or judgment intoxicated by emotion. Look not at the hand holding the cup, but at the road that person has walked.

## Today's Advice

> If you are going to express your heart, do it today, and give it a proper form. Romance is the art of timing.
