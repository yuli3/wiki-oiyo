**The transformation of work and health — power structures in daily life are exposed.** Pluto is the planet of "what does our generation end and remake," and Virgo makes that answer come out carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Pluto ♇ — transformation, power, rebirth |
| Sign | Virgo ♍ — analysis, improvement, usefulness |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 248 years (12–30 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Pluto governs **transformation, power, rebirth**. When that energy passes through Virgo, it shows up carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

This is why the same Pluto can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Virgo's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **analysis that misses no detail**
- **quiet, reliable competence**
- **ceaseless self-improvement**

## Watch out for

- perfectionism delays the start
- the critic's edge turns on the self

## How to read it as a generational placement

Pluto stays in one sign for years or decades, so Pluto in Virgo is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Pluto is in Virgo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Pluto sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
