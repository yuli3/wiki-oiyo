**A slow-boiling force — explosions are rare, but stopping is hard once it moves.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Taurus makes that answer come out slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Taurus ♉ — stability, the senses, accumulation |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Taurus, it shows up slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Taurus's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the stamina to see things through**
- **a grounded feel for real resources**
- **unshakeable calm**

## Watch out for

- change itself can feel like a threat
- stubbornness can block flexibility

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Taurus way (slowly but surely, savoring every sense) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Taurus, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
