**A born front-runner — your very presence is a starting signal.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Aries makes that answer come out quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Aries ♈ — initiative, courage, directness |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Mars |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Aries, it shows up quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aries's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the drive to simply begin**
- **courage that shines in a crisis**
- **unvarnished honesty**

## Watch out for

- haste can blur the finish
- competitiveness invites needless conflict

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Aries way (quickly and bluntly, without hesitation) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Aries, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
