If a sign is the **style** of a planet, the house is the **stage of life** where that planet performs.
Because houses are determined by the exact time and place of birth, even people born on the same day can have completely different house placements.

## A Glance at the 12 Houses

| House | Name | Stage | Natural Sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st House | House of Self | First impressions, body, way of starting | Aries |
| 2nd House | House of Possessions | Money, talents, self-worth | Taurus |
| 3rd House | House of Communication | Speech, learning, siblings, neighborhood | Gemini |
| 4th House | House of Roots | Home, parents, inner foundation | Cancer |
| 5th House | House of Creativity | Romance, play, self-expression, children | Leo |
| 6th House | House of Daily Life | Work, health, routines, service | Virgo |
| 7th House | House of Relationships | Partnerships, marriage, contracts | Libra |
| 8th House | House of Transformation | Shared resources, intimacy, crisis and rebirth | Scorpio |
| 9th House | House of Exploration | Philosophy, higher education, long-distance travel | Sagittarius |
| 10th House | House of Achievement | Career, reputation, social calling | Capricorn |
| 11th House | House of Community | Friends, networks, future vision | Aquarius |
| 12th House | House of the Unconscious | Solitude, spirituality, hidden patterns | Pisces |
