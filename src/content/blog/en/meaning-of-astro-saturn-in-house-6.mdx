**Discipline of work and the body — If you build a system, you will outlast anyone.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Saturn enters the 6th House, the question "What tests must I pass to become resilient?" unfolds in the realms of **work, health, routines, and service.**

## How This Placement Functions

The 6th House deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Saturn enters this house, the energy of **limitations, responsibilities, and the tasks of mastery** resides on that stage.

A Cadent House is a place of learning and adaptation — this placement functions as an internal process that manifests only after mastery is achieved.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of work, health, routines, and service receive Saturn's gift (**solidity**). By referring to the nature of Virgo, the natural sign of this house, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in more depth.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Saturn in the 6th House is **becoming overly fixated on that stage** — balance is required so that the areas of work, health, routines, and service do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Saturn in the 6th House, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Saturn is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
