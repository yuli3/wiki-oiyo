Tae (胎) is the eleventh stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Stages of Life), representing the moment a new life is first conceived within the mother's womb. Although it lacks physical form, it is undeniably present—it is the star of **ideas, hope, and fragile yet growing potential.**

## At a Glance

| Life Stage | Conception — When life is formed in an unseen place |
| State of Energy | Weak, but clearly beginning to grow |

## Meaning and Symbolism

Tae represents an energy that is "not yet, but soon to be." Therefore, the star of Tae shines more in conception than in completion, and more in ideation than in execution. A person who always has new plans growing in their mind—that is the portrait of Tae.

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in Tae

Those with Tae in their Day Pillar (Ilji) are curious and dream big. They excel at tasks that create something from nothing, such as planning, R&D, and creative work, but they often require the support of other characters to push those plans to completion.

- **Strengths**: Fresh ideas and planning ability, pure optimism, and the imagination to envision the future.
- **Points of Caution**: Like a fetus, they are sensitive to external shocks. They may easily shrink back from criticism. If plans frequently remain just plans, it is recommended to have a partner or system nearby to assist with execution.

## Encountering Tae in Daewoon or Sewoon

The energy of Tae is a period where the next chapter is being conceived in an unseen place. Even if immediate results are scarce, the ideas cultivated during this time often become one's main occupation later on; therefore, it is wise not to spare time for learning and conceptualization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Both Tae and Jangsaeng (長生) represent a "beginning." How are they different?**

A. Tae is conception (an unseen beginning), while Jangsaeng is birth (a beginning revealed to the world). If Tae is the idea stage, Jangsaeng is the first stage of execution. Tae is potential still within a protective barrier, while Jangsaeng is an entity growing with the support of the world.
