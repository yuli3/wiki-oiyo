**A mind that thinks through memory — emotion gives logic its grain.** Mercury is the planet of "how do I think and speak," and Cancer makes that answer come out cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thought, language, the way we communicate |
| Sign | Cancer ♋ — protection, memory, care |
| Element · Modality | Water · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Moon |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (frequent retrogrades) |

## Core tendency

Mercury governs **thought, language, the way we communicate**. When that energy passes through Cancer, it shows up cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

This is why the same Mercury can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Cancer's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a protective instinct that shelters others**
- **emotional sensitivity that reads the room**
- **devotion to one's own people**

## Watch out for

- old wounds are remembered and one withdraws
- the tides of mood run high

## How it shows up

- **Love**: mental rapport and the rhythm of conversation are central to attraction.
- **Work & study**: the Cancer way (cautiously at first, then holding close and deep) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mercury is in Cancer, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mercury sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
