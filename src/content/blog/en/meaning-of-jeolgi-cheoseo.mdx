Cheoseo (處暑) is the 14th of the 24 solar terms, falling around August 22-24 when the Sun reaches celestial longitude 150°.

## Meaning and Origin

Cheoseo means that "the heat (暑) settles and comes to a stop (處)." As the Korean proverb says, "After Cheoseo, even mosquitoes' mouths become crooked," meaning the intense heat begins to ease and mornings and evenings turn cooler.

## Customs and Foods

This was a time for poswae (曝曬), drying clothes and books that had become damp during the summer rainy season in the sun. It is also when beolcho, cutting the grass around ancestral graves, traditionally begins.

## Saju and the Solar Term

Cheoseo is a middle qi (中氣), marking the middle of the lunar month. In myeongli, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 seasonal nodes (節), while the middle qi is read as the point when that month's qi becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Time

A solar term for taking out and drying what became damp over summer — a time when both spaces and minds need fresh air.
