<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **head line** is the major line usually found across the middle of the
  palm. Palmistry traditionally connects it with thinking style, attention,
  judgment, planning, and how a person approaches decisions. This is a symbolic
  and cultural reading, not a clinical assessment, medical sign, intelligence
  test, or fixed prediction of life outcomes.
</HighlightBox>

## What It Is / Location

The head line is commonly traced through the center of the palm. It often begins
near the space between the thumb and index finger, sometimes close to the life
line, and then travels across the palm toward the outer edge.

Readers traditionally observe whether it is long or short, straight or sloping,
deep or faint, and whether it appears separate from or joined to the life line
near the beginning. These observations are part of palmistry's visual language.
They are not measurements of intelligence, mental health, neurological status,
or moral character.

## Traditional Interpretations

The head line is traditionally read as a symbol of thought. A straighter head
line is often described as practical, structured, or analytical in folklore. A
more sloping head line may be read as imaginative, intuitive, or flexible. A
long line may be associated with sustained focus, while a shorter line may be
described as direct or action-oriented.

Some traditions pay attention to the line's start. A head line joined to the
life line may be interpreted as cautious or family-influenced, while a separated
start may be read as independent or self-directed. Breaks, chains, or changes in
depth are usually treated as symbols of changing priorities or periods of mental
strain.

These meanings are traditional interpretations only. They should be used as
prompts for reflection, not as evidence about ability, diagnosis, or destiny.

## Common Misunderstanding

The head line does not diagnose ADHD, anxiety, dementia, depression, learning
difference, brain injury, or any other medical or psychological condition. It
also does not reveal IQ, guarantee good judgment, or prove that someone is
rational, creative, careless, or confused.

At its most responsible, a head line reading can invite questions: Do you make
decisions quickly or slowly? Do you trust plans, instincts, or both? Where do
you need clearer thinking before acting? Those questions can be useful without
claiming that the hand contains clinical facts.

Palmistry is for entertainment, self-reflection, and cultural learning. Palm
lines do **not** diagnose health, disease, or medical conditions, and palmistry
is not medical, financial, or legal advice. For the broader context, see
[Palmistry](/en/meaning-of-palmistry/).
