**Accumulation becomes expansion — what is built slowly swells the largest.** Jupiter is the planet of "where do I find growth and meaning," and Taurus makes that answer come out slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter ♃ — expansion, luck, the direction of belief |
| Sign | Taurus ♉ — stability, the senses, accumulation |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 12 years (≈1 year per sign) |

## Core tendency

Jupiter governs **expansion, luck, the direction of belief**. When that energy passes through Taurus, it shows up slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

This is why the same Jupiter can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Taurus's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the stamina to see things through**
- **a grounded feel for real resources**
- **unshakeable calm**

## Watch out for

- change itself can feel like a threat
- stubbornness can block flexibility

## Growth and task

Jupiter changes sign on roughly a 12-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Jupiter in Taurus, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Jupiter is in Taurus, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Jupiter sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
