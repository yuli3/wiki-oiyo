**The Sun fulfilled through relationships — the partner is the co-star of your life.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Sun enters the 7th House, the question "Who am I?" unfolds within the realm of **partnerships, marriage, and contracts.**

## How This Placement Works

The 7th House deals with partnerships, marriage, and contracts. When the Sun enters this house, the energy of **identity, vitality, and the core of the self** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of partnership, marriage, and contracts receive the Sun's gift (**vitality**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Libra, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more multidimensional way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Sun in the 7th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is required to ensure that the areas of partnership, marriage, and contracts do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How do houses and signs differ?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Sun in the 7th House, the stage is partnership, marriage, and contracts, while the tone of performance is determined by the sign in which the Sun is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
