Eul (乙) represents a vine that grows by winding and curving. Unlike a giant tree that only reaches upward, it goes around rocks and climbs over walls. Its essence is a resilient vitality that is soft yet capable of surviving in any environment.

## Personality and Temperament

The Eul Day Master is sociable, possesses a keen sense of reality, and has an excellent ability to adjust to situations. They are better at maneuvering and negotiation than direct confrontation, and they prioritize practical gains over outward appearances. While they may appear gentle on the outside, their inner resolve is tougher than anyone else's.

- **Strengths**: Adaptability, social finesse, and persistent survival skills. The ability to carve out a place for themselves anywhere.
- **Points of Caution**: A tendency toward dependency, often seeking something to lean on (such as Jia Wood (甲), organizations, or people). They may appear indecisive, and prioritizing practical gains too much can lead to a loss of trust.

## Suitable Fields

Fields that require flexibility and intuition, such as sales and marketing, diplomacy and mediation, the service industry, and the arts.

## Relationships with Other Characters

Beside Jia Wood (甲), they find stability but may delay their independence. They are vulnerable to Xin Metal (辛, scissors), but when they encounter Bing Fire (丙, the sun), they bloom.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is Eul Wood weaker than Jia Wood?**

A. It is simply a different type of strength. It is the giant tree that breaks in a typhoon, not the grass. In Saju (Four Pillars), Eul Wood is the representative character of 'Yuneungjegang (柔能制剛)'—the soft overcoming the hard—and it often demonstrates greater survival instincts during turbulent times.
