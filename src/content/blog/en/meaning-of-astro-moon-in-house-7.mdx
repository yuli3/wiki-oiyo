**The Moon that waxes when together — the weather of your relationships is your own weather.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When the Moon enters the 7th House, the question "What makes me feel secure?" unfolds in the realm of **partnerships, marriage, and contracts**.

## How This Placement Functions

The 7th House deals with partnerships, marriage, and contracts. When the Moon enters this house, the energy of **emotions, instincts, and the need for security** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of partnerships, marriage, and contracts receive the Moon's gift (**a sense of security**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Libra, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more multidimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Moon in the 7th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of partnership, marriage, and contracts do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (the style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Moon in the 7th House, the stage is partnerships, marriage, and contracts, and the "acting tone" is determined by the sign in which the Moon is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **birth time and location**. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
