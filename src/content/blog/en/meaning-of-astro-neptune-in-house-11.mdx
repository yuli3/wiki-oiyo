**Companions of the Idealists — Dream together, but keep your feet on the ground.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Neptune enters the 11th House, the question "Where do I dream, and where do I blur?" unfolds in the realm of **friends, networks, and future visions**.

## How This Placement Works

The 11th House deals with friends, networks, and future visions. When Neptune enters here, the energy of **dreams, ideals, and the dissolution of boundaries** resides on that stage.

The Succedent House is a place of stabilization and accumulation — this placement builds results slowly but steadily.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of friends, networks, and future visions receive the gift of Neptune (**inspiration**). By referencing the nature of its natural sign, Aquarius, you can read the atmosphere of this stage more three-dimensionally.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Neptune in the 11th House is **becoming overly immersed in that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of friends, networks, and future visions do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Neptune in the 11th House, the stage is friends, networks, and future visions, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Neptune is located.

**Q. What is needed to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **birth time and location**. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretations are significantly less reliable.
