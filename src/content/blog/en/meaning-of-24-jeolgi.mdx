The 24 solar terms (二十四節氣) are a seasonal calendar that divides the Sun's path, the ecliptic, into 24 sections of 15 degrees each.
Unlike the lunar calendar, which follows the waxing and waning of the Moon, the solar terms follow the **position of the Sun**,
so they have long served as a standard for farming and seasonal life. In saju myeongri, the month pillar (月柱) also changes by solar term, not by the lunar calendar.
