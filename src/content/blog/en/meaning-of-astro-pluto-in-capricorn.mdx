**The transformation of institutional power — the bones of nations and corporations are reset.** Pluto is the planet of "what does our generation end and remake," and Capricorn makes that answer come out step by step, systematically toward the summit.

## Core tendency

Pluto governs **transformation, power, rebirth**. When that energy passes through Capricorn, it shows up step by step, systematically toward the summit.

This is why the same Pluto can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Capricorn's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **patience that endures the long game**
- **the skill to build structures**
- **trustworthiness that carries responsibility**

## Watch out for

- emotion is postponed in favor of results
- harshest of all on oneself

## How to read it as a generational placement

Pluto stays in one sign for years or decades, so Pluto in Capricorn is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Pluto is in Capricorn, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Pluto sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
