**Self-care** is the practical form of stress relief. When you turn the vague goal of "relieving stress" into **concrete actions across three pillars**, it becomes actionable.

## The 3 Pillars of Self-Care

### Physical Care
Sleep, nutrition, exercise, and rest. Many issues that appear to be emotional are often rooted in sleep deprivation and blood sugar levels. If physical signs of stress (headaches, heartburn, neck/shoulder tension, changes in sleep patterns) appear, physical recovery is the top priority.

### Mental Care
Managing the quality of stimuli—information diet, hobbies that allow for immersion, and learning. As the [Yerkes-Dodson Law](/en/meaning-of-yerkes-dodson-law/) suggests, an optimal level of stimulation can actually be a source of vitality.

### Emotional & Daily Care
Labeling emotions ([Emotion Diary](https://oiyo.net/en/emotion-diary)), securing time without a "mask," and spending time with people you enjoy. Happiness studies show that **who you are with** significantly impacts your sense of well-being, and that time spent in intimate relationships (friends, partners, family) is most effective when it provides both "fun" and "meaning."

## How Do You Know You Are Relieved?

That is a good question. The criteria are based on changes in signals rather than subjective mood—the quality of sleep, physical tension, the recovery of emotional range, and whether "you feel like doing things again." Avoidance (binge eating, excessive drinking, infinite scrolling) rather than relief only worsens these signals.
