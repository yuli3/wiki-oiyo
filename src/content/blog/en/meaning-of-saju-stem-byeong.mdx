Byeong (丙) represents the sun. It is the brightest and hottest energy among the ten heavenly stems, illuminating all things equally without hiding anything. The surroundings of a person with a strong Byeong Fire energy are always bright and lively.

## Personality and Temperament

The Byeong Fire Day Master is passionate and honest; their emotions and thoughts are clearly visible on their face. They are straightforward, hold no grudges, and are not afraid to stand in front of others. Because they have a clear sense of right and wrong, they cannot tolerate injustice.

- **Strengths**: Positive energy, expressiveness, and fairness. They possess the power to brighten their surroundings and uplift the atmosphere.
- **Points of Caution**: The sun cannot shine on one spot for too long. They may get bored easily, cause hurt through excessive bluntness, and risk overlooking substance while chasing after splendor.

## Suitable Fields

Fields involving stages and the public, such as broadcasting/entertainment, education/lecturing, sales, and politics.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When meeting Im (壬) Water (the ocean), they shine like the sun over a lake (Water-Fire equilibrium), and when illuminating Sin (辛) Metal (jewelry), they enhance each other's brilliance. When present with Jeong (丁) Fire (a candle), the light of the smaller fire is obscured.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I am a Byeong Fire Day Master, but I am introverted. Is this possible?**

A. Yes, it is. The Day Master is the "archetype" of essential energy, but its manifestation depends on the entire configuration of the chart. If the Water (水) energy is strong, it is like the sun being covered by clouds, leading to restrained expression; however, the inner passion and the clear sense of right and wrong often remain intact.
