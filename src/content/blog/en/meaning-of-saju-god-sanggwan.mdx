Sang-gwan (傷官), literally meaning "to injure the Gwan (官·order)," is the energy that seeks to break existing frameworks to advocate for something better. It is the most flamboyant of the Sipsin, embodying both genius and rebellion in one.

## At a Glance

## Personality Traits

Those with a prominent Sang-gwan possess excellent oratorical skills and brilliant ideas; they cannot help but point out injustices when they see them. They are natural performers who captivate audiences and drive innovation by constantly asking "Why?"

## Career and Profession

It is the representative Sipsin for fields where speech and creativity are the primary weapons, such as broadcasting, lecturing, marketing, planning, arts, and criticism.

## Wealth

When it meets the Jae-seong (wealth element) (Sang-gwan-saeng-jae), it creates an optimal structure where ideas translate directly into money. However, a system is required to manage the resulting volatile income fluctuations.

## Excess and Deficiency

When in excess, the risk of gossip and verbal mishaps increases, often leading to friction with superiors and organizations. The lifelong challenge for Sang-gwan is realizing that even "the right words" can cause injury (傷) if the timing and delivery are wrong.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Are those with a Sang-gwan structure unsuited for organizational life?**

A. While they may experience frequent friction in vertical or bureaucratic organizations, they often become aces in organizations that value creativity (planning, creative departments, R&D). It is not the organization itself, but the degree of freedom in expression that determines whether it is a good fit.

*Sipsin is a language for reading the relationship between other characters based on the Il-gan (the self). The expression of the same Sipsin varies depending on its position, strength, and interactions (Hap/Chung); there is no such thing as a "good" or "bad" Sipsin.*
