A king looks straight ahead, holding his sword upright. Among tarot's court figures, this is the rare card that faces almost directly forward. The King of Swords is the final judge who rules through intellect and principle.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | King — The one who governs |
| Key words | Fair authority, strategic intelligence, principle, final judgment |

## Upright Meaning

This is a time to make decisions through logic and principle. Fairness that is not swayed by emotion creates authority, and you may be asked to bring a complex problem to its final order.

- **Love**: A rational and trustworthy person. Their expression may be dry, but their promises are exact.
- **Work & Money**: Well suited to strategy, law, and the role of judge or evaluator. Financially, principle-based systematic investment is appropriate now.

## Reversed Meaning

Abuse of authority, cold dogmatism, or decisions with only a head and no heart. Law exists for people.

## Today's Advice

> Before judging, ask one last question: not only "is it fair?" but also "is it warm?"
