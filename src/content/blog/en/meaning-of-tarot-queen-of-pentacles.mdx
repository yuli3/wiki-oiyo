A queen sits on a throne in a garden of flowers and rabbits, holding a pentacle in her lap and looking down at it. The Queen of Pentacles is someone who translates abundance into care — a warm yet meticulous master of practical stewardship.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — earth element, material matters, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | Queen — the one who holds |
| Key words | Practical care, abundant householding, grounded wisdom, place of rest |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when your ability to care for work and home, money and heart, shines across the board. The practical care that enriches those around you eventually returns to you.

- **Love**: Steady love that looks after food, clothing, and shelter together; a relationship where stability and warmth coexist.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable signs in operations, management, and welfare-related areas. Financially, your sense of balance is alive: frugal, but willing to spend where it matters.

## Reversed Meaning

Your own storehouse may be empty because you have cared for everyone else, or affection may be expressed only through material things. Put yourself first on the list of people to care for.

## Today's Advice

> If you cared for someone today, prepare one meal for yourself with the same sincerity.
