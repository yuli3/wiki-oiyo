A rider wearing a laurel wreath marches through the cheers of a crowd. Six of Wands is the card of public victory and recognition granted to the one who passed through the competition of Five of Wands.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 6 — Victory and recognition |
| Key words | Victory, public recognition, triumphal march, confidence |

## Upright Meaning

Your effort is publicly recognized: passing an exam, winning an award, earning a promotion, or receiving attention. Confidence rises, and support from others follows.

- **Love**: A relationship blessed by those around you, or a time when your charm receives attention.
- **Work & Money**: Good news around presenting results, promotion, or winning a contract. Financially, bonuses or rewards may follow.

## Reversed Meaning

Expected recognition may be delayed, or you need to watch for arrogance and envy after a victory. Falling from the horse hurts most during the parade.

## Today's Advice

> Enjoy the applause, but keep holding the reins to the end.
