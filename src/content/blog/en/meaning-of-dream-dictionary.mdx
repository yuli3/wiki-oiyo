## How to Read Dream Interpretations

1. **Emotion comes first.** The meaning of the same symbol can reverse depending on what you felt in the dream: fear, calm, or joy.
2. **Look back at your recent days.** Jung saw dreams as "compensation for what waking consciousness has ignored." What have you been suppressing lately?
3. **Recurring dreams are messages.** If the same dream repeats, your unconscious may be asking the same question again and again.
