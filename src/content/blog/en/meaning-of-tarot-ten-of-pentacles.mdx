Inside a city gate are an elder, a couple, a child, and dogs, while ten pentacles are arranged in the shape of the Tree of Life. This is wealth and stability that continue beyond one generation: the destination of the material journey.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material reality, money, body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 10 — Completion |
| Key words | Inheritance, generational abundance, family lineage, lasting wealth |

## Upright Meaning

What has been built over a long time becomes a solid structure. Good news arrives around things that will last longer than you: family, assets, tradition, inheritance, real estate, family business, and long-term contracts.

- **Love**: A union blessed by family, or a relationship with a stable future in view. This is a classic positive card for marriage questions.
- **Work & Money**: The completion of a strong organization or system. Financially, this is a good time for asset planning that crosses generations, such as pensions, gifts, and real estate.

## Reversed Meaning

Family conflict around inheritance or money, or emptiness behind stability. Check whether the structure is complete but the heart inside it is empty.

## Today's Advice

> Will the system you are building now still work ten years from now? Make today's decision by that standard.
