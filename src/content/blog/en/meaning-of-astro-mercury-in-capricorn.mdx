**A person who speaks like a report — spare, practical language with no excess.** Mercury is the planet of "how do I think and speak," and Capricorn makes that answer come out step by step, systematically toward the summit.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thought, language, the way we communicate |
| Sign | Capricorn ♑ — goals, structure, achievement |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Saturn |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (frequent retrogrades) |

## Core tendency

Mercury governs **thought, language, the way we communicate**. When that energy passes through Capricorn, it shows up step by step, systematically toward the summit.

This is why the same Mercury can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Capricorn's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **patience that endures the long game**
- **the skill to build structures**
- **trustworthiness that carries responsibility**

## Watch out for

- emotion is postponed in favor of results
- harshest of all on oneself

## How it shows up

- **Love**: mental rapport and the rhythm of conversation are central to attraction.
- **Work & study**: the Capricorn way (step by step, systematically toward the summit) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mercury is in Capricorn, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mercury sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
