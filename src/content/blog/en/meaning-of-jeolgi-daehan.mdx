Daehan (大寒) is the 24th of the 24 solar terms, arriving around January 19-21 when the Sun reaches celestial longitude 300°.

## Meaning and origin

Daehan means "great (大) cold (寒)" and is the final solar term among the 24 solar terms. Although its name suggests the deepest cold, in practice it is often milder than Sohan. About fifteen days later comes Ipchun, the Onset of Spring, so ending and beginning meet here.

## Customs and foods

This is a time to close the old seasonal cycle and prepare for Seollal. On Jeju Island, the period from five days after Daehan to three days before Ipchun was called Singugan (新舊間), when people concentrated moves and home repairs into that interval.

## Saju and solar terms

Daehan is a mid-qi (中氣), marking the middle of the month. In myeongnihak, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 sectional terms (節), while the mid-qi is read as the point when that month's energy becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for this time

"At the end of Daehan, there is bright spring (陽春)" — every ending is another name for a beginning. Read the year's final cold as a promise of spring.
