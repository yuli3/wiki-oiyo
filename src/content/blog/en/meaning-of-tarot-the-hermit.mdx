On the summit of a snow-covered mountain, an old man in a gray cloak stands holding a lantern. Inside the lantern shines a six-pointed star, the Seal of Solomon. He is not lost. He has chosen to leave in order to find a deeper answer.

## Reading the Symbols

The lantern represents self-kindled wisdom, the staff is the support of experience, and the mountain summit symbolizes distance from the secular world. His lantern does not shine far, because lighting only the next step is enough.

## Upright Meaning

It is time to pause and have time to yourself. When you step back from daily busyness, answers you could not see before begin to surface. This is an ideal period for study, research, and self-inquiry.

- **Love**: A time when you need space for yourself. If you are single, getting clear within yourself comes before rushing into a meeting.
- **Work & Study**: Results come through solo work and deeper research. You may meet a mentor, or become a mentor yourself.
- **Money**: A time to reduce spending and strengthen your foundation. Study comes before flashy investments.

## Reversed Meaning

The Hermit reversed shows reflection turning into isolation. It can mean that time alone has become escape, or that you are hiding in busyness without reflection. Distinguish between closing the door and closing your heart.

## Card Combinations

- **The Hermit + The Star**: Hope after solitary searching. A sign that the dark tunnel is nearing its end.
- **The Hermit + The Lovers**: A lesson in balancing time alone with relationship.

## FAQ

**Q. If The Hermit appears, should I take a break from dating?**

A. Not necessarily. The core message is to sort out your relationship with yourself. It means pausing a romance you enter without knowing what you want, not forbidding love itself.
