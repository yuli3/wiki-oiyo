**A person whose presence walks through the door before they do — you were born to be the protagonist of your life.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Sun enters the 1st House, the question "Who am I shining as?" unfolds in the realms of **first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things.**

## How This Placement Works

The 1st House deals with first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things. When the Sun enters this house, the energy of **identity, vitality, and the core of the self** resides on that stage.

The Angular Houses are the most prominent positions in the chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things receive the Sun's gift (**vitality**). Referring to the nature of Aries, the natural ruler, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Sun in the 1st House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed so that the realms of first impressions, the body, and the way one initiates things do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Sun in the 1st House, the stage is your first impression, body, and way of initiating things, while the tone of your "performance" is determined by the sign the Sun is in.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **birth time and place**. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
