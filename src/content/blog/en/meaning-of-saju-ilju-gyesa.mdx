The Gye-Sa (癸巳) Ilju is the 30th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Gye (Yin Water), symbolizing soaking rain, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Sa (Snake), symbolizing insight. It is the image of heat haze rising in the spring sun—a symbol of softly permeating intelligence.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gye-Sa Ilju is a type of "talented scholar" who is quick-witted and sociable. By sitting on the Jeong-jae (Proper Wealth), they are adept at financial management and become indispensable wherever they go.

- **Strengths**: Sharp intuition, sociability, and a frugal sense for wealth management.
- **Points of Caution**: They tend to change their minds frequently, which can lead to a lack of persistence. It is recommended to finish one task before moving on to the next.

## Structural Reading — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gye (癸) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gye/) sits atop the [Sa (巳) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the 10-God is [Jeong-jae (正財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeongjae/), and the 12-Unseong is [Tae (胎)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-tae/). Jeong-jae is the star of fixed wealth, providing a foundation oriented toward stability through diligent saving and management. The position of Tae (Embryo) creates a structure of conception, always harboring new possibilities.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Jang-ryu-su (長流水, Long-flowing water), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
