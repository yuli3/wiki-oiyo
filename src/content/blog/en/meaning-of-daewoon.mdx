## Definition

Daewoon is a major cycle in Saju, usually interpreted in **10-year periods**.

If the natal Saju chart is the basic structure, Daewoon is the time layer that shows what kind of season or environment that structure encounters.

## Core Structure

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 10-year cycle | Daewoon is usually read as a decade-scale flow. |
| Starting age | Calculated from birth data and seasonal markers. |
| Heavenly stem and earthly branch | Two layers used to interpret the cycle. |
| Relationship to natal chart | Meaning changes according to the original Saju structure. |

## Difference From Annual Luck

If Daewoon is the larger season, annual luck is closer to the weather of a specific year.

The same year can feel different depending on which Daewoon cycle a person is in.

## Careful Use

Daewoon is not a fixed verdict on life. It is better treated as a symbolic tool for identifying repeated themes, available resources, and practical tasks in a period.

Important decisions should also include real conditions such as health, relationships, finances, and timing.
