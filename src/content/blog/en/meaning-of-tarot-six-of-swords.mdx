A boatman rows a hunched mother and child toward calmer waters on the far shore. Six swords stand in the boat. Even if you carry your wounds with you, leaving is part of recovery.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 6 — Movement toward recovery |
| Key words | Crossing over, a healing passage, toward calmer waters, companionship |

## Upright Meaning

You are moving from a difficult phase toward a more peaceful place. This is not a dramatic reversal, but it is a definite improvement. The rough waters are receding behind you.

- **Love**: A calm recovery period after conflict, or a relationship that starts again after changing its environment.
- **Work & Money**: A transition for recovery, such as a department move, job change, or relocation. Financially, the bleeding stops and you enter a more stable zone.

## Reversed Meaning

Lingering attachment that keeps you from leaving even though you need to, or drifting without knowing where you are going. The oar has meaning only when the destination is set.

## Today's Advice

> You do not leave after you are fully healed. You heal as you leave.
