Sohan (小寒) is the 23rd of the 24 solar terms. It begins around January 5-7, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 285°.

## Meaning and Origin

Despite its name, "small (小) cold (寒)," Sohan is often the coldest solar term of the year in practice. The Korean proverb "Daehan goes to Sohan's house and freezes to death" captures that paradox.

## Customs and Foods

People prepared for the severe cold by bringing in firewood and food in advance, while farming households planned the year's agricultural work and waited for spring.

## Saju and the Solar Terms

The calendar used in myeongnihak moves by solar terms. Sohan is one of the 12 sectional terms (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in saju changes to the [Chuk (丑) month](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-chuk/).** This is why the solar term, not the lunar calendar date, is used when determining the sexagenary stem-branch of the month of birth.

## Wisdom for This Time

Preparing for spring at the coldest point of the year: like a farmer at Sohan, design the next season while standing in the middle of hardship.
