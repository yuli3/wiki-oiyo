The Gap-Shin (甲申) Ilju is the 21st pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Gap (Yang Wood), a giant tree reaching straight toward the sky, sitting upon the Earthly Branch Shin, the talented monkey. It is the image of a pine tree rooted in a rocky cliff—a figure that becomes stronger in difficult terrain.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gap-Shin Ilju is a challenger type forged in environments full of change. As it occupies the position of *Jeolcheobongsaeng* (絶處逢生—finding life in the midst of death/severance), it finds a way out of every crisis and is quick to act.

- **Strengths**: Ability to break through crises, quick wit, and survival skills in harsh environments.
- **Points of Caution**: Life tends to be subject to frequent changes. Finding a single place to set down roots is the key to stability.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Gap (甲) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gap/) sits atop the [Shin (申) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sin-monkey/). Based on the main qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the Ten God is [Pyeongwan (偏官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeongwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Jeol (絶)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jeol/). Pyeongwan is the star of challenge and responsibility—it creates a strong vessel that is tempered by enduring pressure. Because it is in the position of *Jeol*, it is a dynamic structure characterized by repeated changes and comebacks.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to *Cheonjungsu* (泉中水), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
