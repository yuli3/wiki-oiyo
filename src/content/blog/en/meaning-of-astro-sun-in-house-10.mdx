**The Sun shining at the peak — Career is the backbone of life.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Sun enters the 10th house, the question "Who am I shining as?" unfolds in the realm of **career, reputation, and social calling**.

## How This Placement Works

The 10th house deals with career, reputation, and social calling. When the Sun enters this house, the energy of **identity, vitality, and the core of the self** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Flourishes

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of career, reputation, and social calling receive the Sun's gift (**vitality**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Capricorn, one can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Sun in the 10th house is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of career, reputation, and social calling do not become the entirety of life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (stage). If you have the Sun in the 10th house, the stage is your career, reputation, and social calling, while the tone of expression is determined by the sign the Sun is located in.

**Q. What do I need to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
