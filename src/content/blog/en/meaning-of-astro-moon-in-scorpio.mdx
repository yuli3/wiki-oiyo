**An all-or-nothing emotional life — once trust is built, it holds deeper than anyone.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Scorpio makes that answer come out deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Scorpio ♏ — intensity, insight, regeneration |
| Element · Modality | Water · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Pluto |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Scorpio, it shows up deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Scorpio's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **insight that pierces to the essence**
- **all-in immersion**
- **the power to rebuild after collapse**

## Watch out for

- suspicion and control tighten relationships
- feelings hidden too long erupt

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Scorpio way (deeply and intensely, all the way to the end) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Scorpio, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
