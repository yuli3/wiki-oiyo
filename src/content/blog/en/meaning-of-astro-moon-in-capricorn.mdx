**A Moon that finds safety in structure — feelings steady once duties are in order.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Capricorn makes that answer come out step by step, systematically toward the summit.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Capricorn ♑ — goals, structure, achievement |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Saturn |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Capricorn, it shows up step by step, systematically toward the summit.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Capricorn's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **patience that endures the long game**
- **the skill to build structures**
- **trustworthiness that carries responsibility**

## Watch out for

- emotion is postponed in favor of results
- harshest of all on oneself

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Capricorn way (step by step, systematically toward the summit) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Capricorn, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
