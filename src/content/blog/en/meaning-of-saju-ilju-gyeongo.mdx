The Gyeong-O (庚午) pillar is the 7th of the Sexagenary Cycle (六十甲子). It represents the Heavenly Stem Gyeong (庚), which is Yang Metal (hard iron or raw ore), sitting atop the Earthly Branch O (午), the horse of passion. It is the image of iron being heated in a furnace—a symbol of loyalty forged through intense heat.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gyeong-O pillar represents a "warrior" archetype, characterized by a strong sense of justice, loyalty, and a high regard for honor. They are straightforward and passionate, which attracts many followers, and they cannot tolerate injustice.

- **Strengths**: A strong sense of justice and loyalty, and a charismatic, open-hearted personality that draws people in.
- **Points of Caution**: They can be hot-tempered; it is important to be mindful of impulsive outbursts.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Gyeong (庚) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-gyeong/) sits on the [O (午) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-o/). Based on the primary qi of the daily branch, the 10 God is the [Direct Officer (正官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeonggwan/), and the 12 Stages of Qi is the [Bath (沐浴)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-mokyok/). The Direct Officer is the star of honor and order, providing a foundation of dignity and a desire for recognition within established norms. The "Bath" stage indicates that while their senses and charm shine brightly, the structure is also prone to fluctuations.

In terms of Nabeum (phonetic element), it belongs to Nobang-to (Roadside Earth), a classification frequently used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same phonetic element.
