**Mars in its own home — ignition is quick and the breakthrough is strong.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Aries makes that answer come out quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Aries ♈ — initiative, courage, directness |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Mars |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Aries, it shows up quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aries's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the drive to simply begin**
- **courage that shines in a crisis**
- **unvarnished honesty**

## Watch out for

- haste can blur the finish
- competitiveness invites needless conflict

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Aries way (quickly and bluntly, without hesitation) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Aries, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
