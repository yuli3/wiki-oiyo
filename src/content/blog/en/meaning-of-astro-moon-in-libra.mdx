**A Moon that quiets in togetherness — discord is felt in the body first.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Libra makes that answer come out gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Libra ♎ — balance, relationship, aesthetics |
| Element · Modality | Air · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Libra, it shows up gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Libra's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a balancing sense that mediates conflict**
- **a refined eye for beauty**
- **natural negotiation skills**

## Watch out for

- long hesitation before a decision
- avoiding conflict hides true feelings

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Libra way (gracefully and fairly, in step with the other) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Libra, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
