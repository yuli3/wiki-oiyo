**A Moon quick to ignite — anger flares fast and recovers fast.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Aries makes that answer come out quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Aries ♈ — initiative, courage, directness |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Mars |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Aries, it shows up quickly and bluntly, without hesitation.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aries's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the drive to simply begin**
- **courage that shines in a crisis**
- **unvarnished honesty**

## Watch out for

- haste can blur the finish
- competitiveness invites needless conflict

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Aries way (quickly and bluntly, without hesitation) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Aries, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
