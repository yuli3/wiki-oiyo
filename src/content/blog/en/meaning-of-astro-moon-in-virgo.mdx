**A Moon that finds calm at a tidy desk — anxiety eases by checking the details.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Virgo makes that answer come out carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Virgo ♍ — analysis, improvement, usefulness |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Virgo, it shows up carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Virgo's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **analysis that misses no detail**
- **quiet, reliable competence**
- **ceaseless self-improvement**

## Watch out for

- perfectionism delays the start
- the critic's edge turns on the self

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Virgo way (carefully and precisely, seeking a better way) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Virgo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
