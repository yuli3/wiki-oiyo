**Slow but exact — once this mind understands, it does not forget.** Mercury is the planet of "how do I think and speak," and Taurus makes that answer come out slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thought, language, the way we communicate |
| Sign | Taurus ♉ — stability, the senses, accumulation |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (frequent retrogrades) |

## Core tendency

Mercury governs **thought, language, the way we communicate**. When that energy passes through Taurus, it shows up slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

This is why the same Mercury can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Taurus's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the stamina to see things through**
- **a grounded feel for real resources**
- **unshakeable calm**

## Watch out for

- change itself can feel like a threat
- stubbornness can block flexibility

## How it shows up

- **Love**: mental rapport and the rhythm of conversation are central to attraction.
- **Work & study**: the Taurus way (slowly but surely, savoring every sense) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mercury is in Taurus, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mercury sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
