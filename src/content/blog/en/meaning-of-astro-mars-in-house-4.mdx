**Energy poured into the household — family matters can become the ignition point for drive.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mars enters the 4th house, the question "What do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the realm of **home, parents, and the inner foundation**.

## How This Placement Works

The 4th house deals with home, parents, and the inner foundation. When Mars comes here, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the places in a chart where expression is most visible — this placement is easy for other people to notice as well.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of home, parents, and the inner foundation receives Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Cancer, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more layered way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 4th house is **becoming overly concentrated on this stage** — balance is needed so that home, parents, and the inner foundation do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (stage). With Mars in the 4th house, the stage is home, parents, and the inner foundation, and the acting tone is set by the sign where Mars is placed.

**Q. What do I need to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
