The **Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA)** issues statutory Special Meteorological Reports (*Gisang Teukbo*) to protect public life and infrastructure against acute natural disasters. Warnings are structured into two distinct escalation tiers:

1. **Advisory (주의보, Juyibo)**: Issued when a hazardous meteorological event is expected to cause localized disruption and caution is required.
2. **Warning (경보, Gyeongbo)**: Issued when severe meteorological phenomena are forecast to inflict widespread infrastructural damage, casualties, or severe transportation shutdowns.

---

## 1. Winter Hazard Thresholds: Cold Waves and Heavy Snow

### Cold Wave Criteria (한파, Hanpa)
Cold wave declarations consider both absolute morning lows and the magnitude of day-over-day temperature drops:

| Level | 24-Hour Temperature Drop Criteria | Absolute Minimum Temperature Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold Wave Advisory** | Morning low drops by $\ge 10^\circ C$ compared to previous day, falling to $\le 3^\circ C$, with expected damage | Morning low $\le -12^\circ C$ persistent for $\ge 2$ consecutive days |
| **Cold Wave Warning** | Morning low drops by $\ge 15^\circ C$ compared to previous day, falling to $\le 3^\circ C$, with severe disruption | Morning low $\le -15^\circ C$ persistent for $\ge 2$ consecutive days |

### Heavy Snow Criteria (대설, Daeseol)
Snow warnings evaluate projected 24-hour accumulated snowfall depth:
- **Heavy Snow Advisory**: Expected 24-hour fresh snow depth $\ge 5.0\,cm$ (or $\ge 20.0\,cm$ in mountainous mountainous terrain).
- **Heavy Snow Warning**: Expected 24-hour fresh snow depth $\ge 20.0\,cm$ (or $\ge 30.0\,cm$ in mountainous terrain).

## 2. Summer Hazard Thresholds: Heatwaves and Torrential Rain

### Heatwave Criteria (폭염, Pok-yeom)
Since 2020, KMA has transitioned heatwave standards from simple dry-bulb temperatures to **Perceived Daily Maximum Temperature (체감온도)**, integrating ambient humidity into thermal stress assessments:

| Level | Perceived Temperature Threshold | Duration and Vulnerability Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Heatwave Advisory** | Daily maximum perceived temperature $\ge 33^\circ C$ | Expected to persist for $\ge 2$ consecutive days |
| **Heatwave Warning** | Daily maximum perceived temperature $\ge 35^\circ C$ | Expected to persist for $\ge 2$ consecutive days, or widespread heat illness casualties anticipated |

### Heavy Rain Criteria (호우, Hou)
Precipitation warnings evaluate both acute short-duration rain rates and aggregate accumulation:
- **Heavy Rain Advisory**: 3-hour accumulated rainfall $\ge 60\,mm$, OR 12-hour accumulated rainfall $\ge 110\,mm$.
- **Heavy Rain Warning**: 3-hour accumulated rainfall $\ge 90\,mm$, OR 12-hour accumulated rainfall $\ge 180\,mm$.
