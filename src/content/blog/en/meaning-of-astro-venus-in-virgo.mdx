**Love spoken through small kindnesses — quiet, steady, and difficult to fake.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Virgo makes that answer come out carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Virgo ♍ — analysis, improvement, usefulness |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Virgo, it shows up carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Virgo's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **analysis that misses no detail**
- **quiet, reliable competence**
- **ceaseless self-improvement**

## Watch out for

- perfectionism delays the start
- the critic's edge turns on the self

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Virgo way (carefully and precisely, seeking a better way) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Virgo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
