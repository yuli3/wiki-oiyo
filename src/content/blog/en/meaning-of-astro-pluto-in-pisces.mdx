**The transformation of the collective unconscious — a vast turning point where boundaries dissolve.** Pluto is the planet of "what does our generation end and remake," and Pisces makes that answer come out boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Pluto ♇ — transformation, power, rebirth |
| Sign | Pisces ♓ — empathy, imagination, transcendence |
| Element · Modality | Water · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Neptune |
| Orbital cycle | about 248 years (12–30 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Pluto governs **transformation, power, rebirth**. When that energy passes through Pisces, it shows up boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

This is why the same Pluto can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Pisces's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **empathy that feels others' emotions as one's own**
- **imagination that pictures beyond the real**
- **unconditional acceptance**

## Watch out for

- blurred boundaries lead to burnout
- escapism becomes a sweet trap

## How to read it as a generational placement

Pluto stays in one sign for years or decades, so Pluto in Pisces is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Pluto is in Pisces, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Pluto sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
