The Muja (戊子) Ilju is the 25th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Mu—the steady, immovable Yang Earth of a great mountain—sitting atop the Earthly Branch Ja, the wise rat of the deep night. It is the image of a spring bubbling up from beneath a great mountain—a symbol of inexhaustible practicality.

## Personality and Temperament

The Muja Ilju is a pragmatic type with a sharp sense for wealth and a thrifty nature. Behind a dependable and sturdy exterior lies a meticulous calculator, leaving no gaps in household management or business affairs.

- **Strengths**: Sharp financial sense, thrifty management skills, and a sense of stability.
- **Points of Caution**: Being overly focused on practical gains can make one appear stingy. A generous hand will only make the spring flow deeper.

## Structural Analysis — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Mu (戊) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-mu/) sits atop the [Ja (子) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-ja/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10 God is [Jeongjae (Direct Wealth)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeongjae/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Tae (Embryo)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-tae/). Jeongjae is the star of fixed wealth, rooted in a stability-oriented approach of diligently accumulating and managing assets. As it sits in the 'Tae' (Embryo) stage, it is a structure of conception that always harbors new possibilities.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Byeokryeok-hwa (Thunderbolt Fire), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri alongside paired pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
