A merchant holds scales in one hand and gives coins with the other. Six of Pentacles is the circulation of wealth: humble generosity that knows the roles of giver and receiver can change at any time.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material reality, money, body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 6 — Restored circulation |
| Key words | Giving and receiving, generosity, fair distribution, financial balance |

## Upright Meaning

The balance of giving and receiving is restored. You may have room to give, or you may receive the help you need. Both sides are part of the same circulation. Receiving is a practice too.

- **Love**: Balanced give-and-take. If only one person is giving, it is time to inspect the scales.
- **Work & Money**: Movement of resources through mentoring, sponsorship, bonuses, and support. Financially, donations and gifts may circulate, and loans or investment funding also have a favorable flow.

## Reversed Meaning

Conditional kindness, debts you cannot repay, or control disguised as generosity. Generosity without scales becomes a liability.

## Today's Advice

> Give without conditions, receive without shame, but keep accurate records on both sides.
