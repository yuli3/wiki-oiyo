**A generation of relationship revolution — partnership becomes an experiment.** Uranus is the planet of "what does our generation break open," and Libra makes that answer come out gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Uranus ♅ — innovation, rupture, the impulse toward freedom |
| Sign | Libra ♎ — balance, relationship, aesthetics |
| Element · Modality | Air · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 84 years (≈7 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Uranus governs **innovation, rupture, the impulse toward freedom**. When that energy passes through Libra, it shows up gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

This is why the same Uranus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Libra's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a balancing sense that mediates conflict**
- **a refined eye for beauty**
- **natural negotiation skills**

## Watch out for

- long hesitation before a decision
- avoiding conflict hides true feelings

## How to read it as a generational placement

Uranus stays in one sign for years or decades, so Uranus in Libra is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Uranus is in Libra, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Uranus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
