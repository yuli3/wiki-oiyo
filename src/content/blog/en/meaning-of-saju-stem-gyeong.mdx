Gyeong (庚) is cast iron before refinement and raw ore in a rocky mountain. It represents the cool, cutting energy of autumn known as suksaljigi (肅殺之氣) — the force that clears away what has not matured and leaves only the fruit.

## Personality and Temperament

A Gyeong metal day master is straightforward, quick to decide, and values loyalty. This stem has the temperament of a reformer who overturns what is wrong, handling matters by principle without taking sides. It is often described as a character with the spirit of a soldier or swordsman.

- **Strengths**: Drive, decisiveness, and loyalty. The ability to cut through a crisis like a blade.
- **Points to watch**: Unrefined metal is rough. Excessive bluntness can create enemies, flexibility may be lacking, and once a relationship turns sour, it may be cut off in a single stroke.

## Suitable Fields

Fields that require decisive action and firm resolve, such as the military, police, prosecution, sports, surgery, engineering, and restructuring.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When Gyeong meets Jeong fire (the furnace), it is reborn as a fine sword — the greatest growth narrative for Gyeong metal. With Eul wood, the Eul-Gyeong combination creates harmony between softness and strength.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I am Gyeong metal, but I am indecisive. Why is that?**

A. Gyeong metal's decisiveness is a story that comes "after refinement." If fire (火) energy is weak and the person remains in a raw-ore state, a strong nature may hesitate because it has not yet found direction. As discipline, experience, hardship, and training accumulate, the original decisiveness awakens over time.
