A person on a hill defends their position against six wands rising from below. Being challenged because you have reached higher ground is both the price and the proof of success.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 7 — Defense and conviction |
| Key words | Defending your position, challengers, holding your beliefs, maintaining advantage |

## Upright Meaning

Your position, opinion, or achievement is being challenged, but the high ground is still yours. Do not retreat. Defend your stance consistently.

- **Love**: Struggling to protect a relationship, or the arrival of a rival. A steady attitude is the answer.
- **Work & Money**: Pushback from those targeting your results, or intensifying market competition. Financially, defensive management becomes more important than aggressive gains.

## Reversed Meaning

You may be exhausted by pressure from every direction and considering giving up, or you may be clinging to something that is no longer worth defending. Reset the priorities of your defensive line.

## Today's Advice

> You do not have to block every arrow. Protect only the one central stronghold.
