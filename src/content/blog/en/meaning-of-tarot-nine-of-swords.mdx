A person wakes in the middle of the night on a bed, face covered in their hands, while nine swords hang on the wall. The swords are on the wall, not driven into the body — much of the pain is playing in the theater of the mind.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 9 — the dark night |
| Key words | Anxiety, sleeplessness, self-blame, the darkness before dawn |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when worry and anxiety eat into the night. Yet the truth of this card is that things feel larger than they actually are. Put the anxiety on paper, and its size begins to shrink.

- **Love**: Excessive worry about a relationship and regrets that arrive at dawn. If you check with the other person, half of it will turn out to be needless fear.
- **Work & Money**: Self-blame over mistakes and anxiety about results. If financial worry feels large, face the exact numbers instead of vague fear.

## Reversed Meaning

The end of a nightmare, identifying the true shape of anxiety, and the beginning of asking for help. Dawn is getting closer.

## Today's Advice

> Do not trust thoughts from 3 a.m. It will not be too late to judge again in the morning.
