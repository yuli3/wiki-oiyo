**Mars against the system — it comes alive when fighting unjust rules.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Aquarius makes that answer come out differently, yet for everyone.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Aquarius ♒ — originality, solidarity, the future |
| Element · Modality | Air · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Uranus |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Aquarius, it shows up differently, yet for everyone.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aquarius's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **ideas ahead of their time**
- **a horizontal sense of solidarity**
- **objectivity unswept by emotion**

## Watch out for

- difference for its own sake is a trap
- close relationships can be awkward

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Aquarius way (differently, yet for everyone) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Aquarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
