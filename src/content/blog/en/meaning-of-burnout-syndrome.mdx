**Burnout** is an occupational phenomenon defined by the World Health Organization (WHO) in ICD-11 as a syndrome resulting from "**chronic workplace stress** that has not been successfully managed" (classified as an occupational phenomenon, not as a disease).

## Three Dimensions (WHO / Maslach)

1. **Energy depletion and exhaustion** — fatigue that does not recover even after rest
2. **Mental distance** — cynicism, indifference, and distancing from work
3. **Reduced professional efficacy** — a sense of helplessness that "nothing changes no matter what I do"

Christina Maslach's Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI) is the original model behind this three-dimensional structure.

## Burnout = Accumulated Distress

In terms of the distinction between [eustress and distress](/en/meaning-of-eustress-distress/), burnout is a **state in which distress has accumulated without recovery**. There is also an opposite extreme: the helplessness that can arise when someone repeats only overly easy tasks with no stimulation at all (boreout). As the [Yerkes-Dodson law](/ko/meaning-of-yerkes-dodson-law/) shows, a healthy state is not zero stress, but an appropriate level of stimulation.

## Direction of Recovery

- **Reduce load** — distinguish what to remove, postpone, and delegate
- **Secure recovery resources** — the basics of sleep, meals, and exercise
- **Rebuild efficacy** — start with small tasks that can be completed
- If the feeling of exhaustion persists for a long time, professional counseling is recommended
