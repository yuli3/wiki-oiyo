A figure in a black cloak grieves while looking at three spilled cups. Yet behind them, two cups still stand, and across the river there is a bridge leading home.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 5 — loss and grief |
| Key words | Loss, regret, mourning, the two cups that remain |

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when sorrow over what was lost fills the heart. Grief is a necessary process, so do not rush it. Still, the truth that not everything has been lost — the two remaining cups — is also real.

- **Love**: The pain of separation, regret, a gaze fixed on a past relationship. Only after grieving enough can you turn around.
- **Work & Money**: Self-blame over a failed project or loss. A financial loss is already in the past — write a new list of the resources that remain.

## Reversed Meaning

The end of mourning, the beginning of recovery and forgiveness. You are taking your eyes off the spilled cups and crossing the bridge.

## Today's Advice

> Today, write down the names of the two cups that remain, not the three that were lost.
