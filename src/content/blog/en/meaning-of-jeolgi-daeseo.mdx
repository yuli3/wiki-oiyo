Daeseo (大暑) is the 12th of the 24 solar terms, entering around July 22–24 when the sun reaches 120° celestial longitude.

## Meaning and Origins

Daeseo means "great (大) heat (暑)," and it is the solar term that marks the hottest part of the year. It overlaps with the period around Jungbok, and the heat is intense enough to inspire the saying that even a goat's horn would melt.

## Customs and Foods

This is the height of bokdalim customs for enduring the sambok heat. People restored their strength with foods such as samgyetang and red bean porridge, and it is also when Korean melons and watermelons are at their best.

## Saju and Solar Terms

Daeseo is a mid-qi (中氣), marking the middle of the lunar month. In myeongli, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 nodes (節), while the mid-qi is read as the time when that month's energy becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Season

At the hottest time of year, simply enduring can be work in itself. Put condition management before performance.
