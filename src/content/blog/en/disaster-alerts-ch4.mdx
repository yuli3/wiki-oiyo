An earthquake disaster text alert sometimes arrives a few seconds before the shaking, and sometimes almost at the same moment. What to do in that short time is already set out. This chapter organizes South Korea's Ministry of the Interior and Safety earthquake public action guidelines (지진 국민행동요령) by place.

---

## 1. While the ground shakes: protect your body first

- **Indoors**: Get under a table to protect your body and hold on tightly to the table legs.
- **At school**: Get under your desk and hold on tightly to the desk legs.
- Do not run outside while it is shaking. Falling glass or signboards can injure you.

## 2. When the shaking stops: power, gas and exits

1. **Shut off the electricity and gas.**
2. **Open the door to secure an exit.** If the building twists, doors may not open.
3. Use the **stairs** to get out. **Do not take the elevator.**

## 3. By place

| Where you are | What to do |
|---|---|
| **In an elevator** | Press all the floor buttons, get off at the first floor where the door opens, then use the stairs |
| **At school** | When the shaking stops, follow your teacher's directions, keep away from windows and go to the sports field |
| **Outside a building** | Keep away from glass, signboards, walls and utility poles, protect your head with a bag or your hands, and move to open ground |
| **When evacuating** | Do not use a car; walk to a wide open space such as a sports field or park |

## 4. After you evacuate

Act on **official information**, such as the radio or public announcements from government agencies. Give priority to disaster text alerts and broadcasts over unsourced rumors circulating on messaging apps. Aftershocks may follow, so do not go straight back into a cracked building.

## 5. What you can do ahead of time

- Fix heavy furniture to the wall, and do not keep objects that could fall where you sleep.
- Check the outdoor earthquake evacuation sites near your home and workplace (Chapter 3).
- Knowing the earthquake alert thresholds lets you estimate how large an earthquake is when an alert arrives (Chapter 1).

## Summary

- While it shakes, **protect your body under a table**; when it stops, **shut off power and gas and open the door**.
- Use the **stairs** to get out; elevators are off-limits.
- Outdoors, move away from buildings to **open ground**, on foot rather than by car.
- After you evacuate, follow official guidance.
