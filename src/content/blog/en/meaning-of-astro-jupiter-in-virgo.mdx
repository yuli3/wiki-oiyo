**Luck in the details — refined skill becomes the path of expansion.** Jupiter is the planet of "where do I find growth and meaning," and Virgo makes that answer come out carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter ♃ — expansion, luck, the direction of belief |
| Sign | Virgo ♍ — analysis, improvement, usefulness |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 12 years (≈1 year per sign) |

## Core tendency

Jupiter governs **expansion, luck, the direction of belief**. When that energy passes through Virgo, it shows up carefully and precisely, seeking a better way.

This is why the same Jupiter can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Virgo's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **analysis that misses no detail**
- **quiet, reliable competence**
- **ceaseless self-improvement**

## Watch out for

- perfectionism delays the start
- the critic's edge turns on the self

## Growth and task

Jupiter changes sign on roughly a 12-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Jupiter in Virgo, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Jupiter is in Virgo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Jupiter sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
