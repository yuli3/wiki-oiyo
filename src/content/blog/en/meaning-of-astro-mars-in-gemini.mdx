**Mars that fights with words — debate becomes sparring.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Gemini makes that answer come out lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Gemini ♊ — curiosity, language, connection |
| Element · Modality | Air · Mutable |

| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Gemini, it shows up lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Gemini's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **fast learning and a gift for explaining**
- **sociability that works anywhere**
- **a knack for linking situations**

## Watch out for

- scattered attention can stay shallow
- words can outrun the heart

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Gemini way (lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Gemini, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
