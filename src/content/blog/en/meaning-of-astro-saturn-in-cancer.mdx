**A lesson in emotional expression — weakness can be shown without collapse.** Saturn is the planet of "what tests make me stronger," and Cancer makes that answer come out cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Saturn ♄ — limits, responsibility, the task of mastery |
| Sign | Cancer ♋ — protection, memory, care |
| Element · Modality | Water · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Moon |
| Orbital cycle | about 29 years (≈2.5 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Saturn governs **limits, responsibility, the task of mastery**. When that energy passes through Cancer, it shows up cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

This is why the same Saturn can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Cancer's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a protective instinct that shelters others**
- **emotional sensitivity that reads the room**
- **devotion to one's own people**

## Watch out for

- old wounds are remembered and one withdraws
- the tides of mood run high

## Growth and task

Saturn changes sign on roughly a 29-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Saturn in Cancer, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Saturn is in Cancer, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Saturn sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
