**The transformation of technological power — networks become the new throne.** Pluto is the planet of "what does our generation end and remake," and Aquarius makes that answer come out differently, yet for everyone.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Pluto ♇ — transformation, power, rebirth |
| Sign | Aquarius ♒ — originality, solidarity, the future |
| Element · Modality | Air · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Uranus |
| Orbital cycle | about 248 years (12–30 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Pluto governs **transformation, power, rebirth**. When that energy passes through Aquarius, it shows up differently, yet for everyone.

This is why the same Pluto can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aquarius's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **ideas ahead of their time**
- **a horizontal sense of solidarity**
- **objectivity unswept by emotion**

## Watch out for

- difference for its own sake is a trap
- close relationships can be awkward

## How to read it as a generational placement

Pluto stays in one sign for years or decades, so Pluto in Aquarius is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Pluto is in Aquarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Pluto sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
