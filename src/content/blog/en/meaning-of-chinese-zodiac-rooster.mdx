<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **Rooster** is the tenth animal in the Chinese zodiac and is paired with
  the Earthly Branch **You**. It is a symbolic and cultural cycle marker, not a
  deterministic predictor of personality, fortune, or destiny.
</HighlightBox>

## Cultural associations and traditional traits

The Rooster is traditionally associated with precision, visibility, discipline,
timing, announcement, and alertness. The rooster's daily crowing makes it a
natural symbol of noticing the hour, calling attention, and marking the start
of activity.

In zodiac folklore, Rooster-year people may be described as organized,
observant, neat, candid, hardworking, image-conscious, or attentive to detail.
These descriptions are cultural associations rather than psychological
measurements. They should be read as symbolic language, not as proof that a
person is vain, critical, honest, disciplined, lucky, or unlucky.

## Earthly Branch and cycle position

The Rooster corresponds to **You**, the tenth of the 12 Earthly Branches. It
follows Monkey and precedes Dog in the animal sequence. The animal is the
popular mnemonic attached to the branch; the full traditional year name also
contains a Heavenly Stem, creating one step in the 60-year sexagenary cycle.

Representative Rooster-year labels include **1981, 1993, 2005, and 2017**.
These are common Gregorian-year references. People born near January or early
February should check the relevant calendar boundary, since popular zodiac
counting and Four Pillars calculation do not simply begin with January 1.

## Compatibility folklore

Compatibility folklore often groups the Rooster with **Ox** and **Snake** in a
harmonious triad. Rooster and **Rabbit** are commonly treated as a clashing
pair. These pairings are traditional symbolic categories, not guarantees about
real relationships. Human compatibility depends on context, communication,
values, and choices.

## Common Misunderstanding

The Rooster sign does not determine whether someone is orderly, outspoken,
critical, proud, successful, or unsuccessful. It is a birth-year animal label
attached to the Earthly Branch You. A full East Asian fortune reading uses the
Four Pillars, or saju, which reads year, month, day, and hour together through
Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. See the broader
[Chinese zodiac definition](/en/meaning-of-chinese-zodiac/) and the related
[Palja definition](/en/meaning-of-palja/) for context.

## Responsible use

Rooster symbolism is a cultural and symbolic tradition for reflection and
entertainment. It should not be used as deterministic prediction or as evidence
for judging personality, compatibility, or life direction.
