A young person stands on a windy hill, sword in hand, looking in every direction. The Page of Swords is lively curiosity toward the world — an apprentice of intellect who asks, observes, and learns.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | Page — the one who learns |
| Key words | Curiosity, observation, information gathering, immature speech |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when curiosity and the desire to learn come alive. Gather information and ask questions. However, spreading collected information without verification is a common mistake of the Page.

- **Love**: A stage of exploring the other person, light flirtation, and interesting conversation. Keep the boundary between interrogation and genuine interest.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable signs for research, study, and certification preparation. Financial information also comes in actively, so make source-checking a habit.

## Reversed Meaning

Gossip, half-formed information, and suspicion that behaves like surveillance. A sword is not there only to stab; it is also there to separate and clarify.
