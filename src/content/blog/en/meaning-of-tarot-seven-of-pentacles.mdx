A farmer leans on a hoe and looks at the pentacle bush he has grown. The harvest is not here yet. This is a time of midterm evaluation, weighing whether to wait longer or move to another field.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material reality, money, body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 7 — Pause for evaluation |
| Key words | Progress review, patience, investment review, the farming of waiting |

## Upright Meaning

This is a midpoint review of your efforts. Even if results feel slow, the roots are still growing. Still, evaluate objectively and distinguish between crops worth waiting for and crops that should be turned under.

- **Love**: A midpoint check on the relationship. Look honestly at whether it is ripening or withering.
- **Work & Money**: A good time to evaluate a long-term project or review an investment portfolio. A rushed harvest leaves only unripe fruit.

## Reversed Meaning

Doubt about poor returns compared with the effort invested, or attachment to sunk costs. Do not bury your future just because the time already spent feels too precious to lose.

## Today's Advice

> "Will this field still be worth tending a year from now?" Let this one question complete your review.
