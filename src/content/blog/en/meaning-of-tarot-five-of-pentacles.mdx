Two people pass through a snowstorm. Beside them, the lit stained glass of a church glows, but they walk past without knocking on the door. The deeper problem is not lack, but isolation.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material life, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 5 — trial of lack |
| Key words | Poverty, isolation, hard time, unseen help |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period of feeling lack in money, health, or belonging. But the key point in the image is that help is right beside you. Look at whether pride or resignation is keeping you from knocking on the door.

- **Love**: A lonely period even when you are together, or a bond that deepens as you endure hardship together.
- **Work & Money**: The tunnel of reduced income or a difficult job search. Money may be tight, but there are definite doors to knock on, such as support programs or help from people around you.

## Reversed Meaning

The end of the tunnel, the beginning of recovery, the acceptance of help. Do not distrust the light that has come in.

## Today's Advice

> Contact one place where you can ask for help today. Doors open to those who knock.
