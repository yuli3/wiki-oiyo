Mu (戊) is a towering great mountain. It does not move easily, holds all kinds of things within it, and can also become an embankment that blocks the flow of water. As the fifth character, standing at the center of the ten heavenly stems, it is the character that holds the center.

## Personality and Temperament

The Mu Earth day master is dependable and reserved, and treats promises and trust as essential. Mu Earth often takes on the role of mediating conflicts between people, and has a steady nature that is not easily shaken by ordinary difficulties. Because it does not readily reveal its inner thoughts, it can also leave a mysterious impression.

- **Strengths**: Trustworthiness, stability, and inclusiveness. A weighty presence that becomes the center of gravity in organizations and relationships.
- **Points to watch**: A mountain cannot move on its own. Mu Earth can be slow to change, stubborn, and sparing with expression, which may make others feel frustrated. Once offended, it can become as immovable as a mountain.

## Suitable Fields

Fields where trust and weight become assets, such as finance and real estate, administration, mediation and law, and construction.

## Relationships with Other Characters

When Mu meets Gui water, like drizzle, the dry mountain becomes moist (Mu-Gui combination). When Bing fire, like the sun, shines on it, all things on the mountain grow. When Jia wood takes root, the mountain becomes green.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is Mu Earth a boring person?**

A. A mountain is quiet not because it is empty, but because it holds everything. Mu Earth's humor and affection are limited editions that are slowly revealed only to people who are close. In a relationship where trust has been built, Mu Earth is more reliable and warm than anyone.
