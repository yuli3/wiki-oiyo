Ipchu (立秋) is the 13th of the 24 solar terms, falling around August 7-9, when the Sun reaches celestial longitude 135°.

## Meaning and Origin

Ipchu means "autumn (秋) rises or begins." The midday heat still lingers, but the texture of the morning and evening breeze begins to change — it is the paradoxical solar term in which autumn begins within the heat.

## Customs and Foods

Around this time, people planted cabbage and radish for kimchi-making. Clear weather on Ipchu was taken as a sign of a good harvest, and because rice was ripening in earnest, farming households watched the sky closely.

## Saju and the Solar Terms

The calendar of myeongnihak moves by the solar terms. Ipchu is one of the 12 solar nodes (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in saju changes to [Sin (申) month](/ko/meaning-of-saju-branch-sin-monkey/).** This is why the sexagenary sign of the birth month is determined by solar terms rather than by the lunar calendar date.
