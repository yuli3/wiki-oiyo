**Love built through trust — it does not begin lightly, and it takes responsibility deeply.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Capricorn makes that answer come out step by step, systematically toward the summit.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Capricorn ♑ — goals, structure, achievement |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Saturn |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Capricorn, it shows up step by step, systematically toward the summit.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Capricorn's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **patience that endures the long game**
- **the skill to build structures**
- **trustworthiness that carries responsibility**

## Watch out for

- emotion is postponed in favor of results
- harshest of all on oneself

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Capricorn way (step by step, systematically toward the summit) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Capricorn, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
