**The dream of the abyss — a generation drawn to the mysteries of death and rebirth.** Neptune is the planet of "what does our generation dream of, and what enchants it," and Scorpio makes that answer come out deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Neptune ♆ — dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries |
| Sign | Scorpio ♏ — intensity, insight, regeneration |
| Element · Modality | Water · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Pluto |
| Orbital cycle | about 165 years (≈14 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Neptune governs **dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries**. When that energy passes through Scorpio, it shows up deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

This is why the same Neptune can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Scorpio's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **insight that pierces to the essence**
- **all-in immersion**
- **the power to rebuild after collapse**

## Watch out for

- suspicion and control tighten relationships
- feelings hidden too long erupt

## How to read it as a generational placement

Neptune stays in one sign for years or decades, so Neptune in Scorpio is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Neptune is in Scorpio, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Neptune sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
