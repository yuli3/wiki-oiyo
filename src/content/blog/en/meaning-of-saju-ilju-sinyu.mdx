The Sin-Yu (辛酉) Ilju is the 58th pillar of the sexagenary cycle (六十甲子). It consists of the Heavenly Stem Sin (Yin Metal), representing a sharply shining jewel, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Yu (the Rooster, Metal). It is the image of a perfectly polished gem—a sharpness that requires no further refinement.

## Personality and Temperament

The Sin-Yu Ilju represents a craftsman-like personality characterized by perfectionism and strong self-esteem. Because it sits in the position of *Geonrok* (Establishing Prosperity), individuals of this pillar often become the standard in their own fields.

- **Strengths**: Highest level of perfection, unwavering professionalism.
- **Points of Caution**: You are prone to noticing the flaws of others too easily. It is often sufficient to voice only half of your criticism.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Sin (辛) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-sin/) sits atop the [Yu (酉) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-yu/). Based on the main qi of the Ilji (daily branch), the 10 God is [Bigyeon (比肩)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-bigyeon/), and the 12 Stages of Qi is [Geonrok (建祿)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-geonrok/). Bigyeon is the star of the ego and subjectivity; the strength to stand on one's own is supported by the Ilji. As it occupies the *Geonrok* position, it is a solid structure that relies on one's own abilities to achieve independence.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to *Seokryumok* (Pomegranate Wood), a classification frequently used in traditional Saju analysis to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
