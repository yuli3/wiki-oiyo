A young person stands in the middle of a desert, gazing with wonder at a staff that has begun to sprout. The Page of Wands is someone who has discovered the passion within for the first time — still awkward, but burning with the purest fire.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | Page — the one who learns |
| Key words | Discovering passion, spirit of exploration, good news, beginner's excitement |

## Upright Meaning

Excitement arrives around a new interest, skill, or field. Do not try to be good at it immediately; explore first. Welcome news related to passion may also come.

- **Love**: Fresh affection and an excited beginning. A light spirit of adventure opens the door to connection.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable signs for learning a new field, apprenticeship, or an intern stage. This is a period when experience accumulates more than money.

## Reversed Meaning

A mood that catches fire quickly and cools just as quickly, or excitement without direction. The problem is not passion itself, but the lack of a system for consistency.

## Today's Advice

> Keep going for just three weeks. That is the minimum time for a spark to become a campfire.
