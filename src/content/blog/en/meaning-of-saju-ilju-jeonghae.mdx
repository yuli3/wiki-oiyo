The Jeonghae (丁亥) Ilju is the 24th pillar of the Sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Jeong (丁), a Yin Fire symbolizing a candle that illuminates the darkness, sitting upon the Earthly Branch Hae (亥), the abundant Pig. It is the image of a lighthouse shining over the night sea—a guide that lights the way in the darkness.

## Personality and Temperament

The Jeonghae Ilju is a guide-type personality, characterized by gentleness and highly developed intuition. With a nature that quietly illuminates its surroundings, it earns trust and has a natural affinity for honorable positions.

- **Strengths**: Warm intuition and a character that inspires trust.
- **Points of Caution**: Being too kind can lead to difficulty in saying no and a tendency to procrastinate on decisions. A lighthouse must remain steadfast.

## Structural Analysis — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Jeong (丁) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-jeong/) sits upon the [Hae (亥) Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-hae/). Based on the primary energy (Jeonggi) of the day branch, the 10-God is the [Jeonggwan (正官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeonggwan/), and the 12-Stage Life Cycle is [Tae (胎)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-tae/). Jeonggwan is the star of honor and order, forming a foundation of dignity that seeks recognition within established norms. The position of Tae (Embryo) signifies a structure of conception that always harbors new possibilities.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Oksangto (屋上土, Earth on the Roof), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
