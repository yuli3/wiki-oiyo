A giant wheel floats above the clouds, with a sphinx, a serpent, and Anubis moving around its edge. In the four corners, the symbols of the four evangelists — angel, eagle, lion, and bull — read their books. The wheel turns without asking us. What we can choose is our posture upon it.

## Reading the Symbols

The TARO letters on the wheel can become ROTA, the Latin word for "wheel," when rotated. The sphinx represents the unchanging center within change, while the two beings moving up and down symbolize that rising and falling are part of one movement.

## Upright Meaning

The current of luck is turning in your direction. Chance-like opportunities, unexpected meetings, the end of stagnation — yet because the wheel keeps turning, you need to decide while the flow is favorable.

- **Love**: A fated turning point. It suggests an accidental reunion or the sudden beginning of a new connection.
- **Work & Study**: Results you have been waiting for, a transfer, a new assignment, or another shift in circumstances. Get onto the flow.
- **Money**: Entry into an upward cycle. However, gambling-style investment that relies only on "luck" calls forth the wheel's opposite side.

## Reversed Meaning

The Wheel of Fortune reversed shows a section where the flow is briefly turning downward. This may be a time when results do not match your effort, but the essence of the wheel is that it keeps turning. What you need to do in a downward phase is not blame yourself, but prepare for the next rise.

## Card Combinations

- **Wheel of Fortune + The Sun**: Golden timing, with both the flow and the result in good condition.
- **Wheel of Fortune + The Hanged Man**: A wheel that cannot be turned right now. Waiting itself is the strategy.

## FAQ

**Q. If Wheel of Fortune appears, does that mean effort does not matter?**

A. The opposite. The wheel only tells you about "timing"; it is a prepared person who makes use of that timing. Even when the same luck arrives, one person gets on board while another lets it pass. Effort is the work of making a vessel that can receive luck.
