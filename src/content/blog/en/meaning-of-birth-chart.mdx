<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  A **birth chart** is a symbolic map of the sky at a specific birth time and place. It is also called a **natal chart**.
</HighlightBox>

## The Four Layers

1. **Planets** show psychological drives.
2. **Zodiac signs** show expression style.
3. **Houses** show life areas.
4. **Aspects** show relationships between planetary drives.

Reading a chart means combining these layers. "Venus in Scorpio in the 7th house" is not just Venus, Scorpio, or the 7th house alone. It is value, intimacy, and partnership woven into one sentence.

## Why Birth Time Matters

The birth time determines the Ascendant and house layout. Without a reliable time, astrologers can still read planets and signs, but house-based interpretation becomes uncertain.

### FAQ

**Is a birth chart a fixed fate?**  
No. In psychological astrology it is read as a map of tendencies, tensions, and developmental themes.

**Can I read a chart without exact birth time?**  
Partly. Planetary signs are usable, but rising sign and houses may be unreliable.
