**A placement of work luck and health luck — when routines settle, growth can become substantial.** A house is the **life stage** where a planet acts. When Jupiter enters the 6th house, the question "where do I find growth and meaning?" unfolds in the realm of **work, health, routines, and service**.

## How This Placement Works

The 6th house deals with work, health, routines, and service. When Jupiter enters this house, the energy of **expansion, luck, and the direction of belief** comes to reside on that stage.

Cadent houses are places of learning and adaptation — this placement works as an inner process, then becomes visible after skill and familiarity develop.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of work, health, routines, and service receives Jupiter's gift (**expansion**). Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Virgo, can help read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Jupiter in the 6th house is **overconcentration on that stage** — balance is needed so that work, health, routines, and service do not become all of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses and signs different?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (stage). With Jupiter in the 6th house, the stage is work, health, routines, and service, while the acting tone is set by the sign where Jupiter is located.

**Q. What do you need to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
