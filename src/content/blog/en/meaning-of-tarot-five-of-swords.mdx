Behind a victor gathering swords, the defeated walk away. The sky is torn. You won, but what did you win for? This card asks about the cost of victory.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 5 — the cost of conflict |
| Key words | Hollow victory, pride fight, winning and losing |

## Upright Meaning

Even if you win a conflict, you may pay with a relationship or reputation. You do not need to win every argument. Some swords are better left on the ground.

- **Love**: A relationship bruised by pride battles. At the end of "I am right," you may be left alone.
- **Work & Money**: In the middle of office politics or dispute. Financially, legal or conflict-related costs can eat into profit, so calculate the practical benefit.

## Reversed Meaning

The end of a war of attrition, the beginning of reconciliation, or the recognition of defeat and its lesson. You did not lose; you ended it.

## Today's Advice

> Write down exactly what you gain by winning this fight. If the answer is weak, put down the sword.
