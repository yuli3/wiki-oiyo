The Im-Sul (壬戌) Ilju is the 59th pillar of the sexagenary cycle, where the Heavenly Stem Im (Yang Water), representing a vast and deep ocean, sits upon the Earthly Branch Sul (the loyal Dog), representing the earth. It is the image of a deep lake held within an autumn mountain—a figure of weight concealing great strategy.

## Personality and Temperament

The Im-Sul Ilju is a strategist type who possesses both intellect and a sense of responsibility. Driven by the energy of the Goegang (魁罡), they are calm in ordinary times but make decisive, profound moves at critical moments.

- **Strengths**: Profound strategy, heavy reliability, and the ability to make the winning move in a crisis.
- **Points of Caution**: Because they do not reveal their inner thoughts, they are often misunderstood. Even a lake must show its ripples from time to time.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Im (壬) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-im/) sits upon the [Sul (戌) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sul/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (daily branch), the 10 God is [Pyeongwan (偏官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeongwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Gwandae (冠帶)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-gwandae/). Pyeongwan is the star of challenge and responsibility—it creates a strong vessel that is tempered by enduring pressure. As it sits in the position of Gwandae, it is a structure of ascent, stepping onto the stage with ambition.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Daehaesu (Great Ocean Water), a classification often used in traditional Myeongri alongside paired pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
