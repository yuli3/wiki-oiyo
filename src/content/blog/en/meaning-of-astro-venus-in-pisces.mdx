**Venus at its deepest — devoted, but learning to tell dream from reality.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Pisces makes that answer come out boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Pisces ♓ — empathy, imagination, transcendence |
| Element · Modality | Water · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Neptune |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Pisces, it shows up boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Pisces's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **empathy that feels others' emotions as one's own**
- **imagination that pictures beyond the real**
- **unconditional acceptance**

## Watch out for

- blurred boundaries lead to burnout
- escapism becomes a sweet trap

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Pisces way (boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Pisces, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
