Against a background of rolling waves, a figure juggles two pentacles with an infinity-shaped cord. This is the card of finding balance amid unstable conditions — flexible management in motion.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material life, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Position | 2 — Balancing |
| Key words | Juggling, priorities, flexible finances, keeping two things in play |

## Upright Meaning

Work and home, two assignments, income and expenses — this is a time when several balls are in the air at once. Perfect balance does not exist; the secret is finding the rhythm.

- **Love**: The task of distributing time for the relationship amid a busy daily life. Even short but regular time keeps the balance.
- **Work & Money**: A period of multitasking and flexible response. Financially, cash-flow management is key — match the rhythm of what comes in and goes out.

## Reversed Meaning

Overload just before dropping the balls, or a loss of priorities. This is not the time to choose one of two things, but to set down three out of five.

## Advice for Today

> Not every ball is made of glass. Let go first of the balls that will bounce back even if they fall.
