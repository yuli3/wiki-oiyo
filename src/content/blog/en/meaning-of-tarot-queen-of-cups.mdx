A queen sits on a seaside throne, gazing at a closed chalice. The Queen of Cups is a person who rules the sea of emotion — the healer archetype, deeply empathetic without being swept away.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | Queen — the one who holds |
| Key words | Deep empathy, healing, mature intuition, sanctuary for the heart |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when empathy and intuition deepen. People's hearts gather toward you, and comfort and advice carry great power. Care for your own emotions with the same sincerity.

- **Love**: Deep and devoted love, and a relationship that feels emotionally safe. You can read the heart behind the other person's words.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable signs in counseling, care, and the arts. Financially, your intuition is accurate, so avoid any deal that feels subtly wrong.

## Reversed Meaning

Exhaustion from excessive empathy, or the loss of emotional boundaries. You may be drowning in someone else's sea while your own cup runs empty.

## Today's Advice

> Today, listen to your own heart with the same ears you use to hear others.
