Eight cups are stacked neatly, and a figure walks away toward the mountains under moonlight. This is not leaving because what was built is lacking, but because it is not enough to satisfy the soul — a card of mature farewell.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 8 — practiced departure |
| Key words | Leaving, seeking deeper meaning, releasing lingering attachment, pilgrimage |

## Upright Meaning

It is time to leave behind what you worked hard to build and seek deeper satisfaction. This is not escape but graduation — a departure only someone who has truly stayed can choose.

- **Love**: Ending a relationship where the heart has already left, or deciding to give up surface comfort for the sake of a deeper bond.
- **Work & Money**: A transition away from work that is stable but meaningless. Even if there is a financial cost, the move is aligned with the right direction.

## Reversed Meaning

You may be staying out of attachment when you need to leave, or trying to escape when you need to stay. Examine the motive behind departure honestly.

## Today's Advice

> Tie your shoes only when it is not "because I hate it here," but "because I need to go there."
