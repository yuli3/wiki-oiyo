The Sin-Hae (辛亥) Ilju is the 48th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Sin (Yin Metal), a sharply shining gemstone, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Hae (the Pig), which represents abundant water. It is the image of a jewel washed in clear water—polished, refined, and brilliant.

## Personality and Temperament

The Sin-Hae Ilju is a talented type, characterized by intelligence and brilliant eloquence. With the wit of the Hurting Officer, they pierce through to the core of matters, and like a polished jewel, they stand out wherever they go.

- **Strengths**: Piercing intelligence, radiant eloquence, and talent.
- **Points of Caution**: As one's talents shine brighter, humility is put to the test. A jewel does not boast of itself.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Sin (辛) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-sin/) sits atop the [Hae (亥) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-hae/). Based on the main qi of the daily branch, the Ten God is the [Hurting Officer (傷官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-sanggwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life is the [Bath (沐浴)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-mokyok/). The Hurting Officer is the star of eloquence and creativity—the ability to break molds and express oneself is both their charm and a potential source of gossip. The Bath stage indicates a structure where senses and charm shine, but also one prone to fluctuation.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Chacheongeum (釵釧金), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
