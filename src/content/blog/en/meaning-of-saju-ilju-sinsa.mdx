The Sinsa (辛巳) Ilju is the 18th pillar of the sexagenary cycle, where the Heavenly Stem Sin (辛)—the Yin Metal representing a sharply shining jewel—sits upon the Earthly Branch Sa (巳), the snake representing insight. It is the image of a jewel refined and glowing within fire; a configuration where trials become brilliance.

## Personality and Temperament

The Sinsa Ilju is a perfectionist who is sophisticated and values honor. They shine brightest under pressure and reveal their true worth on formal stages.

- **Strengths**: Refined sense and dignity, mental resilience to withstand pressure.
- **Points to Note**: They apply harsh standards to themselves. They need to practice self-compassion.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Sin (辛) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-sin/) sits atop the [Sa (巳) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10 God is [Jeong-gwan (正官)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeonggwan/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Sa (死)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-sa/). Jeong-gwan is the star of honor and order—the foundation is a sense of dignity that seeks recognition within established norms. Because it sits in the 'Sa' (Death) stage, it is a structure of deep immersion, focusing on one path.

In terms of Nabeum (phonetic element), it belongs to Baek-rap-geum (白鑞金 / Pewter Metal), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
