**A heart that settles only once talked through — conversation is comfort.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Gemini makes that answer come out lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Gemini ♊ — curiosity, language, connection |
| Element · Modality | Air · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Gemini, it shows up lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Gemini's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **fast learning and a gift for explaining**
- **sociability that works anywhere**
- **a knack for linking situations**

## Watch out for

- scattered attention can stay shallow
- words can outrun the heart

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Gemini way (lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Gemini, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
