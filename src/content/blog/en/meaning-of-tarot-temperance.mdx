An angel stands with one foot in water and one foot on land, pouring water between two cups. The water flows against gravity. Temperance is not simple restraint, but an active skill that blends opposing elements to create something better.

## Reading the Symbols

The two feet placed across water and earth represent the coexistence of emotion and reality, the stream between the two cups symbolizes constant adjustment, and the triangle on the chest points to sacred balance. The crown at the end of the path is the reward for this process.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time when everything begins to resolve once you avoid extremes and find the middle point. Gradual improvement works better than a quick solution, and the answer is blending two things rather than choosing only one. This is also a good card for healing the body and mind.

- **Love**: The process of two different people learning to adjust to one another. When you acknowledge differences in emotional temperature, the relationship deepens.
- **Work & Study**: A period of collaboration and mediation. You shine in the role of a bridge between departments, generations, or positions.
- **Money**: The principle of diversification and balance. This is a good time to rebalance a portfolio.

## Reversed Meaning

Temperance reversed shows that the ratio has broken down. Work and rest, emotion and reason, spending and saving: somewhere, there is excess. Impatience is the main culprit that breaks every balance, so slow the pace first.

## Card Combinations

- **Temperance + The Star**: The gentlest combination of healing and hope. A clear sign of recovery.
- **Temperance + The Devil**: A confrontation between moderation and excess. Your self-control is being tested.

## FAQ

**Q. Does Temperance mean I should simply hold back?**

A. It is not forced suppression, but appropriate blending. It does not mean reducing what you want to zero; it means finding the right amount. In dieting terms, it is not starvation, but adjusting the diet.
