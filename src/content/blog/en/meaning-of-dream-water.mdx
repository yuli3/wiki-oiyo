Water dreams are among the broadest dreams to interpret. In traditional Korean dream interpretation, clear and abundant water was seen as a symbol of wealth and blessing, while muddy or overflowing water was read as worry and confusion. Modern psychology tends to view water as the flow of emotion and the unconscious. The meaning changes depending on whether you were looking at the water or entering it.

## At a Glance

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Main symbols | Emotion, wealth, cleansing, the unconscious, flow |
| When it is auspicious | Clear water flowing abundantly, or drinking clean water, suggests recovery and opportunity. |
| When it is troubling | Muddy water, flooding, or being swept away may reflect emotional overload and confusion. |
| Psychological meaning | It is often interpreted as repressed emotion, adaptation to change, or a message from the unconscious. |

## Interpretations by Situation

- **Seeing clear water**: This symbolizes a clearer mind and the possibility that matters will flow smoothly.
- **Drinking water**: This is read as replenished energy, healthy recovery, and a willingness to accept needed information or help.
- **A flood**: This may show that emotions have exceeded your range of control or that you feel overwhelmed by major change.
- **Falling into muddy water**: This warns that you may become caught in an unclear problem in a relationship or at work.
- **Crossing a river or sea**: This is interpreted as a transition to another stage, moving, a career change, or a psychological rite of passage.

## Psychological Reading

From a Freudian perspective, water is often interpreted in connection with birth, the wish to be protected, and emotional dependency. Entering water may show a desire to leave real-life tension and return to a safe place.

For Jung, water is one of the most representative symbols of the unconscious. Calm water is often read as contact with the inner self, while rough currents show the power of emotions that have not yet been handled.

## If You Had This Dream

First try to remember the water's color, depth, and movement. If you have recently been postponing emotions, it may help to let them flow through conversation or writing. Do not read a water dream only as money luck. Check the state of your mind as well.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is a dream about clear water a good dream?**

A. Generally, it is read as auspicious. If the water was abundant and you felt at ease, the meanings of recovery, cleansing, and opportunity are especially strong.

**Q. Is falling into water a bad dream?**

A. If you felt intense fear, it may express pressure. But if you swam out on your own, it can also mean you have the strength to handle and escape a problem.
