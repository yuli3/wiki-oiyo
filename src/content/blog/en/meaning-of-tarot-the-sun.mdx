In front of a wall of blooming sunflowers, a child rides a white horse with both arms open wide. This is the reward given to the traveler who has passed through the night road of The Moon: bright daylight with nothing to hide and nothing to fear.

## Reading the Symbols

The child symbolizes recovered innocence and freedom, the white horse represents purified energy, and the sunflower symbolizes the vitality of living in alignment with the sun. The straight and curved rays of the sun show the complete integration of reason and emotion.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time of clear success and good results. The fruits of your effort are publicly recognized, and health and vitality increase. It is also the ideal time to reveal what has been hidden.

- **Love**: Open love, a public relationship, blessings for marriage. It can also indicate joy related to children.
- **Work & Study**: Public achievement such as passing an exam, receiving an award, or being promoted. Your results move onto the stage.
- **Money**: Abundance and ease. Just be careful that excitement does not turn into excessive spending.

## Reversed Meaning

The Sun reversed is still the sun. Success is not canceled; it is only a little dimmed or delayed. The only shadow is carelessness from overconfidence, or a mindset that cannot enjoy joy when it arrives.

## Card Combinations

- **The Sun + The World**: Great success and completion. One of the finest combinations in tarot.
- **The Sun + The Moon**: Clarity after confusion. The whole truth is revealed.

## FAQ

**Q. Is it true that The Sun reversed is not bad either?**

A. Yes. The Sun is one of the few cards whose essence does not darken even when reversed. It has the nuance of "success, but a little later" or "joy, but with some lingering discomfort." The message is to check your attitude toward receiving the result, rather than the result itself.
