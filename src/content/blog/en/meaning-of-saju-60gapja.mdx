The Sexagenary Cycle (六十甲子) consists of 60 combinations formed by pairing the Ten Heavenly Stems and the Twelve Earthly Branches in sequence. The pillar of the day you were born is the **Ilju (日柱)** — the central axis that defines "the self" in Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny). If you do not know your Ilju, please check it first using the [Saju Calculator](https://oiyo.net/en/saju/calculator).
