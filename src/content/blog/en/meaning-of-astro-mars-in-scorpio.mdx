**Mars at its deepest — quiet, relentless, and willing to go to the end.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Scorpio makes that answer come out deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Scorpio ♏ — intensity, insight, regeneration |
| Element · Modality | Water · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Pluto |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Scorpio, it shows up deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Scorpio's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **insight that pierces to the essence**
- **all-in immersion**
- **the power to rebuild after collapse**

## Watch out for

- suspicion and control tighten relationships
- feelings hidden too long erupt

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Scorpio way (deeply and intensely, all the way to the end) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Scorpio, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/en/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
