**Finding stability through achievement — you feel at ease when things go well at work.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When the Moon is in the 10th House, the question "What makes me feel secure?" unfolds in the realms of **career, reputation, and social calling.**

## At a Glance

| Category | Content |
|---|---|

| House | 10th House · House of Achievement — Career, fame, the pinnacle of life |

| Natural Sign · Classification | Capricorn · Angular House |

## How This Placement Works

The 10th House deals with career, reputation, and social calling. When the Moon enters this house, the energy of **emotions, instincts, and the need for security** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Functions Well

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of career, reputation, and social calling receive the Moon's gift (**a sense of security**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Capricorn, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of the Moon in the 10th House is **becoming overly focused on that stage** — balance is needed to ensure that the areas of career, reputation, and social calling do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What is the difference between a house and a sign?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have the Moon in the 10th House, the stage is your career, reputation, and social calling, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which the Moon is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your exact **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
