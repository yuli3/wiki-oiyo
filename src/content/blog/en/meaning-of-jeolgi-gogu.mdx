Gogu (穀雨) is the 6th of the 24 solar terms, falling around April 19-21, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 30°.

## Meaning and Origins

Gogu means "rain (雨) that nourishes grain (穀)." It is the final solar term of spring, when spring rain is understood to enrich all grains. Around this time, people soaked rice seeds and prepared seedbeds.

## Customs and Foods

People believed that drinking "Gogu water," sap collected from birch and hardy kiwi trees deep in the mountains, was good for the body. Ujeoncha (雨前茶), tea made from leaves picked before Gogu, is still regarded as a premium tea today.

## Saju and the Solar Term

Gogu is a mid-qi (中氣), marking the middle of the lunar month. In myeongli, the boundaries of the month pillar (月柱) are based on the twelve seasonal nodes (節), while the mid-qi is read as the point when that month's qi becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Time

As rain helps grain grow, this is a time for steady watering — try adding a little care each day to something you have already started.
