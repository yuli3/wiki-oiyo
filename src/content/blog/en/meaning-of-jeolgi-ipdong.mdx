Ipdong (立冬) is the 19th of the 24 solar terms, falling around November 6-8 when the sun reaches celestial longitude 225°.

## Meaning and origin

Ipdong means "winter (冬) stands up" or "winter begins," marking the start of the winter season. Water begins to freeze, the ground hardens, and animals enter hibernation.

## Customs and foods

The period around Ipdong was considered the right time for kimjang, the large-scale preparation of kimchi for winter. Villages began their biggest winter preparation by making kimchi together through communal labor. There was also a custom called chigyemi (雉鷄米), in which food was offered to village elders.

## Saju and solar terms

The calendar of Korean Four Pillars astrology moves by solar terms. Ipdong is one of the 12 solar term nodes (節), and **from this day, the month pillar (月柱) in Saju changes to the [Hae (亥) month](/ko/meaning-of-saju-branch-hae/).** This is why the sexagenary sign of a birth month is determined by solar terms, not by lunar calendar dates.

## Wisdom for this time

Preparing for winter is half the work of getting through it. This is a time to secure the body, the home, and plans for closing out the year.
