Since December 2020 in multi-family apartments and December 2021 across all single-family homes, South Korea has enforced a nationwide **Mandatory Dedicated Clear PET Bottle Separation Scheme**. This policy separates transparent, unpigmented drinking water and soft drink bottles from colored plastics and generic polymers, addressing a structural bottleneck in high-value circular recycling.

Before this statutory rule, clear PET was co-mingled with tinted plastics and household sundries, leading to polymer contamination. Recycled flakes could only be downcycled into low-grade industrial staples such as non-woven stuffing or roadside geotextiles. High-grade circular fibers and bottle-to-bottle food-grade pellets had to be imported from abroad.

---

## 1. The 4-Step Statutory Preparation Protocol

Under guidelines established by the Ministry of Environment and the Korea Resource Circulation Service, every household must follow four mechanical steps before depositing clear PET:

```
[1. Empty & Rinse] ──> [2. Strip Outer Label] ──> [3. Squeeze Air Flat] ──> [4. Screw Cap On]
```

1. **Empty and Rinse**: Residual sugars, dairy proteins, or oil droplets quickly foster mold and bacterial biofilms inside collection bins, discoloring flakes during optical sorting. Wash out all contents with clean water.
2. **Strip Outer Label**: Outer wrap labels are typically manufactured from polypropylene (PP) or oriented polystyrene (OPS). Even micro-fragments of label adhesive melt during thermal extrusion, introducing black particulate inclusions into virgin-grade polyester filaments.
3. **Compress Air Flat**: Tightly compress the bottle lengthwise to reduce storage and logistical transport volume by over 70%.
4. **Fasten Cap Firmly**: Bottle caps and neck rings are fabricated from high-density polyethylene (HDPE) or polypropylene (PP). Although chemically distinct from the PET body, recyclers recommend screwing the cap on after flattening to prevent re-inflation and keep internal cavities clean.

## 2. Density Separation: Why Caps Float and PET Sinks

A common consumer inquiry is why HDPE/PP caps can remain attached when sorting clear PET. The answer lies in downstream industrial float-sink sink-float density tanks:

| Polymer Type | Component | Specific Gravity (Density) | Behavior in Water Tank (1.00 g/cm³) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate)** | Main Bottle Body | **1.33 ~ 1.40 g/cm³** | **Sinks rapidly to bottom** |
| **HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)** | Bottle Screw Cap | **0.94 ~ 0.96 g/cm³** | **Floats to surface** |
| **PP (Polypropylene)** | Retaining Neck Ring | **0.90 ~ 0.91 g/cm³** | **Floats to surface** |

When shredded flakes enter pure water wash tanks, gravity performs automated physical separation: the pure PET chips sink and are gathered from the base for food-contact bottle-to-bottle pelletizing, while HDPE/PP caps float and are skimmed off the surface for secondary molding.

## 3. Disqualification Criteria: Non-Recyclable Containers

Not all transparent plastic containers qualify as clear PET. The following items must be directed to mixed plastics or combustible bags:

- **Colored Beverage Bottles**: Green or brown beer and cider bottles are prohibited from the clear PET stream.
- **Disposable Takeout Cups**: Clear coffee cups are often molded from polystyrene (PS) or polylactic acid (PLA) bioplastics, which decompose into acids that ruin PET batch viscosity.
- **Egg Cartons and Berry Clamshells**: Molded food blister trays have high oil contamination and silicone release coatings that prevent clean fiber drawing.
