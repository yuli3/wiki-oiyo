A person walks toward a village, bent forward while carrying all ten wands in their arms. This card shows the weight of success carried alone, the overload that arrives just before completion.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 10 — completion under overload |
| Key words | Heavy responsibility, overwork, almost at the destination, sorting out burdens |

## Upright Meaning

The goal is in sight, but you are carrying too much. This is a time of achievement, and also a time to learn how to share the load and put some of it down before the next cycle begins.

- **Love**: You may be carrying the responsibilities of the relationship alone. Asking for shared responsibility is not weakness.
- **Work & Money**: Heavy workload and obligations you have taken on. Even if income has increased, if your quality of life is shrinking, it is time to change the structure.

## Reversed Meaning

The Ten of Wands reversed can show the edge of burnout, or the liberation that comes when you finally put the burden down. Some burdens were never yours in the first place.

## Today's Advice

> You can put down three of the ten wands and still reach the village. Decide which three they are.
