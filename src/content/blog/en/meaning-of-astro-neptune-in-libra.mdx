**The dream of harmony — a collective longing for relationship and peace.** Neptune is the planet of "what does our generation dream of, and what enchants it," and Libra makes that answer come out gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Neptune ♆ — dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries |
| Sign | Libra ♎ — balance, relationship, aesthetics |
| Element · Modality | Air · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 165 years (≈14 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Neptune governs **dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries**. When that energy passes through Libra, it shows up gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

This is why the same Neptune can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Libra's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a balancing sense that mediates conflict**
- **a refined eye for beauty**
- **natural negotiation skills**

## Watch out for

- long hesitation before a decision
- avoiding conflict hides true feelings

## How to read it as a generational placement

Neptune stays in one sign for years or decades, so Neptune in Libra is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Neptune is in Libra, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Neptune sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
