Usu (雨水) is the 2nd of the 24 solar terms. It arrives around February 18-20, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 330°.

## Meaning and Origin

Usu means "snow melts and becomes rain (雨水)." As the saying goes, "At Usu and Gyeongchip, the Daedong River thaws." It is a solar term of thawing, when frozen rivers loosen and wild geese return north.

## Customs and Foods

Farming households began preparing in earnest for the year's farming, and Usu was also considered a good time to make jang (醬), fermented sauces and pastes. People said jang made at Usu would not lose its flavor.

## Saju and the Solar Term

Usu is a mid-qi (中氣), marking the middle of the lunar month. In myeongnihak, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 section terms (節), while the mid-qi is read as the point when that month's energy becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Time

A time when what accumulated through winter melts and begins to flow — a fitting solar term for clearing away old burdens and old emotions.
