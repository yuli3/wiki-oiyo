Beneath the sun, two people stand facing each other, and above them the archangel Raphael spreads his wings in blessing. Behind the woman stands the Tree of Knowledge with the serpent, and behind the man stands the Tree of Life with flames. Beyond simple romance, The Lovers is a card of the fundamental choice: what will you unite with?

## Reading the Symbols

The angel's blessing symbolizes a destined connection beyond conscious calculation, the serpent and apple represent the responsibility that follows choice, and the two trees symbolize the mutual complementing of different natures.

## Upright Meaning

This is a rare time when the direction of the heart and the direction of the mind align. Whether in love or career, union with someone or something that shares your values creates powerful synergy.

- **Love**: One of the strongest love cards in the entire tarot. It means a fated meeting or decisive progress in a relationship.
- **Work & Study**: Favorable partnership and collaboration. Teamwork with colleagues who share your values produces results.
- **Money**: Possibility of joint investment or business partnership. However, it works only when trust is the foundation.

## Reversed Meaning

The Lovers reversed shows a fracture in union. It points to clashing values, an unbalanced relationship, or indecision between two options. It can also be a message to face the fact that you cannot have both.

## Card Combinations

- **The Lovers + The Hierophant**: The classic combination for marriage and official union.
- **The Lovers + The Devil**: A powerful but unhealthy attraction. Love and obsession must be distinguished.

## FAQ

**Q. How should I read The Lovers in a work question?**

A. Read it as "the choice that matches your values." If you are at a crossroads between two offers, it often points to the side your heart is drawn to, or the side where the people you will work with feel right.
