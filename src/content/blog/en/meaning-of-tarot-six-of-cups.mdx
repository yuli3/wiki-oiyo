A cup filled with flowers is being offered to a child. Six of Cups is warmth arriving from the past: memories, home, old connections, and a reunion with the innocence you thought you had lost.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 6 — Harmony and restoration |
| Key words | Memories, innocence, old connections, gifts of nostalgia |

## Upright Meaning

Good things from the past come to warm the present. A message from an old friend, a place full of memories, a childhood dream: this is a time when nostalgia becomes a gift rather than a burden.

- **Love**: The reappearance of an old connection, or pure feelings like first love. In questions about reunion, this is often read as a positive sign.
- **Work & Money**: Past experience and old networks become helpful. Financially, opportunities appear through old things, including secondhand goods, retro styles, and tradition.

## Reversed Meaning

This shows a state of being trapped in the past and unable to live in the present. Memory is a place to visit, not a place to live.

## Today's Advice

> What you should bring back from the past is not the person you were then, but the heart you had then.
