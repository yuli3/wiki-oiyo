Two people face each other as if making a vow, each holding up a cup. The Two of Cups is not one-sided but mutual — the moment when heart meets heart at the same level.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotion, relationships, intuition, love |
| Position | 2 — Balance and meeting |
| Key words | Mutual attraction, partnership, emotional exchange, reconciliation |

## Upright Meaning

A relationship with mutual understanding is forming. In love, friendship, or business partnership, balanced emotional exchange becomes possible. If there has been conflict, the way toward reconciliation opens.

- **Love**: Confirmation of mutual interest, the beginning of a relationship, reunion, and reconciliation. If you wondered, "Am I the only one who likes them?" the answer is no.
- **Work & Money**: A partnership or contract based on trust. Financially, a mutually beneficial deal is completed.

## Reversed Meaning

A relationship whose balance has been broken — a cup where only one side fills or only one side empties. It can also suggest a break caused by misunderstanding.

## Advice for Today

> Are you giving as much as you receive, and receiving as much as you give? Bring the cups to the same height.
