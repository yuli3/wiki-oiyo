**A mind that leaps by the big picture — stronger in principles than particulars.** Mercury is the planet of "how do I think and speak," and Sagittarius makes that answer come out far and free, toward a larger meaning.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thought, language, the way we communicate |
| Sign | Sagittarius ♐ — exploration, meaning, optimism |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Jupiter |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (frequent retrogrades) |

## Core tendency

Mercury governs **thought, language, the way we communicate**. When that energy passes through Sagittarius, it shows up far and free, toward a larger meaning.

This is why the same Mercury can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Sagittarius's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **an openness to set out anywhere**
- **an optimistic vision**
- **philosophical insight**

## Watch out for

- details and promises can be neglected
- frankness can sound careless

## How it shows up

- **Love**: mental rapport and the rhythm of conversation are central to attraction.
- **Work & study**: the Sagittarius way (far and free, toward a larger meaning) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mercury is in Sagittarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mercury sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
