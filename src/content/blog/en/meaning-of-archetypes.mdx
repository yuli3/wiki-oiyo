## 1. Origin: Humanity's Shared Memory

While analyzing his patients' dreams, Carl Jung discovered a surprising fact: images of ancient myths that they had never seen before appeared in their dreams. He concluded that this was not individual experience, but a heritage of the "Collective Unconscious" passed down through generations of human ancestors.

## 2. 12 Major Archetypes

Brand consultant Carol Pearson and others modernized Jung's theories to present 12 archetypes.

### ① Ego - Achieving Worldly Goals

- **Innocent:** Optimistic and pursues happiness. (e.g., Forrest Gump)
- **Orphan:** Realistic and wants a sense of belonging. (e.g., Pinocchio)
- **Hero:** Overcomes adversity and proves strength. (e.g., Mulan)
- **Caregiver:** Altruistic and protects others. (e.g., Mother Teresa)

### ② Soul - Pursuing Meaning and Spirituality

- **Explorer:** Sets out in search of freedom and self-actualization. (e.g., Indiana Jones)
- **Rebel:** Destroys outdated rules and dreams of revolution. (e.g., Robin Hood)
- **Lover:** Pursues intimacy, passion, and beauty. (e.g., Romeo and Juliet)
- **Creator:** Exercises imagination to create new value. (e.g., Steve Jobs)

### ③ Self - Order and Integration

- **Jester:** Speaks truth with humor and brings joy. (e.g., Charlie Chaplin)
- **Sage:** Explores wisdom and truth. (e.g., Yoda)
- **Magician:** Creates change and presents a vision. (e.g., Gandalf)
- **Ruler:** Leads organizations responsibly and creates prosperity. (e.g., Mufasa)

## 3. Hero's Journey

Mythologist Joseph Campbell stated that all archetypal stories follow a single pattern.

1.  **Call:** Invited to leave the ordinary and embark on an adventure.
2.  **Ordeal:** Fights monsters and overcomes crises of death.
3.  **Reward:** Obtains treasure (enlightenment) and returns.
    Your life is also on this journey. Which stage are you at right now?

## 4. Shadow

Every archetype has a dark side.

- When a hero falls, they fall into **arrogance**.
- When a caregiver falls, they **suffocate** others (victim cosplay).
- When a ruler falls, they become a **tyrant**.
  True growth happens when you do not suppress the shadow, but acknowledge and integrate it.

## 5. Conclusion: Write Your Myth

To the question "Who am I?", archetypes answer, "You are a hero, a sage, and also a magician."
Archetypes are not fixed. Take out and wear the mask you need depending on the situation. Like a ruler at work, like a lover in front of your partner, like a sage when you are alone. You are an actor with a thousand faces.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is there a best archetype?**
A: No. The archetype that fits the situation is the best archetype. A hero is needed on the battlefield, but in a peaceful home, a caregiver is needed more than a hero.

**Q: Is it the same as what's used in brand marketing?**
A: That's right. Great brands like Nike (Hero), Disney (Magician), and Harley-Davidson (Rebel) all clearly express specific archetypes to stimulate people's unconscious.
