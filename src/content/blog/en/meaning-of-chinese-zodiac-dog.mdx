<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **Dog** is the eleventh animal in the Chinese zodiac and is paired with
  the Earthly Branch **Xu**. It is a symbolic and cultural marker in a repeating
  calendar cycle, not a deterministic personality type or fortune predictor.
</HighlightBox>

## Cultural associations and traditional traits

In East Asian zodiac folklore, the Dog is commonly associated with loyalty,
protection, sincerity, watchfulness, justice, and dependable companionship.
These associations draw on the animal's long-standing role as a guardian and
social companion in human life.

Popular descriptions may call Dog-year people honest, faithful, cautious,
principled, protective, skeptical, or service-minded. These traits are
traditional symbolic themes, not evidence about any individual person's
character. The Dog sign can be meaningful as cultural language, but it should
not be used to decide whether someone is trustworthy, pessimistic, lucky,
unlucky, or suited for a particular life path.

## Earthly Branch and cycle position

The Dog corresponds to **Xu**, the eleventh of the 12 Earthly Branches. It
follows Rooster and precedes Pig in the animal sequence. In the traditional
calendar, the animal label is the familiar public form of the branch, while the
full year name also includes one of the 10 Heavenly Stems.

Representative Dog-year labels include **1982, 1994, 2006, and 2018**. These
are common Gregorian-year references. People born in January or early February
should verify the relevant calendar boundary, because zodiac years may be
counted from Lunar New Year in popular use or from solar terms in Four Pillars
practice.

## Compatibility folklore

Compatibility folklore often groups the Dog with **Tiger** and **Horse** in a
harmonious triad. Dog and **Dragon** are commonly described as a clashing pair.
These pairings are symbolic classifications within traditional matching
language, not evidence that relationships must work or fail. Real compatibility
comes from people, behavior, values, and choices.

## Common Misunderstanding

The main misunderstanding is that the Dog sign determines a person's loyalty,
morality, caution, relationship style, or destiny. It does not. A birth-year
animal is a broad cultural label. Full East Asian fortune reading uses the Four
Pillars, or saju, which considers year, month, day, hour, Heavenly Stems,
Earthly Branches, and interpretive relationships. See the broader
[Chinese zodiac definition](/en/meaning-of-chinese-zodiac/) and the related
[Palja definition](/en/meaning-of-palja/) for that larger structure.

## Responsible use

Dog symbolism belongs to cultural tradition, reflection, and entertainment. It
should not be used as deterministic prediction, evidence about personality, or
a basis for judging someone's future.
