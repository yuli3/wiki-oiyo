A figure on a castle wall holds a globe in one hand and looks out over the distant sea. The first achievement is already in hand, and now the decision is whether to move into a wider world — this is the strategist's card.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Position | 2 — Choice and perspective |
| Key words | Planning, a view of the wider world, designing the next stage |

## Upright Meaning

This is a time to plan between your current safe position and a larger possibility. It is ideal for widening your view and drawing a long-term roadmap.

- **Love**: A time to think about the relationship's next stage. Conversation about the future lifts the relationship to another level.
- **Work & Money**: Reviewing a larger stage such as expansion, overseas opportunities, or a career move. Financially, it is a good time to create an investment plan.

## Reversed Meaning

A state of only making plans without leaving, or a decision trapped in a narrow field of vision. Check whether the safety inside the wall is preventing growth.

## Advice for Today

> Drawing the map and setting out on the road are different things. Choose a departure date.
