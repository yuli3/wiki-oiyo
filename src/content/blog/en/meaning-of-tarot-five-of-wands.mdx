Five young people are tangled together, each holding a staff. But if you look closely, no one is injured — this is not war but contest, the friction that trains everyone involved.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Rank | 5 — competition and friction |
| Key words | Competition, clashing opinions, constructive conflict, practice match |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period of active competition and clashing opinions. It may look like a draining struggle, but ideas are refined and ability is revealed through the process. Compete fairly.

- **Love**: Push-and-pull dynamics and the appearance of a rival. The way conflict is handled, more than the conflict itself, shows the level of the relationship.
- **Work & Money**: Competitive bids, exams, or internal competition. Financially, watch for increased costs caused by competition.

## Reversed Meaning

Problems may fester because conflict is avoided, or you may be trapped in a meaningless drain of energy. First ask whether this fight is worth fighting.

## Today's Advice

> If you try to learn rather than win, all this friction becomes training with no tuition fee.
