Sul (戌) represents the fields after the harvest and the Dog that guards the house at night. As earth (火庫, fire storage) that harbors the embers of a hearth (Ding fire), it embodies the energy of a guardian who protects and brings things to completion.

## Personality Manifestation

Those with a strong presence of Sul-earth are loyal and possess a strong sense of responsibility; they remain faithful to the people and organizations they trust until the end. They are naturally vigilant and do not open up easily, but once someone is considered "on their side," they become the most reliable ally.

## San-hap (Triad) and Chung (Clash)

It forms a Fire-frame (火局) through the In-O-Sul (寅午戌) triad. It clashes with Jin (辰) in the Jin-Sul-Chung (辰戌沖)—a collision between the ideal (Dragon) and the guardian (Dog), often signifying changes in large organizations or belief systems.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Why is Sul-earth called a "lonely character"?**

A. Because those who guard must stand at the perimeter rather than within the group. The habit of shouldering responsibilities alone can lead to feelings of loneliness; however, if one learns to entrust part of the perimeter to reliable colleagues, this guardian's gravity transforms into true charisma.

*In Saju, Earthly Branches represent 'time and environment.' The manifestation of the same character varies depending on whether it is located in the Year, Month, Day, or Hour pillar, and how it interacts with surrounding characters through combinations (hap) and clashes (chung).*
