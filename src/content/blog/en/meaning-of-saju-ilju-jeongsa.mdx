The Jeong-Sa (丁巳) pillar is the 54th in the sexagenary cycle. It consists of the Heavenly Stem Jeong (丁), a Yin Fire representing a candle illuminating the darkness, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Sa (巳), the snake of insight. It is the image of a torch that does not extinguish even in the wind—a manifestation of concentrated passion.

## Personality and Temperament

The Jeong-Sa pillar is a competitive type with intense focus and passion. Because it sits in the position of the "Emperor" (帝旺), it hides an explosive power that belies its nature as Yin Fire.

- **Strengths**: A laser-like focus and intense, burning passion.
- **Points of Caution**: Once it ignites, it burns until it is exhausted. For a torch, managing its "fuel" is essential.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Jeong (丁) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-jeong/) sits atop the [Sa (巳) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/). Based on the main energy (Qi) of the branch, the Ten God is [Rob Wealth (劫財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-geopjae/), and the Twelve Stages of Life is the [Emperor (帝旺)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jewang/). Rob Wealth is the star of competition and ambition; it thrives in competitive environments but also brings fluctuations in wealth. As it occupies the position of the Emperor, it is a strong and prosperous structure where energy reaches its peak.

In terms of Nabeum (phonetic element), it belongs to Sa-Jung-To (沙中土), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same phonetic energy.
