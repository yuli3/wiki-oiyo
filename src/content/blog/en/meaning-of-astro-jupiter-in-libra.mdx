**Luck that grows between people — partnership is the engine of growth.** Jupiter is the planet of "where do I find growth and meaning," and Libra makes that answer come out gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter ♃ — expansion, luck, the direction of belief |
| Sign | Libra ♎ — balance, relationship, aesthetics |
| Element · Modality | Air · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 12 years (≈1 year per sign) |

## Core tendency

Jupiter governs **expansion, luck, the direction of belief**. When that energy passes through Libra, it shows up gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

This is why the same Jupiter can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Libra's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a balancing sense that mediates conflict**
- **a refined eye for beauty**
- **natural negotiation skills**

## Watch out for

- long hesitation before a decision
- avoiding conflict hides true feelings

## Growth and task

Jupiter changes sign on roughly a 12-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Jupiter in Libra, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Jupiter is in Libra, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Jupiter sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
