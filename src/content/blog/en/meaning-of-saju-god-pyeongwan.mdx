Pyeongwan (偏官), also known as Chilsal (七殺), is an energy that exerts strong pressure on the self. It is the most dramatic character in the Sipsin system—if you can handle it, it makes you a hero; if you cannot, it suppresses you.

## At a Glance

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Definition | The element that exerts control (剋) over the self with the same Yin-Yang polarity |
| Governed Realms | Challenge, Charisma, Pressure, Honor |
| Key Keywords | Decisiveness, Overcoming crises, Charisma, Responsibility |

## Personality Manifestation

Those with a prominent Pyeongwan remain calm in crises and often grow stronger in harsh environments. They are strict with themselves and others, possessing a sense of chivalry that drives them to spare no effort for what they believe is right.

## In Work and Career

Positions involving high pressure and responsibility—such as the military, police, firefighting, prosecution, emergency medicine, corporate restructuring, and venture business—are the stages where Pyeongwan thrives.

## In Wealth

Because wealth follows honor and authority in this structure, the position or title comes before direct financial management (理財).

## When Excessive or Deficient

Excessive Pyeongwan leads to chronic tension, burnout, and an overbearing attitude. It requires management through Siksang (expression/hobbies) and Inseong (rest/study) to dissipate the 'Sal' (killing energy).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does a strong Pyeongwan mean a difficult life?**

A. It simply means the ocean has larger waves. Just as the same wave is a stage for a surfer, Pyeongwan grants those who are tempered a breakthrough ability and charisma that others lack. It is not the amount of hardship, but the degree of tempering that determines one's destiny.
