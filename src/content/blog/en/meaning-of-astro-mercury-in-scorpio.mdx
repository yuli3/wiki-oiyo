**An investigator's mind — the questions go straight to hidden motives.** Mercury is the planet of "how do I think and speak," and Scorpio makes that answer come out deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thought, language, the way we communicate |
| Sign | Scorpio ♏ — intensity, insight, regeneration |
| Element · Modality | Water · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Pluto |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (frequent retrogrades) |

## Core tendency

Mercury governs **thought, language, the way we communicate**. When that energy passes through Scorpio, it shows up deeply and intensely, all the way to the end.

This is why the same Mercury can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Scorpio's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **insight that pierces to the essence**
- **all-in immersion**
- **the power to rebuild after collapse**

## Watch out for

- suspicion and control tighten relationships
- feelings hidden too long erupt

## How it shows up

- **Love**: mental rapport and the rhythm of conversation are central to attraction.
- **Work & study**: the Scorpio way (deeply and intensely, all the way to the end) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mercury is in Scorpio, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mercury sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
