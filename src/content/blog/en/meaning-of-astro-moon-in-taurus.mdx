**One of the Moon's most comfortable seats — stability and a good meal are medicine for the heart.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Taurus makes that answer come out slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Taurus ♉ — stability, the senses, accumulation |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Taurus, it shows up slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Taurus's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the stamina to see things through**
- **a grounded feel for real resources**
- **unshakeable calm**

## Watch out for

- change itself can feel like a threat
- stubbornness can block flexibility

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Taurus way (slowly but surely, savoring every sense) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you have a strong instinct for accumulation and tangible assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Taurus, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
