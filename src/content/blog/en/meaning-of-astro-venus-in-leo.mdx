**Love as a festival — bright expression and unmistakable specialness are desired.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Sun |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Leo way (boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
