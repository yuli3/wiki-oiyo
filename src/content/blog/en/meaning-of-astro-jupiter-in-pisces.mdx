**Jupiter's old home — the more generously it gives, the wider the mysterious math becomes.** Jupiter is the planet of "where do I find growth and meaning," and Pisces makes that answer come out boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter ♃ — expansion, luck, the direction of belief |
| Sign | Pisces ♓ — empathy, imagination, transcendence |
| Element · Modality | Water · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Neptune |
| Orbital cycle | about 12 years (≈1 year per sign) |

## Core tendency

Jupiter governs **expansion, luck, the direction of belief**. When that energy passes through Pisces, it shows up boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

This is why the same Jupiter can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Pisces's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **empathy that feels others' emotions as one's own**
- **imagination that pictures beyond the real**
- **unconditional acceptance**

## Watch out for

- blurred boundaries lead to burnout
- escapism becomes a sweet trap

## Growth and task

Jupiter changes sign on roughly a 12-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Jupiter in Pisces, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Jupiter is in Pisces, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Jupiter sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
