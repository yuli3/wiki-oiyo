Inside a church, a knight lies on a tomb with hands folded. This is not death but rest: a sacred pause in which armor is removed and the mind is restored before the next battle.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 4 — Stability and rest |
| Key words | Rest, recovery, strategic retreat, recharging |

## Upright Meaning

It is time to stop fighting for a while and recover. Rest is not laziness but strategy. The more fully you pause now, the clearer your judgment will be in the next phase.

- **Love**: A cool-down period in the relationship. Distance may be maintenance, not separation.
- **Work & Money**: A vacation or recharge is needed. Financially, this is also a pause for review rather than attack.

## Reversed Meaning

This can show the compulsion of needing rest but being unable to take it, or the stagnation of having recovered but refusing to get up. Even rest has a graduation point.

## Today's Advice

> Give yourself fifteen minutes today with no information coming in.
