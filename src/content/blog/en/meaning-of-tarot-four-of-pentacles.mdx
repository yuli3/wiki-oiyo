One pentacle rests on the crown, one at the chest, and two beneath the feet: a figure clutches all four with his whole body. Stability has been achieved, but both hands are occupied by holding on.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Pentacles — Earth element, material matters, money, the body, practical achievement |
| Rank | 4 — Stability and holding on |
| Key words | Possession, securing stability, stinginess, resistance to change |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when financial stability and ownership are being secured. The power to protect what you have is a virtue, but remember that a clenched hand cannot receive anything new.

- **Love**: A desire to control the relationship out of fear of losing it. Notice the boundary between stability and possessiveness.
- **Work & Money**: Conservative management is favored now. Financially, you are strong at saving and defense, but excessive frugality can become an opportunity cost.

## Reversed Meaning

There are two ways to open the hand: becoming more generous, or losing what you held too tightly. It is time to redefine your relationship with money and control.

## Today's Advice

> Instead of listing what you have, write down what each thing is for.
