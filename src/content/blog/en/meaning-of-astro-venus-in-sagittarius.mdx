**Love that leaves together — it settles with someone who respects freedom.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Sagittarius makes that answer come out far and free, toward a larger meaning.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Sagittarius ♐ — exploration, meaning, optimism |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Jupiter |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Sagittarius, it shows up far and free, toward a larger meaning.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Sagittarius's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **an openness to set out anywhere**
- **an optimistic vision**
- **philosophical insight**

## Watch out for

- details and promises can be neglected
- frankness can sound careless

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Sagittarius way (far and free, toward a larger meaning) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Sagittarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
