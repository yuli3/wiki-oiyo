## Definition

The rising sign is the zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. It is also called the **Ascendant**.

If the Sun sign points to core identity, the rising sign is often used to describe first impression, response style, and the social surface of personality.

## Core Structure

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Birth time | The most important input for calculating the rising sign |
| Birth place | Sets the horizon reference |
| First impression | Shows through tone, expression, and approach |
| Life doorway | Symbolizes how the person meets the outer world |

## Difference From Sun Sign

The Sun sign is closer to “who I am.” The rising sign is closer to “how I enter situations.”

This is why two people with the same Sun sign can feel quite different if their rising signs differ.

## Careful Use

The rising sign can change significantly without an accurate birth time. If the birth time is uncertain, interpretation should stay flexible.
