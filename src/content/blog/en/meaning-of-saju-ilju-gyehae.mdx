The Gye-Hae (癸亥) Ilju is the 60th pillar of the sexagenary cycle. It is a combination where the Heavenly Stem Gye (Yin Water), representing gentle, permeating rainwater, sits upon the Earthly Branch Hae (Pig), representing abundance. It is the endless ocean where all rivers converge—the final pillar of the sexagenary cycle, representing return and inclusivity.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gye-Hae Ilju is a sage-like type with infinite imagination and inclusivity. As the end of the sexagenary cycle and the precursor to a new one, they possess a mental world of unfathomable depth.

- **Strengths**: Infinite imagination and an inclusive nature that accepts everything.
- **Points of Caution**: They are often perceived as unpredictable due to the depth of their thoughts. Like the ocean, their depth requires a lighthouse to be understood.

## Reading the Structure — Myeongri Interpretation

With the [Gye (癸) Day Master](/en/meaning-of-saju-stem-gye/) sitting on the [Hae (亥) Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-hae/), the 10 God based on the main qi of the Day Branch is [Geopjae (劫財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-geopjae/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Jewang (帝旺)](/en/meaning-of-saju-stage-jewang/). Geopjae is the star of competition and ambition—it thrives in competitive environments but also brings fluctuations in wealth. As it sits in the position of Jewang, it is a strong, vigorous structure where energy reaches its peak.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Daehaesu (Great Ocean Water), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
