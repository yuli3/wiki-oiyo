A figure sits with arms crossed, smiling in satisfaction, nine cups arranged behind them like a screen. Known as the "wish card," the Nine of Cups is the peak of emotional abundance, when what you hoped for comes true.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 9 — satisfaction after fulfillment |
| Key words | Wish fulfillment, contentment, abundance, self-satisfaction |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when the wish you hoped for can be fulfilled. Both material and emotional life become abundant, and you feel satisfaction with your own life. Enjoy the goodness of the present fully.

- **Love**: The fulfillment of the relationship you wanted, or a satisfying romance. Even if you are alone, you are in such a fulfilled state that you become more attractive.
- **Work & Money**: Goal achievement and reward. Financially, you gain room to enjoy yourself, but watch the endless thirst that keeps wanting nine cups to become ten.

## Reversed Meaning

You may have achieved something but still feel empty, or self-satisfaction may have slipped into arrogance. The issue may not be how many cups you have, but the absence of someone to share them with.

## Today's Advice

> Find one wish that has already come true today and savor it. Satisfaction is also a practice.
