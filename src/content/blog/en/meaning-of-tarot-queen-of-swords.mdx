A queen sits on a throne above the clouds, holding a sword upright and extending one hand. The Queen of Swords is a person made clear by passing through sorrow — a blade of wisdom that is not fooled by sentiment, yet has not lost the heart.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | Queen — the one who holds |
| Key words | Clarity, independence, wisdom from experience, boundary setting |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period when clear judgment and honest communication shine. Set boundaries clearly and speak from facts. Your honesty creates respect.

- **Love**: A mature and independent view of relationships. This is a time when honest words work better than sweet ones.
- **Work & Money**: Favorable signs for roles where discernment is a weapon, such as evaluation, review, and editing. Financially, you can make cool-headed decisions without emotional interference.

## Reversed Meaning

Sharpness may harden into coldness, and boundaries may turn into disconnection. Hold the sword, but do not withdraw the outstretched hand.
