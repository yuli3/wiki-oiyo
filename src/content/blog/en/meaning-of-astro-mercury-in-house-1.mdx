**A mind where thought immediately becomes action — your way of speaking is your calling card.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet acts. When Mercury is in the 1st house, the question "How do I think and speak?" unfolds in the realm of **first impressions, the body, and how one begins**.

## How This Placement Works

The 1st house deals with first impressions, the body, and how one begins. When Mercury enters this house, the energy of **thought, language, and communication style** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the most visibly expressed places in a chart — this placement is easy for other people to notice.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of first impressions, the body, and how one begins receives Mercury's gift: **clarity**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Aries, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in more detail.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mercury in the 1st house is **overconcentration on that stage** — balance is needed so that first impressions, the body, and how one begins do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed(the style), while houses show **where** it unfolds(the stage). With Mercury in the 1st house, the stage is first impressions, the body, and how one begins, while the acting tone is set by the sign where Mercury is located.

**Q. What do I need to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
