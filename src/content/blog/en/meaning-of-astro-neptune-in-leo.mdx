**The dream of romance — love and creation are treated almost as faith.** Neptune is the planet of "what does our generation dream of, and what enchants it," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Neptune ♆ — dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Sun |
| Orbital cycle | about 165 years (≈14 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Neptune governs **dreams, ideals, the dissolving of boundaries**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Neptune can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## How to read it as a generational placement

Neptune stays in one sign for years or decades, so Neptune in Leo is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Neptune is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Neptune sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
