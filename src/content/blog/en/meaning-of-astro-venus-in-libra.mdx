**Venus's other home — an aesthete of relationship who understands the balance of together.** Venus is the planet of "what do I find beautiful, and how do I love," and Libra makes that answer come out gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Venus ♀ — love, beauty, the style of attraction |
| Sign | Libra ♎ — balance, relationship, aesthetics |
| Element · Modality | Air · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Venus |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year |

## Core tendency

Venus governs **love, beauty, the style of attraction**. When that energy passes through Libra, it shows up gracefully and fairly, in step with the other.

This is why the same Venus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Libra's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a balancing sense that mediates conflict**
- **a refined eye for beauty**
- **natural negotiation skills**

## Watch out for

- long hesitation before a decision
- avoiding conflict hides true feelings

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you give and receive affection in this placement's own aesthetic language.
- **Work & study**: the Libra way (gracefully and fairly, in step with the other) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Venus is in Libra, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Venus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
