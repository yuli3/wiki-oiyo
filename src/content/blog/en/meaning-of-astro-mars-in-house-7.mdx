**Mars ignites through relationships — this placement learns not to avoid conflict, but to handle it.** A house is the **life stage** where a planet acts. When Mars is in the 7th house, the question "What do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the realm of **partnerships, marriage, and contracts**.

## How This Placement Works

The 7th house deals with partnerships, marriage, and contracts. When Mars enters this house, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the places in a chart where expression is most visible — this placement is often noticeable to the people around the native.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of partnerships, marriage, and contracts receives Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Libra, can make the atmosphere of this stage easier to read in a more layered way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 7th house is **over-concentration on this stage** — balance is needed so that partnerships, marriage, and contracts do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What is the difference between a house and a sign?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed, its style, while a house shows **where** it unfolds, its stage. With Mars in the 7th house, the stage is partnerships, marriage, and contracts, while the acting tone is set by the sign Mars occupies.

**Q. What do I need in order to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by the **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
