If the Major Arcana shows the large currents of life, the **56 Minor Arcana cards** illuminate the specific
scenes of everyday life. Each of the four suits corresponds to one element and one area of life.

| Suit | Element | Area of Life |
|---|---|---|
| Wands | Fire | Passion, work, creation, will |
| Cups | Water | Emotion, relationships, intuition, love |
| Swords | Air | Thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Pentacles | Earth | Matter, money, the body, achievement |

The numbers are stages of a journey: from the Ace (the seed) to the Ten (completion). The four court cards,
Page, Knight, Queen, and King, show the maturity of a person who handles that element.
