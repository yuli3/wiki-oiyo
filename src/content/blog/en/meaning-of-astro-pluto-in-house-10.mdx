**The Ladder of Power — Learning the essence of influence in your career.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Pluto enters the 10th house, the question "Where do I go to the very bottom and be reborn?" unfolds in the realms of **career, reputation, and social calling.**

## How This Placement Works

The 10th house deals with career, reputation, and social calling. When Pluto enters this house, the energy of **transformation, power, and rebirth** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When Manifested Positively

When this placement functions healthily, the realms of career, reputation, and social calling receive the gift of Pluto (**regenerative power**). By referring to the nature of its natural sign, Capricorn, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Pluto in the 10th house is **becoming overly obsessed with that stage** — balance is needed so that the areas of career, reputation, and social calling do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Pluto in the 10th house, the stage is your career, reputation, and social calling, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Pluto is located.

**Q. What is needed to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **birth time and location**. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
