Gye (癸) is the final character of the ten Heavenly Stems; it represents the drizzle falling from the sky and the spring water flowing from between rocks. While it is the smallest and softest form of water, it is this fine rain, rather than a torrential downpour, that awakens the seeds of all things.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gye Water Day Master is characterized by rich sensitivity and highly developed intuition; they quietly permeate their surroundings and read the hearts of others. While they possess a pure and delicate side, they also harbor an invisible tenacity, much like dripping water that eventually wears away stone. It is a character of imagination and inspiration.

- **Strengths**: Empathy, intuition, and creative inspiration. The power to gently permeate and move hearts and organizations.
- **Points of Caution**: When emotional dampness accumulates, it can lead to deep melancholy and anxiety. Be wary of a tendency to lose touch with reality or to become overly secretive.

## Suitable Fields

Fields where inspiration and empathy are central, such as art and creation, psychology and counseling, religion and philosophy, research, and planning.

## Relationships with Other Characters

With Wu (戊, Mountain), it forms the Wu-Gye Combination (무계합) — it becomes the sweet rain that moistens a dry mountain. When it meets Bing (丙, Sun), a rainbow may appear, but it can also create a situation where mist obscures the sun.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is the Gye Day Master considered a "weak" chart?**

A. The softest water permeates the deepest. The power of Gye lies not in collision, but in infiltration — a method of changing hearts and situations over time. In Saju (Four Pillars), Gye is regarded as the "rain that sprouts seeds," a precious character that provides the nourishment for new beginnings.
