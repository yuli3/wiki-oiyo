Jeol (絶) is the tenth stage of the Sibiunseong (Twelve Stages of Life), representing the point where energy is completely severed. However, because the time in Saju (Four Pillars) is cyclical, Jeol is both an end and **the state of 'nothingness' (無) just before new life is conceived**—it is the most dramatic star of transition in the Sibiunseong.

## At a Glance

## Meaning and Symbolism

Jeol carries the energy of a blank slate. Because it has no fixed form, it can go anywhere and be reborn as anything. This is why it is said that those with the star of Jeol experience many ups and downs in life; at the same time, the power to rise again from the bottom also belongs to this star.

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in Jeol

Those who have Jeol in their Ilji (Day Branch) possess highly developed sensitivity and intuition, allowing them to perceive changes in atmosphere faster than anyone else. They adapt quickly even when their environment changes and do not fear transitioning into completely different fields.

- **Strengths**: Exceptional adaptability and intuition, the courage to start from a blank slate, and the ability to recover from crises.
- **Points of Caution**: They can be easily swayed and often change their minds. The prescription for Jeol is to avoid making major decisions alone and instead seek verification from trusted individuals.

## Encountering Jeol in Daewoon or Sewoon

The luck of Jeol is a period when one cycle completely closes and a new one opens. While it is easy for ruptures such as moving, changing jobs, or breakups to occur, that empty space becomes the very place for a new beginning. Rather than trying to prevent change, it is better to focus your energy on choosing the right direction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Does having Jeol mean my life will be unstable?**

A. Frequent change is not the same as being unfortunate. Those who utilize the energy of Jeol well can achieve multiple transformations that others might not experience in a lifetime, and this becomes their competitive edge.
