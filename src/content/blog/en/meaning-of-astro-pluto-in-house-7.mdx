**The seat of fated relationships — partnership is the crucible of transformation.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Pluto enters the 7th house, the question, "Where do I descend to the depths and be reborn?" unfolds in the realm of **partnerships, marriage, and contracts**.

## At a Glance

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Planet | Pluto ♇ — Transformation, Power, Rebirth |
| House | 7th House · House of Relationships — One-on-one relationships, cooperation, the mirror |

## How This Placement Works

The 7th house deals with partnerships, marriage, and contracts. When Pluto enters this house, the energy of **transformation, power, and rebirth** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When Manifested Positively

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of partnership, marriage, and contracts receive the gift of Pluto (**regenerative power**). Referring to the nature of its natural sign, Libra, allows for a more multidimensional reading of the atmosphere of this stage.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Pluto in the 7th house is **becoming overly fixated on that stage** — balance is required so that the realms of partnership, marriage, and contracts do not become the entirety of your life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How do houses differ from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Pluto in the 7th house, the stage is partnership, marriage, and contracts, and the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Pluto is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation decreases significantly.
