Beneath a tree, a figure sits with arms crossed. Three cups stand before him, while a hand from a cloud offers a fourth cup he does not see. This is an emotional standstill: so accustomed to what he already has that he cannot notice what is new.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotion, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | 4 — Boredom within stability |
| Key words | Boredom, apathy, inward withdrawal, missed opportunity |

## Upright Meaning

This is a period of boredom and low energy. Yet this inward sinking can also become a filtering process that helps you recognize what you truly want. Just be careful not to miss the one opportunity already in front of you.

- **Love**: A stagnant phase in a relationship, or a state of not noticing affection that is coming toward you. Look inside for the source of your indifference.
- **Work & Money**: A rut in which work has lost its meaning. Even when a financial offer arrives, you may feel unmoved. Check whether the problem is the terms or your own motivation.

## Reversed Meaning

This is a sign of recovery: you are emerging from stagnation and turning your eyes back toward the world. It is time to look again at the cup you once refused.

## Today's Advice

> It only takes one lift of the head to see what is inside the fourth cup.
