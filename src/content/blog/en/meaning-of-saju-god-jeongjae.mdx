Jeongjae (正財) means "Rightful Wealth"—it symbolizes the steady accumulation of income, savings, and credit earned through labor and time. It is the foundation of wealth that, while not flashy, is unshakable.

## At a Glance

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Definition | The element I control (剋) with a different Yin-Yang polarity |
| Governed Areas | Fixed wealth, diligence, stability, frugality |
| Key Keywords | Diligence, meticulous management, stable assets, credit |

## Personality Manifestation

Individuals with a prominent Jeongjae are diligent, frugal, and dislike unplanned expenditures. They excel at bookkeeping, organization, and management, and are reliable people who always keep their promises.

## Career and Work

It is the representative Sipsin for fields where precision and consistency are rewarded, such as accounting, finance, administration, banking, and management.

## Wealth

This structure makes the best use of savings and the power of compound interest. However, the only risk is missing out on opportunities due to an excessive focus on safety.

## Excess and Deficiency

When in excess, one may become stingy with money or overly sensitive to minor losses. It can suppress Inseong (study/rest), leading to a "burnout" type where one only earns but fails to replenish their energy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is it difficult to become wealthy if I only have Jeongjae?**

A. While the pace may be slow, the certainty is the highest. Statistically, the royal road to asset formation is steady income plus a high savings rate. The modern optimal solution is to add a spoonful of Pyeonjae-style management (small-scale investment) on top of the stability of Jeongjae.

*Sipsin is a language that interprets the relationship between other elements based on the Ilgan (the Self). The manifestation of the same Sipsin varies depending on its position, strength, and interactions (Hap/Chung); there is no such thing as a "good" or "bad" Sipsin.*
