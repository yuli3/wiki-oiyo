A hand emerging from the clouds holds a chalice overflowing with five streams of water, while a dove descends carrying the host. The Ace of Cups is the moment when the heart first opens — the source from which love, empathy, and inspiration begin to flow.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotions, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | Ace — the seed of a beginning |
| Key words | Emotional opening, new love, a full heart |

## Upright Meaning

New feelings are beginning. Love, friendship, creative inspiration, spiritual fulfillment — the cup of the heart is filling, so do not hoard what overflows. Share it.

- **Love**: The beginning of new love, a confession, a moment of emotional connection. This is one of the strongest positive love cards in tarot.
- **Work & Money**: Inspiration surges in work your heart is drawn to. Satisfaction fills first, and money follows after it.

## Reversed Meaning

The heart is closed, or emotion is leaking away. Before trying to fill the cup, look first for the cracked places — the unresolved feelings.

## Today's Advice

> Express one good feeling you had today to one person. A cup is filled again when it is shared.
