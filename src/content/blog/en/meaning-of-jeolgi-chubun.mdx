Chubun (秋分) is the 16th of the 24 Jeolgi. It falls around September 22-24, when the sun reaches 180° of ecliptic longitude.

## Meaning and Origins

Chubun is the day when the sun crosses the equator again and the lengths of night and day become equal. From this day onward, the nights begin to grow longer — it is the threshold into the season of gathering and storing.

## Customs and Foods

In the fields, rice harvesting begins, and mushrooms come into season. In old royal courts, rites were also offered to the Star of Longevity (老人星) to pray for long life.

## Saju and the Jeolgi

Chubun marks the mid-qi (中氣), the middle point of the month. In Myeongri, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 seasonal nodes (節), while the mid-qi is read as the time when that month's energy becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Time of Year

If Chunbun is the balance of beginning, Chubun is the balance of gathering — count this year's harvest and look for what deserves gratitude.
