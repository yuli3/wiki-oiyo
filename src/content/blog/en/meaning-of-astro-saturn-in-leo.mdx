**A lesson in standing without applause — self-worth beyond recognition is the task.** Saturn is the planet of "what tests make me stronger," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Saturn ♄ — limits, responsibility, the task of mastery |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Sun |
| Orbital cycle | about 29 years (≈2.5 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Saturn governs **limits, responsibility, the task of mastery**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Saturn can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## Growth and task

Saturn changes sign on roughly a 29-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Saturn in Leo, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Saturn is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Saturn sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
