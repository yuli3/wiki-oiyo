Soseol (小雪) is the 20th of the 24 solar terms, falling around November 21-23 when the sun reaches celestial longitude 240°.

## Meaning and Origin

Soseol means "small (小) snow (雪)" and marks the solar term when the first snow begins to fall. As in the proverb "Soseol cold should come even if one must borrow money for it," people believed that a brief spell of cold around this time helped barley farming succeed.

## Customs and Foods

This is a busy time for putting away winter side dishes: tying and drying dried radish greens, preparing dried radish strips, and drying pumpkin slices for later use.

## Saju and Solar Terms

Soseol is a mid-term (中氣), marking the middle of the month. In myeongli, the boundary of the month pillar (月柱) is based on the 12 seasonal nodes (節), while the mid-term is read as the point when that month's qi becomes most concentrated.

## Wisdom for This Time

Just as winter food is stored and preserved, this solar term calls for the wisdom to conserve and allocate the energy needed through the end of the year.
