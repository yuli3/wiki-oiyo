**Someone who shines through stories — words and ideas are your identity.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Gemini makes that answer come out lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Gemini ♊ — curiosity, language, connection |
| Element · Modality | Air · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Mercury |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Gemini, it shows up lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Gemini's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **fast learning and a gift for explaining**
- **sociability that works anywhere**
- **a knack for linking situations**

## Watch out for

- scattered attention can stay shallow
- words can outrun the heart

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Gemini way (lightly and wittily, along several tracks at once) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: information and networks themselves become your assets.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Gemini, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
