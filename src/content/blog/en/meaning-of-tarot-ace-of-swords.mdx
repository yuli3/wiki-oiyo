A hand emerging from the clouds holds a sword upright, piercing a crown. The Ace of Swords is a single line of clarity cutting through fog — the moment when truth is revealed and judgment becomes possible.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | Ace — the seed of a beginning |
| Key words | Clear truth, breakthrough, decision, intellectual awakening |

## Upright Meaning

The core of a tangled problem begins to come into view. This is the best time to make a decision and state it clearly. Truth may be sharp, but it opens a path.

- **Love**: A conversation that faces the truth of the relationship. A vague connection becomes clear.
- **Work & Money**: A breakthrough idea, a clear strategy. In contracts and negotiations, your eye for reading the terms accurately is alive.

## Reversed Meaning

Judgment may be clouded, or truth may be avoided. If you grip the sword the wrong way, you cut yourself — be especially careful with how you use words.

## Today's Advice

> Define today's problem in one sentence. The moment it is defined, half of it is already solved.
