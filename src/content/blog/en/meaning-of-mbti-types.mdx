This page brings the 16 MBTI personality types together in one place. Review each type's nickname and core traits, then click any type to move to a detailed dictionary entry covering strengths, weaknesses, careers, and relationships. The types are divided into four temperament groups — Analysts (NT), Diplomats (NF), Sentinels (SJ), and Explorers (SP).

## Analysts (NT)

| Type | Core traits |
|---|---|
| [INTJ Architect](/ko/mbti-dict-intj/) | An independent perfectionist who designs systems through long-term vision and strategic thinking |
| [INTP Logician](/ko/mbti-dict-intp/) | A theorist who uses curiosity and logic to dig into the foundations of concepts |
| [ENTJ Commander](/ko/mbti-dict-entj/) | A commander who leads organizations with goal-oriented leadership |
| [ENTP Debater](/ko/mbti-dict-entp/) | A witty innovator who enjoys ideas and debate |

## Diplomats (NF)

| Type | Core traits |
|---|---|
| [INFJ Advocate](/ko/mbti-dict-infj/) | A quiet person of conviction who helps others through insight and ideals |
| [INFP Mediator](/ko/mbti-dict-infp/) | An idealistic dreamer who pursues values and authenticity |
| [ENFJ Protagonist](/ko/mbti-dict-enfj/) | A guide who leads people with empathy and charisma |
| [ENFP Campaigner](/ko/mbti-dict-enfp/) | A free-spirited campaigner who inspires others with passion and possibility |

## Sentinels (SJ)

| Type | Core traits |
|---|---|

| [ISFJ Defender](/ko/mbti-dict-isfj/) | A warm guardian who cares for others with devotion and consideration |
| [ESTJ Executive](/ko/mbti-dict-estj/) | A manager who gets work done through structure and efficiency |
