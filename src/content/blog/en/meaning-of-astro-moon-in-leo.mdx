**A heart that fills when applauded — recognition is emotional oxygen.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Leo way (boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
