**A misty atmosphere — mystery is both your charm and a source of misunderstanding.** A house is the **stage of life** where a planet operates. When Neptune enters the 1st House, the question of "where do I dream, and where do I blur?" unfolds in the realms of **first impressions, the physical body, and your approach to beginnings.**

## How This Placement Works

The 1st House governs first impressions, the physical body, and how you initiate things. When Neptune enters this house, the energy of **dreams, ideals, and the dissolution of boundaries** resides on that stage.

Angular houses are the most prominent positions in a chart — this placement is highly visible to those around you.

## When It Manifests Positively

When this placement functions healthily, the areas of first impressions, the physical body, and your approach to beginnings receive the gift of Neptune (**inspiration**). By considering the nature of the natural sign, Aries, you can read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## The Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Neptune in the 1st House is **becoming overly immersed in that stage** — balance is required so that the areas of first impressions, the physical body, and your approach to beginnings do not become your entire life, leaving other houses empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (the stage). If you have Neptune in the 1st House, the stage is your first impressions, body, and approach to beginnings, while the tone of the performance is determined by the sign in which Neptune is located.

**Q. What is needed to determine the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your **time and place** of birth. Without an accurate birth time, the reliability of house interpretation drops significantly.
