An armored warrior stands in a chariot drawn by two sphinxes, one black and one white. No reins are visible. He controls them not through force, but through will. Only when instinct and reason, each trying to run in opposite directions, are gathered into one path can the chariot move forward.

## Reading the Symbols

The black and white sphinxes symbolize opposing impulses within the self, the starry canopy represents cosmic protection, and the composition of leaving the city behind symbolizes departure from the comfort zone toward achievement.

## Upright Meaning

This is a time to break through obstacles with strong momentum. It is especially favorable in competitive situations, and the more firmly you steady your mind, the more speed you gain. It is also connected with movement, relocation, and travel.

- **Love**: Active, winning energy. A hesitant confession or the next step in a relationship receives a green light.
- **Work & Study**: Victory in exams, interviews, or competitive presentations. Narrow your goal to one clear target and results will follow.
- **Money**: Favorable for achieving ambitious financial goals, but beware of spending without brakes.

## Reversed Meaning

The Chariot reversed is either a runaway vehicle without reins or wheels that have stopped moving. It points to being busy without direction, or to drive being scattered between conflicting desires. Slow down and take the wheel again first.

## Card Combinations

- **The Chariot + The Sun**: Clear victory and recognition. One of the strongest combinations for success.
- **The Chariot + The Moon**: Racing through fog. A warning that moving without checking your direction can be dangerous.

## FAQ

**Q. Does The Chariot always mean I will win?**

A. It is closer to saying that "the power to win has been given." Victory comes only when the two sphinxes, the opposing parts of the mind, are integrated. If your goal is split in two, this is a conditional victory card telling you to organize it into one first.
