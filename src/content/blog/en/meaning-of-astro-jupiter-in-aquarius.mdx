**Luck born from difference — opportunity belongs to the one redrawing the board.** Jupiter is the planet of "where do I find growth and meaning," and Aquarius makes that answer come out differently, yet for everyone.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter ♃ — expansion, luck, the direction of belief |
| Sign | Aquarius ♒ — originality, solidarity, the future |
| Element · Modality | Air · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Uranus |
| Orbital cycle | about 12 years (≈1 year per sign) |

## Core tendency

Jupiter governs **expansion, luck, the direction of belief**. When that energy passes through Aquarius, it shows up differently, yet for everyone.

This is why the same Jupiter can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Aquarius's element, **Air**, adds a distance of language and objectivity to this placement.

## Strengths

- **ideas ahead of their time**
- **a horizontal sense of solidarity**
- **objectivity unswept by emotion**

## Watch out for

- difference for its own sake is a trap
- close relationships can be awkward

## Growth and task

Jupiter changes sign on roughly a 12-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Jupiter in Aquarius, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Jupiter is in Aquarius, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Jupiter sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
