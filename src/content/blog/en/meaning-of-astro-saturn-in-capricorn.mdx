**Saturn in its own home — even if late, it arrives more solid than anyone.** Saturn is the planet of "what tests make me stronger," and Capricorn makes that answer come out step by step, systematically toward the summit.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Saturn ♄ — limits, responsibility, the task of mastery |
| Sign | Capricorn ♑ — goals, structure, achievement |
| Element · Modality | Earth · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Saturn |
| Orbital cycle | about 29 years (≈2.5 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Saturn governs **limits, responsibility, the task of mastery**. When that energy passes through Capricorn, it shows up step by step, systematically toward the summit.

This is why the same Saturn can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Capricorn's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **patience that endures the long game**
- **the skill to build structures**
- **trustworthiness that carries responsibility**

## Watch out for

- emotion is postponed in favor of results
- harshest of all on oneself

## Growth and task

Saturn changes sign on roughly a 29-year rhythm, so people born near the same period often share its growth theme. Still, the **house** this planet occupies and its **aspects** to personal planets can turn the same placement into very different life scenes. For Saturn in Capricorn, the key is to translate the one-line signature above into the actual language of your own choices, mentors, limits, and opportunities.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Saturn is in Capricorn, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Saturn sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
