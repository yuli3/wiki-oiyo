A calm king sits on his throne in the middle of a surging sea. The King of Cups does not suppress emotion; he feels it all and still remains steady. This is emotional maturity in its most complete form.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Cups — Water element, emotion, relationships, intuition, love |
| Rank | King — The one who governs |
| Key words | Emotional balance, generous mediation, calm in storms, mature love |

## Upright Meaning

The power to stay centered is active even in emotionally intense situations. You may be asked to serve as a mediator in conflict or as the mature adult in the room.

- **Love**: Mature love with room to embrace another person. The emotional expression may be quiet, but the depth is oceanic.
- **Work & Money**: Well suited to crisis management and resolving conflict within an organization. Financially, the calm to avoid emotional buying or selling protects your returns.

## Reversed Meaning

Suppressed emotion may leak out as coldness or moodiness. Calm and numbness are not the same. Give yourself permission to feel.

> More important than not getting angry is knowing what made you angry.
