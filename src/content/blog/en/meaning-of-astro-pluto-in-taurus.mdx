**The transformation of economic structures — value and ownership are born again.** Pluto is the planet of "what does our generation end and remake," and Taurus makes that answer come out slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|

| Sign | Taurus ♉ — stability, the senses, accumulation |

## Core tendency

Pluto governs **transformation, power, rebirth**. When that energy passes through Taurus, it shows up slowly but surely, savoring every sense.

This is why the same Pluto can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Taurus's element, **Earth**, adds a steadiness of realism and patience to this placement.

## Strengths

- **the stamina to see things through**
- **a grounded feel for real resources**
- **unshakeable calm**

## Watch out for

- change itself can feel like a threat
- stubbornness can block flexibility

## How to read it as a generational placement

Pluto stays in one sign for years or decades, so Pluto in Taurus is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Pluto is in Taurus, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Pluto sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
