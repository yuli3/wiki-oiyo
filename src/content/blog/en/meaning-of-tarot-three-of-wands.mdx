A figure stands on a hill facing the sea, waiting for the ships they sent out. The plan of the Two of Wands has been put into action, and the first results are now approaching from beyond the horizon.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Wands — Fire element, passion, work, creation, will |
| Position | 3 — Expansion and waiting |
| Key words | Growth in progress, trade, ships of opportunity coming in |

## Upright Meaning

The first fruits of your effort are beginning to arrive. Your view widens, and opportunities for collaboration and expansion come into sight. The farther something was sent out, the greater its return can be.

- **Love**: A time when progress in the relationship becomes visible. It can also connect with good news from a long-distance or faraway connection.
- **Work & Money**: Expansion, exports, and remote collaboration go well. The first returns from an investment or effort begin to come in.

## Reversed Meaning

The ships you are waiting for may be delayed, or expansion may not unfold according to plan. This is delay, not failure, so review the route.

## Advice for Today

> Do not become so busy doubting the ships you already launched that you fail to launch the next one.
