The Sin-Myo (辛卯) Ilju is the 28th pillar of the Sexagenary Cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Sin (Yin Metal), a sharply shining jewel, sitting atop the Earthly Branch Myo (Rabbit), which represents gentle growth. It is often visualized as pruning shears trimming spring sprouts—a configuration that shapes things with precision.

## Personality and Temperament

The Sin-Myo Ilju is a detail-oriented type with nimble hands and exceptional sensory perception. They possess a talent for refining, modifying, and perfecting tasks, and they are quick to read current trends.

- **Strengths**: Sophisticated manual dexterity, refined sense of style, and rapid trend detection.
- **Points of Caution**: Their words can sometimes be as sharp as a blade. The shears used for pruning can also cut the sprouts themselves.

## Structural Analysis — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Sin (辛) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-sin/) sits atop the [Myo (卯) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-myo/). Based on the main Qi of the Ilji (Day Branch), the 10 Gods correspond to [Pyeonjae (偏財)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeonjae/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to [Jeol (絶)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-jeol/). Pyeonjae is the star of fluid wealth—the sense for circulating money and opportunities is embedded in the day pillar. The position of Jeol indicates a dynamic structure characterized by repeated changes and rebounds.

In terms of Nabeum Ohaeng, it belongs to Songbaekmok (Pine and Cypress Wood), a classification frequently used in classical Myeongri alongside other pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
