Zi (子) is the first character of the twelve Earthly Branches, representing the dead of night (Zi hour) and the peak of winter. Much like a rat that remains active while everything else is asleep, it is the position where the seeds of the next cycle are conceived in the unseen depths.

## Personality Manifestation

Individuals with a strong Zi (Water) influence are typically brilliant, quick-witted, and adept at gathering and processing information. While they may appear calm on the surface, their minds are constantly calculating. Many are "night owls" who find their peak focus during the night.

## San-he (Three Harmonies) and Chong (Clash)

Zi forms a Water Bureau (水局) through the Shen-Zi-Chen (申子辰) San-he. It also engages in the Zi-Wu (子午沖) clash with Wu (午)—a direct confrontation between water and fire, representing one of the most intense clashes among the twelve Earthly Branches.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is it true that being born during the Zi hour (11:00 PM – 1:00 AM) makes one highly intelligent?**

A. While Zi represents the energy of wisdom and information, intelligence is not determined solely by the birth hour. In Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny), it is more appropriate to interpret this as a "nocturnal sense"—a potential for keen observation that allows one to perceive things others might miss.

*Earthly Branches represent 'time and environment' in Saju. The manifestation of the same character varies depending on whether it is located in the Year, Month, Day, or Hour pillar, and how it interacts with surrounding characters through combinations (Hap) and clashes (Chong).*
