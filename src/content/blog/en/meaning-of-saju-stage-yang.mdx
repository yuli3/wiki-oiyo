Yang (養) is the twelfth and final stage of the Twelve Stages of Life (Sibiunseong), representing the position where a conceived life is nurtured and grows within the mother's womb. It is the final preparation before entering the world—a star of **care, accumulation, and gentle growth**.

## Meaning and Symbolism

Yang does not rush. Just as a child in the womb must complete the full term to be born healthy, the star of Yang symbolizes the wisdom of waiting for the right time and building strength. It is traditionally deeply connected to inherited fortune (inheritance, family business).

## Temperament of a Saju Strong in Yang

A person with Yang in their Day Pillar (Ilji) has a well-rounded personality and dislikes conflict. They have a talent for nurturing and caring for both people and projects, making them well-suited for fields such as education, development, caregiving, and cultivation.

- **Strengths**: Diligence in steady accumulation, harmonious interpersonal relationships, and a nurturing touch that helps people and projects grow.
- **Points of Caution**: If one becomes too comfortable in a protected environment, independence may be delayed. It is necessary to practice making one's own decisions so that gentleness does not lapse into indecisiveness.

## Encountering Yang in Daewoon or Sewoon

The Yang cycle is a preparation period for the next leap forward. It is a time to build skills, acquire qualifications, and gather people. Although it may not be flashy, the accumulation during this period will blossom during the Jangsaeng (Long Life) cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is it true that having Yang means having good fortune regarding parents or inheritance?**

A. While it is true that classical texts view Yang as the "star of nurturing and inheritance," the essence lies not in material inheritance, but in the "ability to nurture what one has received." Whether it is property or talent, the essence of Yang is the power to cultivate what is inherited and pass it on to the next generation.
