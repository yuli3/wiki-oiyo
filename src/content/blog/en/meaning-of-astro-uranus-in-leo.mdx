**A generation of expressive revolution — the grammar of creation and the stage is changed.** Uranus is the planet of "what does our generation break open," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Uranus ♅ — innovation, rupture, the impulse toward freedom |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Sun |
| Orbital cycle | about 84 years (≈7 years per sign) |

## Core tendency

Uranus governs **innovation, rupture, the impulse toward freedom**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Uranus can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## How to read it as a generational placement

Uranus stays in one sign for years or decades, so Uranus in Leo is less a private personality trait than an **era theme shared by a generation**. In an individual chart, read the house placement and the aspects it makes to the Sun, Moon, and other personal planets to see where that era theme becomes personally active.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Uranus is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Uranus sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
