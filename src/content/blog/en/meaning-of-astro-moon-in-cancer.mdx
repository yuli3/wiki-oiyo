**The Moon in its own home — as deep as the sensitivity is, it needs rituals of recovery.** The Moon is the planet of "where do I feel safe," and Cancer makes that answer come out cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon ☽ — emotion, instinct, the need for security |
| Sign | Cancer ♋ — protection, memory, care |
| Element · Modality | Water · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Moon |
| Orbital cycle | about 28 days (2–3 days per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Moon governs **emotion, instinct, the need for security**. When that energy passes through Cancer, it shows up cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

This is why the same Moon can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Cancer's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a protective instinct that shelters others**
- **emotional sensitivity that reads the room**
- **devotion to one's own people**

## Watch out for

- old wounds are remembered and one withdraws
- the tides of mood run high

## How it shows up

- **Love**: emotional safety is the precondition for every kind of closeness.
- **Work & study**: the Cancer way (cautiously at first, then holding close and deep) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Moon is in Cancer, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Moon sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date and time and place. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
