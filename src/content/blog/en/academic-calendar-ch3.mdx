Across the world, national academic calendars cluster into three distinct operational traditions. The overwhelming majority of OECD countries and global universities operate on a **September-Start (Fall) System**, while South Korea, Japan, and parts of the Southern Hemisphere maintain **Spring-Start Systems**.

Understanding this divergence illuminates the structural friction Korean university students encounter when applying for overseas exchange semesters or international research graduate programs.

## 1. Global Academic Calendar Classification

| Academic System | Representing Countries | School Year Cycle | Semester 1 | Semester 2 | Long Vacation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **September Start (Fall-centric)** | **USA, UK, France, Germany, China, Canada** | September 1 ~ June 30 | Sept ~ Dec / Jan | Feb ~ May / June | **Summer (July ~ Aug: 10~12 weeks)** |
| **March Start (Early Spring)** | **South Korea** | March 1 ~ Feb 28/29 | March ~ Mid-July | Late Aug ~ Dec/Jan | **Winter (Jan ~ Feb: 8~9 weeks)** |
| **April Start (Mid Spring)** | **Japan, India** | April 1 ~ March 31 | April ~ July | Sept ~ Dec / Jan ~ March | **Summer (Aug) & Spring (March)** |
| **February Start (Southern Summer)** | **Australia, New Zealand** | Late Jan / Feb ~ Dec | Feb ~ June (Term 1/2) | July ~ Nov (Term 3/4) | **Summer (Dec ~ Jan: 6~7 weeks)** |

## 2. Institutional Friction and Exchange Gaps

The 6-month seasonal mismatch between South Korea's March cycle and the Western September cycle produces persistent structural frictions:

1. **The 6-Month Study Abroad Gap**: Korean high school seniors graduate in February. If they enroll in American, British, or Canadian universities starting in late August, they inevitably face a mandatory 6-month idle gap (March to August).
2. **Exchange Student Odd-Semester Splits**: Western university exchange students arrive in Seoul in late August—halfway through the calendar year. They enter Korea's **Second Semester (Fall)**, meaning they often take intermediate coursework before prerequisite introductory modules taught in Semester 1 (Spring).
3. **Corporate Recruiting Cycles**: South Korea's two massive open-recruitment recruitment windows (*Gongchae*) occur in **March** (Spring recruitment for February graduates) and **September** (Fall recruitment for August graduates), precisely locked to domestic university convocation schedules.
