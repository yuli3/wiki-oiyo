**Mars without a straight line — it reaches the goal by following the current.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Pisces makes that answer come out boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Pisces ♓ — empathy, imagination, transcendence |
| Element · Modality | Water · Mutable |
| Sign ruler | Neptune |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Pisces, it shows up boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Pisces's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **empathy that feels others' emotions as one's own**
- **imagination that pictures beyond the real**
- **unconditional acceptance**

## Watch out for

- blurred boundaries lead to burnout
- escapism becomes a sweet trap

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Pisces way (boundlessly, as if seeping in, following intuition) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Pisces, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
