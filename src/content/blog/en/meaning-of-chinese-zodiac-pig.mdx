<HighlightBox type="abstract" title="Definition">
  The **Pig** is the twelfth animal in the Chinese zodiac and is paired with the
  Earthly Branch **Hai**. It is a symbolic and cultural cycle marker, not a
  deterministic predictor of personality, fortune, or destiny.
</HighlightBox>

## Cultural associations and traditional traits

The Pig is traditionally associated with generosity, ease, abundance,
straightforward enjoyment, appetite for life, and social warmth. In zodiac
symbolism, it often marks the closing position of the 12-animal sequence, so it
can also suggest completion, rest, and accumulated plenty.

In popular descriptions, Pig-year people may be called sincere, kind,
pleasure-loving, tolerant, trusting, or materially comfortable. These are
inherited cultural associations, not psychological measurements or promises of
fortune. The Pig sign should not be used to label someone as indulgent,
careless, wealthy, naive, lucky, or unlucky.

## Earthly Branch and cycle position

The Pig corresponds to **Hai**, the twelfth of the 12 Earthly Branches. It
follows Dog and completes the animal sequence before the cycle returns to Rat.
The animal name is the common mnemonic; Hai is the branch used in the
traditional calendrical system. Combined with Heavenly Stems, the branch
participates in the broader 60-year cycle.

Representative Pig-year labels include **1983, 1995, 2007, and 2019**. These
are common Gregorian-year references. People born near January or early
February should check the specific zodiac-year boundary, because traditional
zodiac years do not simply follow January 1.

## Compatibility folklore

Compatibility folklore often groups the Pig with **Rabbit** and **Goat** in a
harmonious triad. Pig and **Snake** are commonly treated as a clashing pair.
These are symbolic classifications within traditional matching language, not
evidence that a relationship will succeed or fail. Real compatibility depends
on the people involved, their choices, and their circumstances.

## Common Misunderstanding

The Pig sign does not prove that someone is generous, indulgent, fortunate,
carefree, wealthy, or destined for comfort. It is a birth-year animal label
attached to the Earthly Branch Hai. A full East Asian fortune reading uses the
Four Pillars, or saju, which reads year, month, day, and hour together through
Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. See the broader
[Chinese zodiac definition](/en/meaning-of-chinese-zodiac/) and the related
[Palja definition](/en/meaning-of-palja/) for context.

## Responsible use

Pig symbolism is a cultural and symbolic tradition for reflection and
entertainment. It should not be used as deterministic prediction or as evidence
for judging personality, compatibility, or life direction.
