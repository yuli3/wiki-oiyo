When Mars enters the 1st house, the question "What do I move toward, and how do I fight?" unfolds in the realm of **first impressions, the body, and how one begins**.

## How This Placement Works

The 1st house deals with first impressions, the body, and how one begins. When Mars is placed here, the energy of **drive, desire, and the way anger is expressed** comes to reside on that stage.

Angular houses are the places in a chart where expression is most visible — this placement is easy for other people to notice as well.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of first impressions, the body, and how one begins receives Mars's gift: **drive**. Referring to its natural sign, Aries, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more three-dimensional way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mars in the 1st house is **overconcentration on this stage** — balance is needed so that first impressions, the body, and how one begins do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What is the difference between a house and a sign?**

A. A sign shows **how** a planet is expressed (style), while a house shows **where** it unfolds (stage). With Mars in the 1st house, the stage is first impressions, the body, and how one begins; the acting tone is determined by the sign where Mars is placed.

**Q. What do I need in order to know the houses?**

A. Houses are determined by **birth time and place**. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
