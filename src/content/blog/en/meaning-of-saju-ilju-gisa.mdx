The Gisa (己巳) pillar is the 6th in the Sexagenary Cycle (六十甲子). It consists of the Heavenly Stem Gi (己), which represents Yin Earth (the soil that nurtures all things), sitting upon the Earthly Branch Sa (巳), the snake of insight. It represents a fertile field under the sun—a configuration of substance preparing for fruition.

## Personality and Temperament

The Gisa pillar is characterized by a pragmatic nature, marked by sharp intelligence and an excellent sense of reality. They possess the wisdom to observe quietly, judge accurately, and immediately apply what they have learned to practical life.

- **Strengths**: Realistic intelligence, meticulous management skills, and stable social conduct.
- **Points of Caution**: They may sometimes give the impression of being overly calculating. Occasionally, choosing to take a loss can be the key to retaining people.

## Structural Analysis — Saju Interpretation

The [Gi (己) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-gi/) sits upon the [Sa (巳) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sa/). Based on the main Qi of the Day Branch, the Ten Gods correspond to the [Proper Seal (正印)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-jeongin/), and the 12 Stages of Life correspond to the [Emperor (帝旺)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-jewang/). The Proper Seal is the star of scholarship and acceptance—the foundation of the ability to learn, absorb, and receive support from others. As it sits in the position of the Emperor, it is a strong and prosperous structure where the energy reaches its peak.

In terms of Nabeum Five Elements, it belongs to Daerimmok (大林木), a classification often used in traditional Saju studies to group pillars sharing the same Nabeum.
