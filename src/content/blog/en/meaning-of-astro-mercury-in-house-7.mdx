**A mind that builds relationships through conversation — the negotiation table is your home ground.** A house is the **life stage** where a planet acts. When Mercury is in the 7th house, the question "How do I think and speak?" unfolds in the realm of **partnership, marriage, and contracts**.

## At a Glance

| Type | Details |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury ☿ — thinking, language, and communication style |
| House | 7th house · house of relationships — one-to-one relationships, cooperation, and the mirror |
| Life stage | Partnership, marriage, and contracts |
| Natural sign · classification | Libra · angular house |

## How This Placement Works

The 7th house deals with partnership, marriage, and contracts. When Mercury enters this house, the energy of **thinking, language, and communication style** takes up residence on that stage.

Angular houses are the places in the chart where expression is most visible — this placement is also easy for people around you to notice.

## When It Works Well

When this placement functions in a healthy way, the realm of partnership, marriage, and contracts receives Mercury's gift: **clarity**. Referring to the texture of its natural sign, Libra, can help you read the atmosphere of this stage in a more layered way.

## Shadow

Every placement has a shadow. The shadow of Mercury in the 7th house is **becoming overly concentrated on that stage** — balance is needed so that partnership, marriage, and contracts do not become the whole of life while the other houses are left empty.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How are houses different from signs?**

A. Signs show **how** a planet is expressed (style), while houses show **where** it unfolds (stage). With Mercury in the 7th house, the stage is partnership, marriage, and contracts, while the acting tone is determined by the sign Mercury occupies.

**Q. What do I need to know my houses?**

A. Houses are determined by your birth **time and place**. Without an accurate birth time, house interpretation becomes much less reliable.
