A figure lies face down with ten swords in their back, yet the sun is rising on the horizon. Having nowhere lower to fall means there is only one direction left: upward.

## At a Glance

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Suit | Swords — Air element, thought, communication, conflict, truth |
| Rank | 10 — Rock bottom and conclusion |
| Key words | Complete ending, hitting bottom, final act of betrayal, rising sun |

## Upright Meaning

One phase has completely ended. Because the conclusion is undeniable, recovery may actually come faster. The good thing about rock bottom is that you finally know where your footing is.

- **Love**: A definite end to a relationship. It hurts, but there is no fog of lingering attachment. With sunrise, the next chapter begins.
- **Work & Money**: The end of a project, or confirmation of a clear failure. Financially, losses are finalized, and the rebuilding plan now begins.

## Reversed Meaning

This can show a state of being unable to admit something has ended and counting the swords instead, or a sign that the worst has passed and recovery is beginning.

## Today's Advice

> There is only one thing to do today: declare that it is over. Rebuilding can begin tomorrow.
