Beneath the black pedestal where Baphomet sits, two people stand in chains. But on closer look, the chains are loose enough to slip off at any time. The Devil's true power is not the chain itself, but the belief that it cannot be removed.

## Reading the Symbols

The loose chains symbolize voluntary bondage, the inverted pentagram represents matter pressing down on spirit, and the torch symbolizes the spark of desire. Its similarity to the composition of The Lovers is significant. This is the shadow image of love turning into obsession.

## Upright Meaning

This card signals that something is binding you. A bad habit, a draining relationship, an environment you cannot leave: the first step is admitting that the chain exists. It is also a period of intense desire and temptation.

- **Love**: A powerful but draining attraction. Check the boundary between love and obsession, affection and dependency.
- **Work & Study**: Choices bound by money or status. Ask whether fear is the real reason you cannot leave.
- **Money**: A warning light for overspending, debt, or gambling-like investments. It is time to examine the circuitry of desire.

## Reversed Meaning

The Devil reversed is the moment the chain comes loose. Awareness and recovery from addiction, ending a draining relationship, liberation from fear: this is one of the most hopeful reversed cards in tarot.

## Card Combinations

- **The Devil + The Lovers**: Is it fated attraction or obsession? A combination that questions the true nature of the relationship.
- **The Devil + The Star**: Hope at the end of darkness. A signal that the recovery process is beginning.

## FAQ

**Q. If The Devil appears, should I break up with that person?**

A. The card only shows that something is binding you; the interpretation depends on what the chain is. Ask yourself whether the relationship helps both people grow or only drains them, or whether it is a healthy relationship going through a temporary rut. If the card is reversed, it means the process of release has already begun.
