**Anger that moves sideways — held too long, it leaks out as hurt.** Mars is the planet of "what do I move toward, and how do I fight," and Cancer makes that answer come out cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars ♂ — drive, desire, the way anger moves |
| Sign | Cancer ♋ — protection, memory, care |
| Element · Modality | Water · Cardinal |
| Sign ruler | Moon |
| Orbital cycle | about 2 years |

## Core tendency

Mars governs **drive, desire, the way anger moves**. When that energy passes through Cancer, it shows up cautiously at first, then holding close and deep.

This is why the same Mars can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Cancer's element, **Water**, adds a depth of feeling and resonance to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a protective instinct that shelters others**
- **emotional sensitivity that reads the room**
- **devotion to one's own people**

## Watch out for

- old wounds are remembered and one withdraws
- the tides of mood run high

## How it shows up

- **Love**: the heat and tempo of attraction follow this placement's style.
- **Work & study**: the Cancer way (cautiously at first, then holding close and deep) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: money carries emotion too, so watch for mood spending.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Mars is in Cancer, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Mars sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
