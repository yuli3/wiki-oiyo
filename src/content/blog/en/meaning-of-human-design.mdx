## 1. Origin: The Integration of Four Ancient Wisdoms

Human Design is a relatively modern discipline, but its roots run deep.
It sophisticatedly combines Western **astrology** and the **Kabbalah**, along with Eastern **I Ching** and **chakra** systems. By entering your birth date, time, and location, a 'BodyGraph' is generated, mapping your body's energy centers and circuits.

## 2. The 5 Energy Types: Your Vehicle Category

Human Design broadly divides the 70 billion population into 5 types.

### ① Generator - 37%

- **Characteristics:** Possesses an inexhaustible life force (Sacral). Eyes light up when doing something they love.
- **Strategy:** **To Respond.** Do not initiate first; wait until the world asks you a question. Move when a "uh-huh" sound comes from your gut.

### ② Manifesting Generator - 33%

- **Characteristics:** Speed added to the power of the Generator. A master of multitasking.
- **Strategy:** Respond, and then **To Inform** before taking action.

### ③ Projector - 20%

- **Characteristics:** A leader who guides and manages others. Exceptional insight.
- **Strategy:** **Wait for the Invitation.** Do not scatter your wisdom just anywhere. You shine where you are truly recognized.

### ④ Manifestor - 9%

- **Characteristics:** A revolutionary who creates something out of nothing. Dislikes anyone interfering.
- **Strategy:** **To Inform.** When you state in advance the impact your actions will have on those around you, resistance disappears and peace arrives.

### ⑤ Reflector - 1%

- **Characteristics:** Reflects the world and others as they are, like a mirror. Feels like a different person every single day.
- **Strategy:** **Wait a Lunar Cycle.** Observe and make important decisions slowly over 28 days.

## 3. Authority: Your Inner Decision-Maker

Do not make decisions with your Mind. The core of Human Design is returning the power of decision-making to the body.

- **Emotional Authority:** Wait until the emotional wave (joy and sorrow) passes and settles.
- **Sacral Authority:** Trust the gut response happening right now.
- **Splenic Authority:** Trust the intuition that flashes in a split second. (It does not repeat)

## 4. Center: Defined and Undefined

In the BodyGraph, the colored areas (Defined) are the energy you consistently radiate, while the white areas (Undefined) are where you amplify and absorb the energy of others.
An empty center is not your weakness, but a school to learn the various flavors of the world.

## 5. Conclusion: Live as Yourself

Human Design does not tell you to become a "better person." It simply tells you to return to your "original self." Frustration comes when a Generator tries to live like a Manifestor. Love your design. That is the easiest and most natural path to success.
