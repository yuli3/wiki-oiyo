A figure dances inside a laurel wreath, while the four beings seen in The Wheel of Fortune appear again in the four corners. This is the moment when every experience The Fool has walked through is integrated into one. The end is completion, and completion is the door to the next beginning.

## Reading the Symbols

The oval wreath symbolizes a completed cycle and the womb of new life. The two wands show the movement from the Magician's one wand to two, the integration of consciousness and unconsciousness. The dance symbolizes freedom after effort.

## Upright Meaning

A long process is finally being completed. Graduation, a signed contract, project completion, and other recognized achievements are followed by outside acknowledgment. It is also connected with overseas opportunities and expansion, where your world becomes wider.

- **Love**: Completion of a relationship, such as marriage or entry into a stable stage. It can also imply an international connection.
- **Work & Study**: Goal achievement and official recognition. An invitation comes to a larger stage, such as overseas or expanded work.
- **Money**: The fruits of a long-term plan. It is time to close one cycle well and set the next goal.

## Reversed Meaning

The World reversed is being stopped at 98 percent completion. It points to the habit of postponing the final finish, or the feeling of being unable to end something that is already over. The next door opens only when you tie the knot.

## Card Combinations

- **The World + The Fool**: The perfect cycle of completion and new departure. A turning point between life chapters.
- **The World + The Chariot**: The final sprint toward achievement. You are almost there.

## FAQ

**Q. What comes after The World card?**

A. The Fool, number 0, comes again. The Major Arcana of tarot is not a straight line, but a spiral cycle. Completion on one level leads to a new beginning on a higher level. If The World appears, you have earned the right to celebrate your achievement fully and then design the next journey.
