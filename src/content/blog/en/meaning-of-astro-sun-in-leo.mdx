**The Sun in its own home — wither without expression, warm everyone when you shine.** The Sun is the planet of "who do I shine as," and Leo makes that answer come out boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Sun ☉ — identity, vitality, the core of the self |
| Sign | Leo ♌ — expression, pride, generosity |
| Element · Modality | Fire · Fixed |
| Sign ruler | Sun |
| Orbital cycle | about 1 year (≈1 month per sign) |

## Core tendency

The Sun governs **identity, vitality, the core of the self**. When that energy passes through Leo, it shows up boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage.

This is why the same Sun can look like an entirely different person from sign to sign. Leo's element, **Fire**, adds a charge of intuition and momentum to this placement.

## Strengths

- **a presence that draws people in**
- **big-hearted generosity**
- **creative self-expression**

## Watch out for

- it wobbles badly without recognition
- pride makes apologies hard

## How it shows up

- **Love**: you shine when you stay true to yourself even within a relationship.
- **Work & study**: the Leo way (boldly and brightly, from the center of the stage) becomes the default of your working style.
- **Money**: you earn fast and spend fast, so you need a cooling mechanism.

### FAQ

**Q. If my Sun is in Leo, does that fix my personality?**

A. No. A planet–sign placement is only one piece of the birth chart. Depending on its house and the angles (aspects) it makes with other planets, the same placement can show up very differently.

**Q. How do I find my Sun sign?**

A. Calculate your birth chart from your exact birth date. The [birth-chart guide](/ko/meaning-of-birth-chart/) walks through the reading order, and [oiyo.net/en/natal/chart](https://oiyo.net/en/natal/chart) computes it for you.
