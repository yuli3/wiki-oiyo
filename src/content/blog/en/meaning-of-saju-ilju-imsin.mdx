The Im-Shin (壬申) Ilju is the 9th pillar of the Sexagenary Cycle. It represents the Heavenly Stem Im (Yang Water), symbolizing a vast and deep ocean, sitting upon the Earthly Branch Shin (the talented Monkey). It is the image of a great body of water gushing from between rocks—a fountain of inexhaustible strategy.

## Personality and Temperament

The Im-Shin Ilju is a strategist type with a quick wit and excellent adaptability. Because it sits in the position of *Jangsaeng* (Birth), paths open up even in times of difficulty, and they possess a wide radius of activity.

- **Strengths**: Gushing ideas and planning ability, crisis management skills, and the vitality of the *Yeokma* (Travel Star).
- **Points of Caution**: Relying solely on clever tricks prevents one from filling a "large vessel." Depth in a single field is necessary.

## Structural Reading — Myeongri Interpretation

The [Im (壬) Day Master](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stem-im/) sits atop the [Shin (申) Earthly Branch](/en/meaning-of-saju-branch-sin-monkey/). Based on the main qi of the Ilji, the 10 God is [Pyeonin (偏印)](/en/meaning-of-saju-god-pyeonin/), and the 12 Stages of Life is [Jangsaeng (長生)](/ko/meaning-of-saju-stage-jangsaeng/). Pyeonin is the star of intuition and special talents—extraordinary ideas and expertise grow from the Ilji. As it is the position of *Jangsaeng*, this structure is often accompanied by new beginnings, learning, and the support of benefactors.

In terms of Nabeum, it belongs to *Geombonggeum* (Sword-Point Metal), a classification frequently used in traditional Myeongri to group pillars sharing the same phonetic element.
